Welcome to Salento and Cocora

Salento is a small village in the Quindío department, in the coffee-growing heartland of Colombia, and the gateway to the Cocora Valley. The whole region, with its terraced hillsides of coffee, bamboo-and-mud farmhouses and traditions, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site: the Coffee Cultural Landscape of Colombia. Salento itself is a delight, with brightly painted single-storey houses and wooden balconies on Calle Real, a shopping street of crafts and cafés.

The main draw is the Cocora Valley, home to the Quindío wax palm, Colombia's national tree, which grows up to 60 metres, the tallest palm in the world. The palms rise above green pastures and cloud forest, with cows grazing at their feet. The classic loop hike takes 5 to 6 hours through forest, across rope bridges and to a hummingbird sanctuary, before emerging among the palms.

Around Salento, coffee farms offer tours where you can see the process from picking to roasting, and taste the results. Filandia, another pretty village, is nearby. The area is best enjoyed over two or three days, with time to slow down.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Salento and Cocora

The valley is 11 km from the village.

  1. 01

    Cocora Valley

    A valley of green pastures and the national tree, the wax palm, with hiking loops and horse rides.

    Tip Start early to beat cloud and crowds.

  2. 02

    Salento village and Calle Real

    The village's main pedestrian street, lined with painted houses, craft shops and cafés.

    Tip Walk in the late afternoon.

  3. 03

    Mirador view over the valley

    A viewpoint reached by a stairway from the plaza, looking over the village and the hills.

    Tip Go at sunset.

  4. 04

    Horses in Cocora

    Horse rides through the valley are a popular alternative to hiking.

    Tip Choose a reputable stable.

  5. 05

    Coffee farms

    Farms such as Don Elías and El Ocaso offer tours and tastings.

    Tip Book a morning tour.

  6. 06

    Filandia

    Another colourful coffee village nearby, with a viewing tower.

    Tip Go by bus.
