Welcome to Caño Cristales

Caño Cristales is a river in the Serranía de la Macarena, an isolated mountain range where the Andes, the Amazon and the Orinoco plains meet. It is called the river of five colours, or the liquid rainbow, because during a short period of the year its rocky bed is covered by a plant, Macarenia clavigera, that turns red, alongside yellow sand, green algae, blue water and black rocks.

The effect depends on water levels. The colours are best when the river is at a middle level, roughly from late June or July until November, and dull in the heaviest rains or in the dry season. The river runs through the Sierra de La Macarena National Park, where Amazon, Andean and Orinoco species meet, with pools, waterfalls and potholes carved into quartz-rich rock.

Visiting is regulated and remote. Colombia's conflict once kept the area off-limits, and today access is by a short flight from Villavicencio or Bogotá to La Macarena, then guided tours with local operators. Visitors follow strict rules to protect the river, including no sunscreen or insect repellent in the water, and groups are small. It is a special but demanding trip, best for those who are willing to plan and to check current conditions.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Caño Cristales

Tours visit different stretches of the river, with walking of 1 to 4 hours.

  1. 01

    Los Cuarzos waterfall

    A waterfall over quartz-rich rock where the colours are strong.

    Tip Swim in the pool below.

  2. 02

    Manto de la Virgen

    A series of cascades where the red plants glow beneath the surface.

    Tip Bring a waterproof camera.

  3. 03

    The river in colour

    The stretches where the plants, sand and stones combine into stripes of colour.

    Tip Ask guides for the best spots.

  4. 04

    Los Pailones

    Cylindrical potholes carved by whirling water.

    Tip Swim in the pools.

  5. 05

    Piscina del Turista

    A natural pool for swimming.

    Tip Bring water shoes.

  6. 06

    Cristalitos

    A quieter stretch of the river.

    Tip Good for beginners.

Travel tips

Caño Cristales travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Book months ahead.
  • Travel between July and October.
  • Do not use sunscreen or repellent in the water.
  • Follow all guide instructions.
  • Check current safety and access.
  • Bring a waterproof bag.
  • Wear sturdy sandals.

FAQ

Caño Cristales: frequently asked questions

When are the colours best?

Roughly July to October.

Can I go independently?

No. Guided tours are required.

Is it safe?

Tours run regularly, but check current advice.

How do I get there?

By flight to La Macarena.

Is it expensive?

Yes, compared with the rest of Colombia.

Why is it coloured?

An aquatic plant turns red in sunlight.
