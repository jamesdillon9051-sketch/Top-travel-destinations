Welcome to Bogotá

Bogotá is Colombia's capital and largest city, home to about 8 million people, set on a high plateau at 2,640 metres in the Andes and ringed by green peaks. Its climate is cool and often drizzly, and locals dress in layers. The city was founded in 1538, and its old heart, La Candelaria, still has cobbled streets, tile roofs, small plazas and colonial churches.

Bogotá's museums are its highlight. The Museo del Oro holds around 55,000 pieces of pre-Columbian gold, the largest such collection in the world, the Botero Museum shows works by the painter and sculptor Fernando Botero, alongside pieces by Picasso and Monet, and the National Museum tells the country's story. Street art is prominent, and there are tours to show the murals. Above the city, Monserrate, at 3,150 metres, is reached by funicular or cable car for views.

The city also has a strong food and coffee scene, in neighbourhoods such as Chapinero and Usaquén, and it is the base for a day trip to the Zipaquirá Salt Cathedral, a huge underground church in a salt mine. Bogotá is bigger and less immediately charming than Cartagena or Medellín, but it rewards a few days. Take it slowly at first because of the altitude.

Why visit

Why visit Bogotá?

Bogotá has the best museums in Colombia and an energetic cultural scene.

  • The Gold Museum

    Pre-Columbian treasures.

  • La Candelaria

    Colonial streets and murals.

  • Monserrate

    A mountaintop view.

  • Salt Cathedral

    An underground church.

  • Food and coffee

    Ajiaco and specialty cafés.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Bogotá

The main sights are in La Candelaria and the historic centre.

  1. 01

    La Candelaria

    The historic district, with narrow streets, colonial houses, street art, churches and university life.

    Tip Walk with care after dark.

  2. 02

    Monserrate

    A mountaintop sanctuary at 3,150 metres, reached by cable car or funicular, with views over the city.

    Tip Go on a weekday morning.

  3. 03

    Botero Museum

    A free museum housing Botero's rounded figures and his donated collection of European art.

    Tip It is free.

  4. 04

    Zipaquirá Salt Cathedral

    A cathedral built inside a salt mine, 45 km from Bogotá, with lit crosses and vast chambers.

    Tip Take the train or a bus.

  5. 05

    Museo del Oro

    The Gold Museum has one of the largest collections of pre-Hispanic gold in the world.

    Tip Go early on weekdays.

  6. 06

    Plaza de Bolívar

    The main plaza with the cathedral, the Capitol and the Palace of Justice.

    Tip Visit in daylight.

Travel tips

Bogotá travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Rest on the first day for the altitude.
  • Bring layers and a rain jacket.
  • Use Uber.
  • Watch your phone in the street.
  • Book the Gold Museum early.
  • Go to Monserrate in the morning.
  • Avoid La Candelaria at night.
