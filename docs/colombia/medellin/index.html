Welcome to Medellín

Medellín, in the Aburrá Valley of the Andes at about 1,500 metres, is Colombia's second city, home to about 2.5 million people. It is nicknamed the City of Eternal Spring for its mild climate, with daytime temperatures around 25°C for most of the year. In the 1980s and early 1990s the city was known as the base of the Medellín Cartel and among the most violent in the world. Since then, it has transformed, through investment in public transport, libraries and parks in poorer neighbourhoods, and has won awards as one of the world's most innovative cities.

The best symbol is the Metrocable, a cable car network connected to the metro that links hillside barrios to the city, and the outdoor escalators of Comuna 13, once one of the most dangerous areas, now a hub of street art, hip-hop and tours led by residents. In the centre, Plaza Botero displays 23 bronze sculptures by Medellín-born Fernando Botero, and the Museum of Antioquia holds more of his works.

The city is also a comfortable base: El Poblado and Laureles have restaurants, cafés and nightlife, Parque Arví offers nature on the edge of the city, and Guatapé and coffee farms are within easy day trips. Three days is ideal, and visitors should be alert to safety, especially with drinks and strangers, and avoid the cartel-era tourism that glorifies violence.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Medellín

Sights are spread around the valley, linked by the metro.

  1. 01

    Comuna 13

    A hillside neighbourhood of murals, outdoor escalators and food stalls, best seen with a local guide who explains its history.

    Tip Book a guided tour.

  2. 02

    Metrocable at Santo Domingo

    A cable car line that links hillside communities to the metro, and a highlight for views of the city.

    Tip Use the metro card.

  3. 03

    Plaza Botero

    A public square with 23 bronze sculptures by Fernando Botero, next to the Museum of Antioquia.

    Tip Visit in daytime.

  4. 04

    Valley views

    The city fills the valley, best seen from viewpoints such as Pueblito Paisa or the Metrocable.

    Tip Go at dusk.

  5. 05

    Parque Arví

    A nature reserve on the city's edge, reached by cable car.

    Tip Good for a picnic.

  6. 06

    El Poblado

    The upscale district with cafés, restaurants and nightlife.

    Tip Base yourself here.
