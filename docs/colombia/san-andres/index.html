Welcome to San Andrés

San Andrés is a small island, about 13 km long, in the western Caribbean, about 775 km north-west of Colombia's mainland and just 190 km from Nicaragua. It belongs to an archipelago with Providencia and Santa Catalina, and the whole area is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, Seaflower. The islands were settled by English Puritans in the 17th century and by enslaved Africans, and today the Raizal people speak an English-based Creole and follow Protestant traditions, giving San Andrés a different feel from the rest of Colombia.

The island is famous for the Sea of Seven Colours, a shallow lagoon whose water shifts from white and turquoise to deep indigo. Boat trips visit Johnny Cay, a palm-covered sandbank with a beach bar, and Acuario, where you can snorkel in clear water with fish. Around the island, a ring road passes the Hoyo Soplador blowhole, the West View diving platform, and the small village of San Luis.

San Andrés is a duty-free island, and can be crowded with domestic tourists at peak times. It is best for beach and water activities, with warm sea all year, and it is a flight from Bogotá, Medellín or Cartagena. For a quieter alternative, Providencia, a smaller island nearby, is more remote.

Main attractions

Top things to see in San Andrés

Most sights are a boat ride or a ring road drive away.

  1. 01

    Johnny Cay

    A small cay with a white beach and palms, reached by boat, with restaurants and clear water.

    Tip Go early to beat tour groups.

  2. 02

    Acuario and Haynes Cay

    Shallow spots for snorkelling with fish.

    Tip Combine with Johnny Cay.

  3. 03

    Hoyo Soplador

    A blowhole where seawater shoots into the air when the waves are strong.

    Tip Best on windy days.

  4. 04

    West View

    A diving platform and snorkelling spot with steps into the water.

    Tip Bring water shoes.

  5. 05

    Rocky Cay

    A small reef with a shipwreck.

    Tip Snorkel at low tide.

  6. 06

    San Luis

    A quieter east-side village with a beach.

    Tip Good for lunch.

    Best time
