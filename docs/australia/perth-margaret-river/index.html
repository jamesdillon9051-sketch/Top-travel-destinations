Welcome to Perth

Perth is the capital of Western Australia and one of the most isolated major cities in the world, closer to Jakarta than to Sydney. It sits on the Swan River, with the Indian Ocean coast just to the west, and has a Mediterranean climate with more sunshine than any other Australian capital. It grew rich on gold and mining, and it is a relaxed, prosperous and outdoorsy city of about two million people.

Its centrepiece is Kings Park, a 400-hectare park with bushland, botanic gardens and a view over the city and river, and it is easy to reach beaches such as Cottesloe and Scarborough by train and bus. Fremantle, at the mouth of the river, has a historic port, a convict prison listed by UNESCO, a market and a lively food and music scene. Off the coast, Rottnest Island is home to the quokka, the small, smiling marsupial.

The wider south-west is superb. Margaret River, three hours south, is one of Australia's premier wine regions, with cabernet sauvignon and chardonnay, caves, tall forests and surf breaks along a rugged coast. Perth is a good base for a few days of road tripping, and many visitors use it as a stop between the east coast and Asia.

Why visit

Why visit Perth?

Perth and Margaret River show a sunnier, more relaxed side of Australia.

  • Beaches and sunsets

    Long white beaches facing the Indian Ocean, with sunsets over the water.

  • Kings Park

    One of the world's largest inner-city parks, with wildflowers and views.

  • Rottnest Island and quokkas

    Cycle round a car-free island with beaches and friendly marsupials.

  • Margaret River wine and coast

    Cellar doors, caves, forests and surf on a rugged coast.

  • Fremantle

    A historic port with markets, pubs and heritage buildings.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Perth

The city sights can be covered in two days, and Margaret River rewards a couple of nights.

  1. 01

    Kings Park and Botanic Garden

    A vast park above the city, with the Federation Walkway through the treetops, lookouts and native gardens, and spectacular wildflowers in spring.

    Tip Go for sunrise or sunset.

  2. 02

    Cottesloe Beach

    Perth's favourite city beach, with white sand, clear water, a pub above the sand and sunsets over the Indian Ocean.

    Tip Swim between the flags.

  3. 03

    Fremantle

    A port town with Victorian buildings, a heritage market, Fremantle Prison, a UNESCO convict site, and a lively cafe and brewery scene.

    Tip Go on a weekend for the market.

  4. 04

    Rottnest Island

    A car-free island 19 km offshore, reached by ferry from Fremantle or Perth, where you can cycle around beaches and coves and see quokkas. Do not feed or touch them.

    Tip Take a day trip, or stay overnight.

  5. 05

    Margaret River wine region

    More than 100 wineries between Cape Naturaliste and Cape Leeuwin, known for cabernet sauvignon and chardonnay, with cellar doors, restaurants and forest.

    Tip Hire a driver for tastings.

  6. 06

    Busselton Jetty

    A timber jetty extending about 1.8 km into Geographe Bay, one of the longest in the world, with a small train and an underwater observatory.

    Tip Walk or take the train.

  7. 07

    Cape Leeuwin Lighthouse

    The lighthouse at the corner where the Indian and Southern Oceans meet, at the south-west tip of the continent.

    Tip Go for the view.

Things to do

Best things to do in Perth

Perth mixes city, beach and wine country, with time on the coast.

  1. Cycle around Rottnest Island

    A 22-km circuit of beaches, bays and viewpoints, with quokkas.

    Tip Rent a bike at the ferry.

  2. Taste Margaret River wine

    Visit cellar doors, restaurants and breweries.

    Tip Book tastings or a tour.

  3. Explore the caves

    Lake Cave, Jewel Cave and Mammoth Cave are in the region.

    Tip Check the tours.

  4. Watch sunset at Cottesloe

    A local ritual, with a drink.

    Tip Arrive early.

  5. Visit the Pinnacles Desert

    Thousands of limestone spires in Nambung National Park, about 2 hours north.

    Tip Go at sunset.

  6. Look for wildflowers

    August to October, the state is covered with wildflowers.

    Tip Kings Park is an easy place to see them.

Local food

What to eat in Perth

Perth's food scene is fresh and outdoorsy, with seafood, produce and wine.

  • Rock lobster

    Western rock lobster, or crayfish, is a local delicacy.

    Where to try it Fremantle and coastal restaurants.

  • Margaret River wine

    Cabernet sauvignon, chardonnay and semillon sauvignon blanc.

    Where to try it Cellar doors and restaurants.

  • Craft beer

    A strong local brewing scene, particularly in Fremantle.

    Where to try it Breweries in Fremantle and Margaret River.

  • Fish and chips

    Fresh fish and chips at the harbour.

    Where to try it Fremantle's Fishing Boat Harbour.

  • Local produce and chocolate

    Cheese, olive oil and chocolate from farms.

    Where to try it Margaret River farm gates.

Transport

Getting to and around Perth

Perth is a long way from everywhere, and is reached by air.

Getting there

  • Flight to Perth (PER)

    Direct flights from Asia and the Middle East, and domestic flights from east coast cities, about 5 hours from Sydney.

    The airport is close to the city.

  • Drive to Margaret River

    About 3 hours south, on good roads.

    Hire a car in Perth.

  • Indian Pacific train

    A multi-day journey from Sydney to Perth.

    Book well ahead.

Getting around

Perth has good public transport, and a car is needed for the south-west.

  • Trains, buses and ferries

    An integrated system for Perth and Fremantle, with a SmartRider card.

    The free CAT buses serve the centre.

  • Hire car

    Essential for Margaret River and the coast.

    Roads are quiet.

  • Bicycle

    Popular on Rottnest and along the river.

    Wear a helmet.

When to go

Best time to visit Perth

Perth has hot dry summers and mild wet winters. Spring and autumn are best.

  • Spring (September–November)

    Best time

    Warm and sunny, with wildflowers and whales.

  • Summer (December–February)

    Good

    Hot and dry, with sea breezes and beach weather.

  • Autumn (March–May)

    Best time

    Warm and settled, the wine harvest.

  • Winter (June–August)

    Quiet

    Cool and wet, with fewer visitors.

Worth planning around: Extremely hot days in January and February.

Travel tips

Perth travel tips

A few tips help you plan Western Australia.

  • Allow time. Distances are long, and Perth is far from other cities.
  • Hire a car for Margaret River, and share driving.
  • Do not feed or touch quokkas. It is illegal on Rottnest.
  • Wear sunscreen and a hat all year.
  • Book Rottnest ferries and accommodation ahead in summer.
  • Check bushfire warnings in summer.
  • Note that Perth is 2–3 hours behind the east coast.

FAQ

Perth: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Perth?

Two or three days for Perth and Fremantle, plus two for Margaret River.

Is Rottnest Island worth it?

Yes, for the quokkas, beaches and cycling. A day trip is enough.

What is the best time to visit?

September to November and March to April.

Can I visit Margaret River as a day trip?

It is a long day of about 6 hours' driving. An overnight stay is better.

Can I touch a quokka?

No. It is illegal to touch or feed them, and they are wild animals.

Is Perth safe?

Yes, generally. Usual city precautions apply.
