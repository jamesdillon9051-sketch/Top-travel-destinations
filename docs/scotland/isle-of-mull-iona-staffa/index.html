Welcome to Mull, Iona & Staffa

Mull is genuinely the gateway to a trio of Inner Hebridean islands, its main town Tobermory instantly recognisable for the brightly painted buildings lining its small harbour.

A short ferry hop away sits Iona, genuinely one of the most spiritually significant places in Scotland, where St Columba founded a monastery in 563 that helped bring Christianity to much of the country.

Further out again is Staffa, genuinely home to Fingal's Cave, an extraordinary sea cave of perfectly hexagonal basalt columns that inspired Mendelssohn's Hebrides Overture after his own visit in 1829.

Transport

Getting to and around Mull, Iona & Staffa

Mull is genuinely reachable only by ferry, with Iona and Staffa a further crossing beyond it.

Getting there

  • Ferry from Oban

    Genuinely the main route to Mull, a scenic 45-minute crossing with regular sailings.

    Oban itself is about two and a half hours from Glasgow by road or rail.

  • Ferry from Mull to Iona

    Genuinely a short, frequent crossing from Fionnphort at Mull's western tip.

    Only a few minutes each way, running throughout the day.

  • Boat trip to Staffa

    Genuinely departs from Mull, Iona or occasionally Oban directly.

    Weather-dependent, so build flexibility into any itinerary.
