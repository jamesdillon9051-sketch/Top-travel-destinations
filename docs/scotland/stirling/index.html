Welcome to Stirling

Stirling is genuinely a small city with an outsized place in Scottish history, its strategic position controlling the lowest crossing point of the River Forth making it central to centuries of conflict.

Its castle, genuinely one of Scotland's grandest, overlooks the plain where William Wallace and Robert the Bruce both won decisive battles for Scottish independence, at Stirling Bridge in 1297 and Bannockburn in 1314.

Most visitors genuinely visit as a day trip from Glasgow or Edinburgh, though an overnight allows time to properly cover the castle, the Wallace Monument and the battlefields without rushing.
