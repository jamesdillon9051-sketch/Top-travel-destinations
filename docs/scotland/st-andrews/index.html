Welcome to St Andrews

St Andrews is genuinely known worldwide as the home of golf, its Old Course a place of pilgrimage for players long before most visitors ever set foot in the small Fife town surrounding it.

That single reputation genuinely undersells the place — St Andrews is also home to Scotland's oldest university, founded in 1413, and the substantial ruins of what was once the largest cathedral in Scotland, a reminder of the town's earlier role as the country's religious capital.

Most visitors genuinely spend a day or two here, walking the Old Course's famous Swilcan Bridge, the cathedral ruins and the windswept West Sands beach, all within a genuinely compact, walkable town centre.

No, genuinely not — the cathedral ruins, castle, university and beach are all worthwhile regardless of interest in golf.
