Welcome to Mekong Delta

The Mekong River ends its journey of 4,900 km in southern Vietnam, splitting into a fan of channels that empty into the South China Sea. The delta they form is one of the most fertile places in Asia, producing much of Vietnam's rice, fish and tropical fruit. It is a flat landscape of paddies, orchards, fish farms and villages linked by waterways, where boats are still as important as roads.

For visitors, the delta offers a glimpse of everyday rural life in the south, at a slow pace. Typical trips include boat rides through narrow canals lined with nipa palms, visits to workshops making coconut candy, rice paper and puffed rice, orchard walks with fruit tasting, and overnight stays in homestays. The most famous sight is the floating market at Cai Rang near Can Tho, where traders sell produce from boats, though it has shrunk as roads and bridges have improved.

The delta is easy to visit on a day trip from Ho Chi Minh City, but it repays a night or two, particularly for the early-morning atmosphere. Many visitors also use it as a route to or from Cambodia, by boat from Chau Doc to Phnom Penh.

Why visit

Why visit Mekong Delta?

The Mekong Delta is a peaceful contrast to Vietnam's cities, and shows a different side of the country.

  • Waterways and boats

    Life on and beside the rivers, from small sampans to big barges.

  • Floating markets

    Early-morning trade on the water, with produce sold from boats.

  • Orchards and workshops

    Fruit tasting, coconut candy and rice-paper making.

  • Homestays

    A night with a local family, with home cooking.

  • Easy from Saigon

    A day trip or a relaxing overnight stay, within a few hours.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Mekong Delta

The delta is large, and most visitors choose one or two bases.

  1. 01

    The Cai Rang floating market, Can Tho

    The largest floating market in the delta, where wholesalers sell fruit and vegetables from big boats, hanging samples from poles. Best at dawn, from about 5 to 8 am.

    Tip Book a boat the evening before, and go early.

  2. 02

    Can Tho

    The largest city in the delta, with a pleasant waterfront, markets and pagodas, and a base for boat trips.

    Tip Stay a night.

  3. 03

    My Tho and Ben Tre

    Delta towns about 1.5 to 2 hours from Ho Chi Minh City, known for canals, coconut candy workshops and orchard visits. Ben Tre is the coconut capital of Vietnam.

    Tip A good day trip for those short of time.

  4. 04

    Vinh Long and homestays

    A delta town with islands, orchards and family-run homestays, quieter than My Tho.

    Tip Book a homestay for the evening.

  5. 05

    Chau Doc

    A town near the Cambodian border with a floating village, Sam Mountain, Tra Su Cajuput forest and a mixed culture of Vietnamese, Khmer, Cham and Chinese people.

    Tip Take the boat to Phnom Penh if heading to Cambodia.

Things to do

Best things to do in Mekong Delta

Most activities revolve around the rivers and orchards.

  1. Visit a floating market at dawn

    Take a boat to the Cai Rang market and have breakfast on the water.

    Tip Go before 7 am.

  2. Take a canoe through the canals

    A rowing boat takes you through narrow channels under nipa palms.

    Tip Ask for a small boat.

  3. Try a homestay

    Sleep in a village house and eat home-cooked meals.

    Tip Book in advance.

  4. Watch a coconut candy workshop

    See coconut milk boiled and cut into sweets.

    Tip Buy fresh.

  5. Cycle through orchards

    Quiet paths pass fruit trees, and you can taste tropical fruit.

    Tip Go in the morning.

  6. Take a boat to Cambodia

    A river route from Chau Doc to Phnom Penh, about 5 to 6 hours.

    Tip Check visa rules.

Local food

What to eat in Mekong Delta

The delta's food is fresh, sweet and based on river fish, rice and tropical fruit.

  • Elephant-ear fish

    A deep-fried whole fish served upright, wrapped in rice paper with herbs.

    Where to try it Restaurants in My Tho and Can Tho.

  • Hu tieu My Tho

    A clear noodle soup with pork and seafood, the local breakfast.

    Where to try it Street stalls.

  • Coconut candy

    Chewy sweets made from coconut cream, from Ben Tre.

    Where to try it Workshops.

  • Banh xeo

    Sizzling pancakes with pork and shrimp.

    Where to try it Restaurants.

  • Tropical fruit

    Mango, dragon fruit, rambutan and durian, according to season.

    Where to try it Orchards and markets.

Accommodation

Where to stay in Mekong Delta

Choose between a hotel in Can Tho and a homestay on the river.

  • Can Tho

    Hotels along the waterfront and in the centre.

  • Vinh Long and Ben Tre

    Family-run guesthouses among orchards.

    Best for: Homestays

  • Chau Doc

    Riverside hotels near the border.

    Best for: The Cambodian route

Transport

Getting to and around Mekong Delta

The delta lies south and west of Ho Chi Minh City.

Getting there

  • Tour from Ho Chi Minh City

    Day tours to My Tho or Ben Tre, or two-day trips to Can Tho, are widely available.

    Choose a small-group tour.

  • Bus or private car

    About 3 to 4 hours from Ho Chi Minh City to Can Tho, and 1.5 to 2 hours to My Tho.

    The new expressways have cut journey times.

  • Flight to Can Tho (VCA)

    Flights from Hanoi and some other cities.

    Check the schedule.

Getting around

Boats, bicycles and taxis cover the delta.

  • Boat

    The main way to see the rivers and markets.

    Agree the price and length.

  • Bicycle

    Good for orchard lanes.

    Rent from your homestay.

  • Grab and taxi

    Useful in Can Tho and towns.

    Use the app.

When to go

Best time to visit Mekong Delta

The delta is hot and humid all year, with a dry season and a wet season, and the water level rises from August to November.

  • Dry season (December–April)

    Best time

    Sunny and comfortable, though hot by April.

  • Early wet (May–July)

    Good

    Afternoon showers, green fields and fruit season.

  • High water (August–November)

    Quiet

    Heavy rain, with the rivers in flood.

  • Tet (January or February)

    Think twice

    Many businesses close, though the celebrations are vivid.

Worth planning around: Tet, when many boats and shops are closed.

Travel tips

Mekong Delta travel tips

A few tips help you enjoy the delta.

  • Stay a night, to see the floating market at dawn.
  • Choose a small boat, not a big group boat.
  • Bring insect repellent and sun protection.
  • Bargain for boats, and agree the price beforehand.
  • Try fruit in season.
  • Book homestays in advance.
  • Do not expect the floating markets to be as big as in old photos.

FAQ

Mekong Delta: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in the Mekong Delta?

One day for a taste, two days with a night in Can Tho for the floating market.

Is the floating market worth it?

Yes, at dawn. It is smaller than in the past, but still atmospheric.

What is the best time to visit?

December to April, the dry season.

Can I do it as a day trip from Ho Chi Minh City?

Yes, to My Tho or Ben Tre. Can Tho is better with a night.

Is it safe?

Yes, generally. Use reputable boat operators.

Can I travel to Cambodia by boat?

Yes, from Chau Doc to Phnom Penh, about 5 to 6 hours. Check the visa requirements.
