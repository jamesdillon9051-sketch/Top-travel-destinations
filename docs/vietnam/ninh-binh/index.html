Welcome to Ninh Binh

Ninh Binh sits at the southern edge of the Red River Delta, where the flat rice country suddenly gives way to limestone towers, caves and rivers. It was the site of Hoa Lu, the capital of Vietnam under the Dinh and Early Le dynasties in the 10th century, before the court moved to Thang Long, the site of today's Hanoi. Its hills and caves were used as fortresses and hiding places, and the Trang An Landscape Complex, listed by UNESCO as a mixed natural and cultural site in 2014, preserves a series of them.

The scenery is often compared with Ha Long Bay, and the comparison holds. Instead of the sea, there are rice paddies, lotus ponds, rivers and lakes, with limestone karsts rising like fingers from the water. Sampans, small rowing boats, take visitors through caves and along channels between the cliffs, often propelled by rowers using their feet. Film crews chose Trang An for Kong: Skull Island in 2017.

Ninh Binh is close to Hanoi, and many visitors come on a day trip, but the pace and light are best appreciated with a night or two. Cycling between the sites, climbing to viewpoints and eating in village restaurants make it one of the most relaxed stops in the north.

Why visit

Why visit Ninh Binh?

Ninh Binh offers the kind of scenery that people go to Ha Long Bay for, with a slower, more rural feel.

  • A dramatic landscape

    Karst hills, caves, rivers and rice paddies together make an outstanding backdrop.

  • Boat rides

    A journey through caves and along the river at Trang An or Tam Coc is the highlight.

  • Viewpoints

    Hang Mua's 500-step climb gives one of the best views in northern Vietnam.

  • History

    Hoa Lu, the 10th-century capital, and the huge Bai Dinh Pagoda.

  • Easy from Hanoi

    Trains and buses take about two hours.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Ninh Binh

The sights are spread around the province, and are best reached by bicycle, scooter or car.

  1. 01

    Trang An Landscape Complex

    A network of rivers and caves, explored on rowing boats for two to three hours, passing temples, grottoes and karst peaks. It is the most impressive and best organised boat route.

    Tip Go early, and choose a route with more caves.

  2. 02

    Tam Coc

    A river route through three caves, whose name means Three Caves, with rice paddies on either side. It is busier and shorter than Trang An, and best in the ripening season.

    Tip Visit in late May or June to see golden rice.

  3. 03

    Hang Mua (Mua Cave viewpoint)

    A stairway of about 500 steps up a limestone hill, leading to a viewpoint with a dragon statue and a panorama of the rice fields, river and karsts.

    Tip Go early or late, and bring water.

  4. 04

    Hoa Lu ancient capital

    The capital of the Dinh and Early Le dynasties from 968 to 1010, with restored temples dedicated to the kings and surrounded by mountains.

    Tip Combine with Tam Coc.

  5. 05

    Bai Dinh Pagoda

    A huge modern Buddhist complex, the largest in Vietnam, with a long corridor of Buddha statues, a giant bronze Buddha and a tall stupa.

    Tip Allow a couple of hours.

  6. 06

    Van Long Nature Reserve

    A wetland reserve with quiet boat rides among karsts and rare langurs, calmer than Trang An.

    Tip Go for birdwatching and peace.

Things to do

Best things to do in Ninh Binh

Ninh Binh is best on two wheels and on the water.

  1. Take a boat through Trang An

    Sit back as a rower guides you through caves and channels.

    Tip Go in the morning to avoid the crowds.

  2. Climb Hang Mua

    See the whole landscape from the top of the stairs.

    Tip Go at sunrise or late afternoon.

  3. Cycle through the countryside

    Quiet lanes link paddies, villages and temples.

    Tip Rent a bicycle in Tam Coc.

  4. Try a cooking class

    Cook with a local family, using produce from their garden.

    Tip Book through your homestay.

  5. Visit Cuc Phuong National Park

    Vietnam's oldest national park, with ancient trees and a primate rescue centre.

    Tip Allow a day.

  6. Sample goat meat and crispy rice

    Local specialities of Ninh Binh, at restaurants around town.

    Tip Ask for the local version.

Local food

What to eat in Ninh Binh

Ninh Binh's specialities include goat meat and crispy rice, and its village restaurants serve fresh, simple food.

  • Goat meat dishes

    Goat prepared in many ways, from steamed to grilled to hotpot, a regional speciality.

    Where to try it Restaurants in Ninh Binh town.

  • Com chay (burnt rice)

    Crispy fried rice served with pork or seafood, a local dish.

    Where to try it Restaurants around Hoa Lu.

  • Snail noodles

    Rice noodles with river snails, served in a sour broth.

    Where to try it Local shops.

  • Fresh river fish

    Fish from the local rivers, grilled or steamed.

    Where to try it Village restaurants.

  • Egg coffee and fruit

    Hanoi-style coffees and fresh fruit.

    Where to try it Cafés in Tam Coc.

Transport

Getting to and around Ninh Binh

Ninh Binh is about 95 km south of Hanoi.

Getting there

  • Train from Hanoi

    About 2 to 2.5 hours on the Reunification line, with several services a day.

    Book a seat in advance.

  • Bus or limousine van

    About 2 hours from Hanoi, with drop-off in Tam Coc.

    Book reputable companies.

  • Private car

    About 2 hours by expressway.

    Good for day trips.

Getting around

The sights are spread out, and cycling is the pleasant way to see them.

  • Bicycle

    Cheap to rent, and roads are quiet.

    Watch for heat and traffic.

  • Scooter

    Popular for reaching farther sites.

    Wear a helmet, and check your licence and insurance.

  • Grab or private car

    For longer distances such as Bai Dinh.

    Arrange in advance.

When to go

Best time to visit Ninh Binh

Ninh Binh follows the northern climate, with cool winters and hot summers. The golden rice season is late May and June.

  • Spring (March–May)

    Best time

    Green paddies and mild weather.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Good

    Hot and humid. Golden rice in late May and June.

  • Autumn (September–November)

    Best time

    Clear skies and a second rice harvest.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Quiet

    Cool, grey and misty, with fewer visitors.

Worth planning around: Weekends and public holidays, when Trang An is packed.

Travel tips

Ninh Binh travel tips

A few tips help you make the most of the area.

  • Stay a night or two instead of a day trip.
  • Go to Trang An early, before the tour groups.
  • Tip the boat rower, who works hard.
  • Climb Hang Mua at the start or end of the day.
  • Ignore touts who steer you to a different boat station.
  • Wear sun protection, and bring water.
  • Avoid weekends and holidays if you can.

FAQ

Ninh Binh: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Ninh Binh?

Two days lets you see the boats, the viewpoint and Hoa Lu. Many visit in a day, but it is rushed.

Which is better, Trang An or Tam Coc?

Trang An is more scenic, with more caves and a longer route. Tam Coc is shorter and best in rice season.

Is Ninh Binh like Ha Long Bay?

It has a similar limestone landscape, but on land and rivers rather than on the sea.

What is the best time to visit?

March to June and September to November, with golden rice in late May and June.

How do I get there from Hanoi?

By train or bus, about two hours.

Is it safe to cycle?

Yes, on quiet lanes, but take care near roads.
