Welcome to Península Valdés

Península Valdés is a large peninsula on the coast of Chubut province, in Patagonia, almost an island, connected to the mainland by a narrow isthmus. UNESCO inscribed it in 1999 as a site of outstanding marine and coastal wildlife. Its sheltered gulfs, cliffs and beaches provide breeding grounds for a range of marine mammals and seabirds, and its steppe holds guanacos, maras (Patagonian hares) and rheas.

The star attraction is the southern right whale, which comes to the calm waters of Golfo Nuevo, near the village of Puerto Pirámides, to mate and give birth, from about June to December, and is most abundant in September and October. Boat trips from Puerto Pirámides bring visitors alongside the whales, which are curious and often approach the boats. Other highlights include elephant seals on the beaches, sea lion colonies, Magellanic penguins in nearby colonies and, at Punta Norte, orcas that famously beach themselves in a hunting technique, catching sea lion pups in the surf, most often between February and April.

The reserve is remote and windswept, and best explored by car or a tour. Puerto Madryn, a modern coastal town, is the usual base, about 100 km from Puerto Pirámides. To the south, Punta Tombo has one of the largest colonies of Magellanic penguins in the world, and the Welsh-founded towns of Gaiman and Trelew give a taste of Patagonia's Welsh heritage, including tea houses.

Why visit

Why visit Península Valdés?

Valdés is one of the best places in the world to see marine wildlife, and highly accessible.

  • Southern right whales

    Close encounters with mothers and calves from boats in calm bays.

  • Orcas and elephant seals

    Rare behaviour on view at Punta Norte, and giant seals on beaches.

  • Penguins

    Huge colonies of Magellanic penguins from September to March.

  • Diverse wildlife

    Guanacos, maras, rheas and seabirds.

  • Welsh heritage

    Gaiman's tea houses and chapels, a legacy of Welsh settlers in 1865.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Península Valdés

The main sights are around the peninsula, with a day for penguins to the south.

  1. 01

    Puerto Pirámides and whale watching

    The only settlement on the peninsula, where whale-watching boats leave from the beach. Tours last about 1.5 hours, and run from June to December.

    Tip Book the first departure.

  2. 02

    Southern right whales

    Adult whales, mothers and calves are seen at close range in Golfo Nuevo, with tail slaps and breaches.

    Tip Bring a jacket, and keep your camera ready.

  3. 03

    Punta Norte and orcas

    A sea lion colony where orcas rush onto the beach to catch pups, an extraordinary hunting behaviour. Sightings are best at high tide from February to April, and are not guaranteed.

    Tip Check tide times and be patient.

  4. 04

    Punta Delgada and Caleta Valdés

    Colonies of elephant seals, sea lions and penguins, with beaches and cliffs, on the eastern coast.

    Tip Visit in September and October for elephant seal pups.

  5. 05

    Punta Tombo penguin colony

    About 200 km south of Puerto Madryn, the largest mainland colony of Magellanic penguins, open from September to March, where visitors walk along paths among nesting birds.

    Tip A full-day trip from Puerto Madryn.

  6. 06

    Gaiman and the Welsh tea houses

    A small town founded by Welsh settlers in the 19th century, with tea houses serving cakes, scones and tea in a Welsh tradition.

    Tip Combine with Punta Tombo.

Things to do

Best things to do in Península Valdés

Wildlife dominates, with time for scenery and local culture.

  1. Go whale watching

    Take a boat from Puerto Pirámides, between June and December.

    Tip September and October are peak.

  2. Look for orcas at Punta Norte

    Wait at the viewing platform for a chance to see orcas hunting.

    Tip Go from February to April at high tide.

  3. Walk among penguins

    At Punta Tombo and Cabo Dos Bahías.

    Tip Stay on the paths.

  4. Drive the reserve loop

    See guanacos, maras and seabirds from the road.

    Tip Fill up in Puerto Pirámides.

  5. Snorkel or dive with sea lions

    From Puerto Madryn or Puerto Pirámides, with licensed operators.

    Tip Book in advance.

  6. Have Welsh tea in Gaiman

    Sample scones, cakes and tea.

    Tip Go in the afternoon.

When to go

Best time to visit Península Valdés

The peninsula has a windy, semi-arid climate. Wildlife follows the seasons.

  • Spring (September–November)

    Best time

    Peak whales, penguins nesting and elephant seals with pups.

  • Summer (December–February)

    Good

    Warm, with penguin chicks, but fewer whales.

  • Autumn (March–May)

    Good

    Orcas at Punta Norte, from February to April.

  • Winter (June–August)

    Good

    Whales arrive, with cold and windy weather.

Worth planning around: January to May if whales are your priority.

Travel tips

Península Valdés travel tips

A few tips help you see the wildlife.

  • Go from September to October for whales, elephant seals and penguins.
  • Check tides for orca sightings at Punta Norte.
  • Bring warm, windproof clothing, since it is cold on the boat.
  • Book whale-watching trips early in the season.
  • Keep a safe distance from all wildlife.
  • Fill your fuel tank before entering the reserve.
  • Bring binoculars and a zoom lens.

FAQ

Península Valdés: frequently asked questions

When is the best time for whales?

June to December, with September and October the best.

Can I see orcas?

Sometimes, at Punta Norte from February to April, at high tide. Sightings are not guaranteed.

How many days do you need?

Two to three days.

Can I visit as a day trip from Puerto Madryn?

Yes, on a full-day tour.

Do I need a car?

It helps, though tours are available.

When can I see penguins?

From September to March.
