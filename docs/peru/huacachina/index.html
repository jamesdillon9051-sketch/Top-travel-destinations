Welcome to Huacachina

Huacachina is genuinely a real oasis, a small palm-fringed lagoon surrounded by some of the tallest sand dunes in South America, rising more than 100 metres in places right at the village's edge.

It's genuinely built almost entirely around one thing — using those dunes for adventure, whether racing across them in a dune buggy or sandboarding down their steep faces as the light turns gold in the late afternoon.

Most visitors genuinely treat it as a short, high-energy detour on Peru's southern coast, a night or two between Lima and Nazca that feels genuinely unlike anywhere else in the country.

Things to do

Best things to do in Huacachina

Huacachina genuinely exists almost entirely for its dunes, and the classic activities here reward exactly that.

  1. Ride a dune buggy across the sand

    Genuinely the essential Huacachina experience, a fast, steep-dropping ride across the dunes in a modified open-top buggy.

    Tip Genuinely hold on tight — drivers take the steep drops at real speed, and it's a genuinely thrilling ride.

  2. Sandboard down the dunes

    Genuinely accessible even for first-timers, most buggy tours stop partway through for a chance to board down a dune face.

    Tip Genuinely lying flat rather than standing is the easier, more common approach for beginners.

  3. Walk the boardwalk around the lagoon

    A genuinely relaxed way to spend an hour, taking in the oasis at a slower pace than a buggy tour allows.

    Tip Genuinely a nice way to start or end a day, away from the dune activities' adrenaline.

  4. Watch sunset over the dunes

    A genuinely essential end to a Huacachina day, watching the desert's colours shift as the sun drops.

    Tip Genuinely many buggy tours are specifically timed to end at a high dune for sunset.

  5. Relax at an oasis-side café

    A genuinely welcome way to recover after a dusty afternoon on the dunes, with several cafés overlooking the lagoon.

    Tip Genuinely a good spot to rehydrate before or after any dune activity in the heat.

  6. Photograph the oasis from a high dune

    A genuinely rewarding short climb, or buggy ride, for the classic postcard shot of the oasis surrounded by sand.

    Tip Genuinely early morning or late afternoon light gives the most striking contrast between the green oasis and gold dunes.

Huacachina is genuinely reached by road, a short detour from the nearby city of Ica on Peru's southern coastal route.

Travel tips

Huacachina travel tips

Practical notes that make a Huacachina visit noticeably smoother.

  • Book genuinely a sunset-timed dune buggy tour for the best light and cooler temperatures.
  • Wear genuinely sunglasses and cover your face — sand kicks up significantly during buggy rides.
  • Bring genuinely a jacket for after dark — desert temperatures drop sharply once the sun sets.
  • Pack genuinely a change of clothes — sandboarding and buggy rides get genuinely sandy.
  • Stay genuinely hydrated throughout the day, given the desert heat.
  • Combine genuinely Huacachina with Nazca given the short distance between the two.

FAQ

Huacachina: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Huacachina?

One day genuinely covers the oasis and a dune buggy and sandboarding tour comfortably; an overnight allows a more relaxed pace.

Is Huacachina a real oasis?

Yes, genuinely — it's a real natural lagoon, though tourism now sustains the small village that has grown up around it.

Is sandboarding at Huacachina beginner-friendly?

Yes, genuinely — most tours include basic instruction, and lying flat rather than standing is a common, easier approach.

What is Huacachina known for?

Genuinely its palm-fringed oasis surrounded by towering sand dunes, and the dune buggy and sandboarding activities built around them.

Can you visit Huacachina as a day trip from Lima?

It's genuinely possible but rushed, given the four to five hour drive each way — an overnight stay is more comfortable.

Is Huacachina safe?

Generally yes, with genuinely sensible precautions during dune buggy rides — always use the provided seatbelts and follow your driver's instructions.
