Welcome to San Jose

San Jose is Costa Rica's capital and hub, a busy city of around two million people in the Central Valley. It is not a classic beauty, but it has a handful of excellent sights, a good food scene and a mild climate. Most visitors pass through briefly, and a day or two is enough to see the highlights.

The National Theatre, built in 1897 with coffee money, is the most elegant building, and the nearby Pre-Columbian Gold Museum and Jade Museum hold the country's archaeological treasures. The Central Market is a warren of food stalls, and Barrio Escalante is the trendy district for restaurants and craft beer.

The city is also a base for day trips to Poas and Irazu volcanoes, coffee plantations, and the colonial town of Cartago. Keep an eye on belongings in the centre, and use taxis after dark.
