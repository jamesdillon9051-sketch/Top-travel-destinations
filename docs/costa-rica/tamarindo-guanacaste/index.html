Welcome to Tamarindo and Guanacaste

Guanacaste is the sunniest and driest region of Costa Rica, with a long Pacific coast of beaches, rocky headlands and rolling dry forest. Tamarindo, once a sleepy fishing village, is now the main resort, with surf schools, restaurants and nightlife, while quieter beaches are within easy reach.

Surfing is the big draw, with consistent waves for beginners at Tamarindo and tougher breaks at Playa Grande and Playa Negra. Playa Conchal, a beach of crushed shells, and Flamingo and Brasilito are good for swimming and relaxing. From October to February, leatherback turtles nest at Las Baulas National Marine Park.

Inland, you can take horse rides, zipline over the forest or visit hot springs and waterfalls at Rincon de la Vieja. The dry season, from December to April, is hot and sunny, while the green season brings daily rain and fewer tourists.
