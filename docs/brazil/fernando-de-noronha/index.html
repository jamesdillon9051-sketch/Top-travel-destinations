Welcome to Fernando de Noronha

Fernando de Noronha is an archipelago of 21 islands and islets about 350 km off the coast of north-east Brazil, the tips of an underwater volcanic chain. Only the main island is inhabited, with about 3,000 residents, and about 70% of it is a national marine park. UNESCO inscribed the islands in 2001 as part of the Brazilian Atlantic Islands.

The archipelago's beaches, bays and cliffs are widely considered the best in Brazil, and Baía do Sancho has been ranked among the best beaches in the world. The waters are warm and clear, with visibility often more than 30 metres, and full of marine life, including green turtles, spinner dolphins, rays and sharks. Diving and snorkelling are outstanding.

Because of its protected status, visits are tightly controlled. There are daily visitor limits, a mandatory environmental preservation fee that rises with length of stay, and separate park entry tickets. The island is expensive, but it is also unspoilt, quiet and beautiful, with pousadas, small restaurants and buggies, and a relaxed pace. The dry season from August to January is best.

Why visit

Why visit Fernando de Noronha?

Noronha is one of the best places in Brazil for beaches and marine wildlife, in a carefully protected environment.

  • Superb beaches

    Baía do Sancho, Baía dos Porcos and Praia do Leão, among the best in Brazil.

  • Diving and snorkelling

    Clear water, wrecks and abundant marine life.

  • Dolphins and turtles

    Spinner dolphins gather in the Baía dos Golfinhos, and turtles nest on beaches.

  • A protected paradise

    Visitor limits and strict rules keep it unspoilt.

  • Tranquillity

    A slow pace, sunsets and stars.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Fernando de Noronha

The beaches and viewpoints are close together, and easily reached by buggy or on foot.

  1. 01

    Baía do Sancho

    A hidden bay with white sand and turquoise water below cliffs, reached by a steep path and a ladder in the rock. Widely ranked among the world's best beaches.

    Tip Go early, and do not touch the reef.

  2. 02

    Baía dos Porcos and Praia do Leão

    A bay with natural tidal pools and calm snorkelling, and a beach where turtles nest at night in season.

    Tip Check the tides.

  3. 03

    Morro Dois Irmãos

    The island's iconic twin peaks, which rise from the water at Sancho and Porcos bays.

    Tip Admire them from the beach and the boat.

  4. 04

    Baía dos Golfinhos

    A bay where spinner dolphins gather in the morning, seen from a lookout. Boat approaches are prohibited to protect them.

    Tip Go at sunrise.

  5. 05

    Sea turtles

    Green turtles are often seen snorkelling, and the Tamar Project protects nesting beaches.

    Tip Keep your distance.

  6. 06

    Forte dos Remédios and Vila dos Remédios

    A historic fort and the village, the island's small centre with a church, restaurants and sunset views.

    Tip Visit at sunset.

Things to do

Best things to do in Fernando de Noronha

Water activities dominate, with a few land experiences.

  1. Snorkel with turtles

    Baía do Sancho and Baía dos Porcos have clear water and marine life.

    Tip Use reef-safe sunscreen.

  2. Dive the reefs and wrecks

    Excellent diving for beginners and experts.

    Tip Book with a licensed operator.

  3. Watch dolphins from the lookout

    Spinner dolphins gather in the morning.

    Tip Go early.

  4. Take a boat trip

    Sail around the island, with stops for snorkelling.

    Tip Book a sunset trip.

  5. Go on an island buggy tour

    Explore beaches and viewpoints by rented buggy.

    Tip Drive slowly.

  6. Watch the sunset at Forte dos Remédios

    A traditional way to end the day.

    Tip Bring a jacket.

Local food

What to eat in Fernando de Noronha

Food on the island is seafood, and prices are high because of transport costs.

  • Fresh fish

    Tuna, wahoo and other pelagic fish, grilled or in stews.

    Where to try it Restaurants in Vila dos Remédios.

  • Lobster and seafood

    Locally caught lobster and shrimp.

    Where to try it Pousada restaurants.

  • Tapioca and coconut

    Cassava pancakes, and fresh coconut water.

    Where to try it Beach kiosks.

  • Tropical fruit juices

    Cashew, mango and passionfruit.

    Where to try it Cafés.

  • Caipirinha

    The national cocktail, with local flavours.

    Where to try it Sunset bars.

Accommodation

Where to stay in Fernando de Noronha

Accommodation is mainly small pousadas, and prices are high. Book well ahead.

  • Vila dos Remédios

    Guesthouses and small hotels near the centre.

    Best for: Restaurants and convenience

  • Vila do Trinta and Floresta Velha

    Pousadas in residential areas.

    Best for: A quieter stay

  • Coastal pousadas

    Luxury properties with sea views.

    Best for: Sea views

Transport

Getting to and around Fernando de Noronha

The island is reached only by air, from Recife or Natal.

Getting there

  • Flight from Recife

    About 1 hour 20 minutes, with daily flights.

    Book early, as seats are limited.

  • Flight from Natal

    About 1 hour.

    Daily flights.

  • Cruise or private boat

    Some cruises stop at the island, but landing is limited.

    Check the rules.

Getting around

The island is small, and buggies are the main way to move.

  • Buggy rental

    Popular and flexible.

    Drive slowly.

  • Taxis and shuttles

    Serve the main roads and beaches.

    Agree the fare.

  • Walking

    Trails to some beaches.

    Wear sun protection.

When to go

Best time to visit Fernando de Noronha

Noronha is warm year-round, with a dry season from August to January and a rainy season from February to July.

  • Dry season (August–October)

    Best time

    Clear skies, calm seas and the best visibility.

  • Late dry (November–January)

    Best time

    Hot and dry, with good conditions.

  • Rainy season (February–April)

    Quiet

    Warm and showery, with rougher seas and low prices.

  • Late rainy (May–July)

    Good

    Showers, with big surf on the north.

Worth planning around: The wettest months, from March to June.

Travel tips

Fernando de Noronha travel tips

The island has strict rules and high prices.

  • Book flights and accommodation many months ahead.
  • Pay the environmental fee and buy park tickets in advance.
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen and do not touch marine life.
  • Do not use plastic straws or bring single-use items.
  • Bring cash, since ATMs are limited.
  • Follow all park rules.
  • Stay a minimum of four days to make the trip worthwhile.

FAQ

Fernando de Noronha: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Fernando de Noronha?

Four to five days is ideal.

Is it expensive?

Yes. Accommodation, food and fees are high, partly because of the island's isolation and protected status.

What is the best time to visit?

August to January.

Can I swim with dolphins?

No. Contact with spinner dolphins is prohibited. You can watch them from the lookout.

How do I get there?

By flight from Recife or Natal.

Do I need to pay fees?

Yes, an environmental preservation fee and a national park entry ticket. Check current prices.
