Welcome to Lençóis Maranhenses

The Lençóis Maranhenses National Park is a strip of coastline in the state of Maranhão, about 70 km long and 50 km deep, where huge white sand dunes roll inland from the Atlantic. The name means the bedsheets of Maranhão, from the way the dunes look like sheets spread across the land. It is not a desert, since the region receives heavy rain from January to June, and the water collects in hollows between the dunes, forming thousands of lagoons that can be several metres deep.

The lagoons are at their fullest from about June to September, when the water is a striking blue or green against white sand, and in the dry months from November to January many dry out. The dunes are made of quartz sand blown from the coast, and they move each year with the wind. Fish arrive in the lagoons from the rivers and the sea, and survive the dry season buried in the mud as eggs.

The park is remote and uncrowded. Most visitors base themselves at Barreirinhas, the gateway town, or at small villages such as Santo Amaro and Atins, and explore the dunes on 4x4 tours or on foot. It is a landscape that photographs superbly, and a good addition to a longer trip in north-east Brazil.

Why visit

Why visit Lençóis Maranhenses?

Lençóis Maranhenses is a uniquely beautiful landscape, and one of the great natural wonders of Brazil.

  • Dunes and lagoons

    Wave after wave of white sand with clear lagoons between them.

  • Swimming in the dunes

    The lagoons are safe and warm, ideal for a swim.

  • Remote and quiet

    Far from large crowds and cities.

  • River and coast

    Boat trips on the Preguiças River and the Atlantic coast.

  • Adventure

    4x4 tours, multi-day hikes and kitesurfing.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Lençóis Maranhenses

There are several entry points to the park, each with a different style of visit.

  1. 01

    Lagoa Azul and Lagoa Bonita

    Two of the best-known lagoons, near Barreirinhas, reached by 4x4 and a short walk through the dunes.

    Tip Go at sunset for the light and cooler temperatures.

  2. 02

    Santo Amaro do Maranhão

    A small village on the eastern edge of the dunes, with lagoons that can be reached on foot from the village, and a quiet atmosphere.

    Tip A good base for multi-day treks.

  3. 03

    Rio Preguiças boat trip

    A river trip from Barreirinhas past mangroves, dunes, the fishing village of Mandacaru and the Vassouras dunes, ending at the lighthouse.

    Tip Bring a hat and sunscreen.

  4. 04

    Atins

    A remote beach village at the river mouth, with kitesurfing, fresh fish and quiet dunes right at the coast.

    Tip Best reached by boat or 4x4.

  5. 05

    The dunes at sunset

    The dunes change colour as the sun sets, and the lagoons reflect the sky. It is the most popular time for tours.

    Tip Bring a flashlight for the walk back.

Things to do

Best things to do in Lençóis Maranhenses

Tours and guides are the main way to see the dunes.

  1. Swim in a lagoon

    Wade and swim in warm, clear water, in the middle of the dunes.

    Tip Go from June to September.

  2. Take a 4x4 tour to the dunes

    A bumpy ride to the lagoons, with time to walk and swim.

    Tip Book a small group.

  3. Do a multi-day trek

    The Travessia is a walk across the dunes from Atins to Santo Amaro, with overnight stays in villages.

    Tip Go with a guide.

  4. Ride down the Preguiças River

    See mangroves, monkeys and dunes from a boat.

    Tip Book a half- or full-day trip.

  5. Try kitesurfing at Atins

    Strong, steady winds make the coast a kite destination.

    Tip Best from August to December.

  6. Fly over the dunes

    A small-plane flight gives a view of the lagoons.

    Tip Book in advance.

Local food

What to eat in Lençóis Maranhenses

Maranhão's food is fish- and shellfish-based, with regional specialities.

  • Arroz de cuxá

    Rice with a paste of roasted sesame seeds, sorrel and dried shrimp, a Maranhão speciality.

    Where to try it Local restaurants.

  • Fresh fish and shrimp

    Grilled or fried, with rice and beans.

    Where to try it Riverside restaurants.

  • Torta de camarão

    A shrimp pie.

    Where to try it Restaurants in Barreirinhas.

  • Juçara açaí

    Local açaí, eaten as a thick bowl.

    Where to try it Cafés.

  • Tropical fruit and cachaça

    Juices and local cachaça.

    Where to try it Bars.

Transport

Getting to and around Lençóis Maranhenses

Barreirinhas is about 260 km east of São Luís, the state capital.

Getting there

  • Flight to São Luís (SLZ)

    Direct flights from Brasília, São Paulo, Rio and other cities.

    Then a 3 to 4-hour transfer.

  • Transfer to Barreirinhas

    Vans and buses run from São Luís to Barreirinhas, about 3 to 4 hours.

    Book in advance.

  • Onward to Jericoacoara

    A popular route by 4x4 and boat, called the Rota das Emoções.

    Book a tour.

Getting around

Tours are the main way to move, since the park is roadless.

  • 4x4 tours

    Vehicles run from Barreirinhas to the dunes.

    Choose a reputable operator.

  • Boat

    On the Rio Preguiças.

    Wear a life jacket.

  • On foot

    Walking in the dunes is the best way to see the lagoons.

    Go with a guide.

When to go

Best time to visit Lençóis Maranhenses

The rainy season from January to June fills the lagoons, which are at their fullest between June and September, and start to dry from October.

  • Wet season (January–May)

    Good

    Rain fills the lagoons, but skies can be cloudy.

  • Peak lagoons (June–September)

    Best time

    Lagoons full, skies clear and days sunny.

  • Drying (October–November)

    Good

    Lagoons shrink, with hot, windy weather.

  • Dry (December)

    Think twice

    Many lagoons are dry.

Worth planning around: November to January, when most lagoons have dried up.

Travel tips

Lençóis Maranhenses travel tips

A few tips help you get the best from the dunes.

  • Visit from June to September for full lagoons.
  • Take a hat, sunscreen and water, since there is no shade.
  • Go at sunset for cooler temperatures.
  • Book a reputable tour and guide.
  • Bring a flashlight and cash.
  • Do not leave litter, and stay on marked areas.
  • Book accommodation ahead in July.

FAQ

Lençóis Maranhenses: frequently asked questions

When are the lagoons full?

From about June to September, after the rainy season.

How many days do you need?

Three days is enough for the dunes, river and coast.

Is it a desert?

No. It receives heavy rain, which fills the lagoons.

Can I swim in the lagoons?

Yes. They are safe, warm and clear.

How do I get there?

Fly to São Luís, then take a van or car to Barreirinhas, about 3 to 4 hours.

Can I visit without a tour?

Most areas require a tour or guide, since the dunes are confusing.
