Welcome to the Garden Route

The Garden Route runs for about 300 km along the south coast of South Africa, between the towns of Mossel Bay and Storms River, in a strip of land squeezed between the Outeniqua and Tsitsikamma Mountains and the Indian Ocean. Its name comes from the lush green landscape, in which lagoons, lakes, rivers, indigenous forest, fynbos and beaches follow one another, and it is one of the country's most popular tourist destinations.

The main towns each have a distinct character. Wilderness has lagoons and dunes, Knysna has a large lagoon with two rocky Heads at its mouth and a forest full of woodcarvers and cafés, Plettenberg Bay has some of the country's best beaches and the Robberg peninsula, and Tsitsikamma National Park has coastal forest, sea cliffs and a famous suspension bridge. Inland, Oudtshoorn is the ostrich capital, with the spectacular Cango Caves.

It is a place for driving, walking, kayaking and adventure. The Bloukrans Bridge has the world's highest commercial bridge bungee jump, whales and dolphins can be seen from the shore, and indigenous forests hold big yellowwood trees. It is a highlight of any South African trip, and best explored at a leisurely pace, with at least four or five days.

Why visit

Why visit the Garden Route?

The Garden Route is one of the great road trips in Africa, with beauty and variety at every turn.

  • Lagoons and forests

    Knysna's lagoon and the Tsitsikamma forests are highlights.

  • Beaches and coves

    Long, quiet beaches at Plettenberg Bay and Nature's Valley.

  • Adventure

    Bungee, zip lines, kayaking, hiking and surfing.

  • Wildlife

    Whales, dolphins, seals, monkeys, birds and elephants nearby.

  • Small towns

    Craft shops, oysters and easy-going restaurants.

Main attractions

Top things to see in the Garden Route

The route is best done with stops of a night or two in each area.

  1. 01

    Knysna Lagoon and the Heads

    A large estuary lagoon with two sandstone cliffs, the Heads, at its mouth. Boat trips, oyster farms and viewpoints make it the route's hub.

    Tip Try a sunset boat cruise or fresh oysters.

  2. 02

    Tsitsikamma National Park and Storms River Mouth

    A coastal national park of forest and cliffs, with a 77-metre suspension bridge over the river mouth, walks, kayaking and the multi-day Otter Trail.

    Tip Walk to the bridge in the morning.

  3. 03

    Plettenberg Bay and Robberg

    A resort town with white beaches, dolphins and whales offshore, and the Robberg Nature Reserve, a rocky peninsula with a seal colony and cliff-top walks.

    Tip Walk the Robberg circuit.

  4. 04

    Wilderness

    A small village of lagoons, forests and long beaches, with kayaking, paragliding and a national park.

    Tip Kayak the Touw River.

  5. 05

    Bloukrans Bridge

    A 216-metre bridge over a gorge with the world's highest commercial bridge bungee jump, and a walking tour under the arch.

    Tip Book in advance.

  6. 06

    Oudtshoorn and the Cango Caves

    The centre of the ostrich industry in the Little Karoo, with farms and a spectacular limestone cave system, about an hour's drive inland from George.

    Tip Book a caves tour, and visit an ostrich farm.

Things to do

Best things to do in the Garden Route

Outdoor activities and scenic stops fill the days.

  1. Walk to the Storms River suspension bridge

    A short coastal trail leads to a wobbly bridge across the river mouth.

    Tip Go in the morning.

  2. Kayak on the sea or a river

    Paddle up a river gorge in Tsitsikamma or along the coast.

    Tip Book in advance.

  3. Go whale and dolphin watching

    Boat trips from Plettenberg Bay from June to November.

    Tip Choose a licensed operator.

  4. Do the bungee jump

    Leap from the Bloukrans Bridge if you dare.

    Tip Book ahead.

  5. Eat oysters in Knysna

    The town is famous for its oysters, particularly during the Oyster Festival in July.

    Tip Try them fresh from the lagoon.

  6. Hike in the forest

    Trails such as the Otter Trail and shorter walks through indigenous forest.

    Tip Book the Otter Trail far in advance.

Local food

What to eat in the Garden Route

The Garden Route's food is seafood, dairy and local produce, served in relaxed restaurants.

  • Knysna oysters

    Plump, briny oysters farmed in the lagoon.

    Where to try it Waterfront restaurants in Knysna.

  • Fresh fish and calamari

    Line fish, calamari and prawns.

    Where to try it Harbour restaurants.

  • Ostrich meat

    Lean red meat, served as steaks and carpaccio.

    Where to try it Oudtshoorn.

  • Craft beer

    Local microbreweries.

    Where to try it Knysna and Plettenberg Bay.

  • Farm stall lunches

    Homemade pies, cakes and jams at roadside farm stalls.

    Where to try it Along the N2.

Transport

Getting to and around the Garden Route

The Garden Route is best reached by hire car.

Getting there

  • Drive from Cape Town

    About 5 to 6 hours to Knysna, along the N2 highway.

    Allow time for stops.

  • Fly to George

    Direct flights from Cape Town and Johannesburg.

    Pick up a hire car.

  • Drive from Gqeberha

    About 2 to 3 hours to Plettenberg Bay.

    Combine with Addo.

Getting around

A car is best, though the Baz Bus links hostels.

  • Hire car

    Good roads, with easy driving.

    Avoid driving after dark.

  • Baz Bus

    A hop-on hop-off bus for backpackers.

    Book in advance.

  • Tours

    Day tours from towns.

    Book in advance.

When to go

Best time to visit the Garden Route

The Garden Route has a mild, oceanic climate, with rain all year and warm summers.

  • Summer (December–February)

    Best time

    Warm and sunny, and the busiest time.

  • Autumn (March–May)

    Best time

    Warm and calm, with fewer crowds.

  • Winter (June–August)

    Quiet

    Cool and wet, with whales and low prices.

  • Spring (September–November)

    Good

    Mild, with whales and wildflowers.

Worth planning around: The Christmas and Easter holidays if you dislike crowds.

Travel tips

The Garden Route travel tips

A few tips make the drive better.

  • Book accommodation ahead for December and Easter.
  • Stay at least two nights in each area.
  • Drive in daylight, and be careful with livestock on roads.
  • Book the Otter Trail, Bloukrans and popular activities early.
  • Check whale-watching seasons.
  • Bring layers and a rain jacket.
  • Try oysters and local craft beer.

FAQ

The Garden Route: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need for the Garden Route?

Four or five days, with more if you add Oudtshoorn or Addo.

What is the best time to visit?

November to April.

Do I need a car?

It is the best way, though the Baz Bus and tours are available.

Is the Garden Route safe?

Generally yes, with normal precautions.

Is it malaria-free?

Yes.

Can I drive it from Cape Town in a day?

Knysna is 5 to 6 hours away, but it is best to take it slowly.
