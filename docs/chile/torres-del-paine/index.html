Welcome to Torres del Paine

Torres del Paine National Park is the crown jewel of Chilean Patagonia. Its landscape combines the sheer granite spires of the Paine massif, the striped Cuernos, glacial lakes in impossible shades of blue, and the huge Grey Glacier, set in a windswept steppe of guanacos and condors. It is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve.

Most visitors come to hike. The W Trek takes four to five days, linking the Grey Glacier, the French Valley and the Base Torres viewpoint, while the O Circuit adds the remote back side of the massif in seven to ten days. Those short of time can do day hikes, boat trips and drives, and wildlife viewing is excellent, with guanacos, foxes, condors and, with luck, pumas.

The weather is notoriously changeable, and strong winds are common. Refugios and campsites must be reserved well in advance for the summer season, usually many months ahead. Puerto Natales is the base town, with gear shops, restaurants and tour operators.

Why visit

Why visit Torres del Paine?

Torres del Paine offers some of the best hiking scenery in the world.

  • Granite towers

    Iconic peaks in the Andes.

  • Glaciers and lakes

    Grey Glacier and turquoise water.

  • Wildlife

    Guanacos, condors and pumas.

  • Trekking options

    W, O and day hikes.

  • Patagonian steppe

    Big skies and wind.

When to go

Best time to visit Torres del Paine

Summer days are long and trails are open, but winds are strong.

  • November-December

    Good

    Spring flowers and fewer crowds.

  • January-February

    Best time

    Long days, but busy.

  • March-April

    Best time

    Autumn colours and calm.

  • May-October

    Think twice

    Cold and short days; many facilities closed.

Worth planning around: Winter, when trails and services close.

Travel tips

Torres del Paine travel tips

Book early and prepare for the weather.

  • Reserve refugios and campsites months ahead.
  • Layer clothing and bring waterproofs.
  • Use trekking poles.
  • Carry a windproof jacket.
  • Pack food and water.
  • Respect Leave No Trace.
  • Check weather daily.
