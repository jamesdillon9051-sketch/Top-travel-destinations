Welcome to Santiago

Santiago sits in a broad valley between the Andes and the coastal range, home to about seven million people. On a clear winter day the snow-covered mountains rise right behind the skyline, and few capitals have such a dramatic backdrop. The city is modern, orderly and well connected, and most visitors pass through it at the beginning or end of a trip.

The historic centre is compact and walkable, with the Plaza de Armas, the cathedral, the Presidential Palace of La Moneda and the central market, Mercado Central, famed for its seafood. Barrios such as Lastarria and Bellavista have cafes, street art and nightlife, while Cerro San Cristobal and Cerro Santa Lucia give green space and views. The Museo de la Memoria and the Museo Chileno de Arte Precolombino provide context on the country's history.

Santiago is also the centre of Chilean wine. Several well-known estates in the Maipo Valley are less than an hour away, and the Casablanca and Colchagua valleys make longer day trips. In winter, ski fields such as Valle Nevado and La Parva are within two hours.

Why visit

Why visit Santiago?

Santiago is an easy, enjoyable city that rewards a couple of days.

  • Mountain backdrop

    The Andes frame the city.

  • Museums and history

    From pre-Columbian art to modern memory.

  • Food and wine

    Seafood, markets and Maipo vineyards.

  • Barrios with character

    Lastarria, Bellavista and Barrio Italia.

  • Ski and hike

    Mountains within two hours.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Santiago

Most sights are within the central districts and can be reached by metro.

  1. 01

    Plaza de Armas

    The historic heart, with the cathedral and the central post office.

    Tip Visit in the morning.

  2. 02

    La Moneda Palace

    The presidential palace, with a cultural centre underneath.

    Tip Check opening times for tours.

  3. 03

    Cerro San Cristobal

    A hilltop park with a funicular, zoo and viewpoint.

    Tip Go late afternoon for the light.

  4. 04

    Mercado Central

    A market hall famous for fish and seafood stews.

    Tip Eat lunch away from the tourist stalls.

  5. 05

    Lastarria and Bellavista

    Bohemian districts with street art, cafes and the Neruda house.

    Tip Wander in the evening.

  6. 06

    Museo de la Memoria

    A museum about the military dictatorship years.

    Tip Allow two hours.

Things to do

Best things to do in Santiago

Santiago offers a mix of culture and outdoor time.

  1. Ride the funicular

    Up Cerro San Cristobal.

    Tip Buy a combined ticket.

  2. Eat at the central market

    Seafood stews and ceviche.

    Tip Ask for the day's special.

  3. Tour a Maipo winery

    Tastings of Carmenere and Cabernet.

    Tip Book ahead.

  4. Walk Lastarria

    Cafes, galleries and street art.

    Tip Go on a weekend.

  5. See the Andes

    Day trip to Cajon del Maipo.

    Tip Hire a driver.

  6. Ski in winter

    At Valle Nevado or La Parva.

    Tip Book early.

Transport

Getting to and around Santiago

Santiago is the main international gateway.

Getting there

  • Fly to SCL

    Direct flights from the Americas, Europe and Oceania.

    Transfer by shuttle or taxi.

  • Bus from Valparaiso

    About 1.5 hours.

    Frequent services.

  • Bus from Mendoza

    About 7 hours over the Andes.

    Check pass closures in winter.

Getting around

The metro is the easiest way around.

  • Metro

    Clean and cheap.

    Use a Bip! card.

  • Taxi and rideshare

    Convenient in the evenings.

    Use apps.

  • On foot

    The centre is compact.

    Watch pickpockets.

When to go

Best time to visit Santiago

Autumn and spring give mild weather and clear skies, with wine harvest in March and April.

  • March-May

    Best time

    Mild days and the grape harvest.

  • September-November

    Best time

    Spring flowers and comfortable temperatures.

  • December-February

    Good

    Hot and dry, with fewer locals in January.

  • June-August

    Good

    Cool, with occasional smog and nearby skiing.

Worth planning around: Late June and July if you dislike cold and smog.

Travel tips

Santiago travel tips

A few tips for the city.

  • Use the metro, which is fast and safe.
  • Watch for pickpockets in crowded areas.
  • Book winery visits in advance.
  • Carry ID or a copy.
  • Expect late dinners.
  • Learn basic Spanish.
  • Check air quality in winter.
