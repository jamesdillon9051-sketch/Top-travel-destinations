Welcome to Puerto Varas and the Lake District

Puerto Varas is a lakeside town built by German settlers in the 19th century, and its wooden houses, kuchen cafes and church give it a distinct European feel. It faces the glassy Lake Llanquihue, with the snow-capped cone of Osorno volcano across the water and the smoking Calbuco nearby.

It is a gateway to the Chilean Lake District. Vicente Perez Rosales National Park, the oldest in Chile, includes the Petrohue Falls and Lake Todos los Santos, with its emerald water. Visitors can hike, kayak, raft, ski on Osorno in winter or take a ferry across the Andes to Argentina.

The town is also close to Puerto Montt and Chiloe, making it a good base for a mix of nature and culture. It is quiet, easy and comfortable, and a good place to slow down after the more demanding parts of Chile.
