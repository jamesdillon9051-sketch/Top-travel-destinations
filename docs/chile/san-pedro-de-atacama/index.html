Welcome to San Pedro de Atacama

San Pedro de Atacama is a small oasis of adobe buildings and dusty streets on the edge of the driest non-polar desert in the world. At 2,400 metres, it serves as the base for exploring a landscape of salt flats, volcanoes, lagoons and geysers, all within a few hours of town.

Tours leave at all hours, from sunrise trips to the El Tatio geysers, where steam rises at 4,300 metres, to afternoon excursions to the Valle de la Luna, where sunset turns the rocks red. The Salar de Atacama has flamingo lagoons, and Laguna Cejar and other salt lakes allow floating like in the Dead Sea. At night, the skies are famously clear and local operators run astronomy tours.

The altitude and dryness are real factors, so plan an easy first day, drink water and wear sun protection. Tours are the main way to see the area, though bicycles can reach nearby sights.

Why visit

Why visit San Pedro de Atacama?

San Pedro is one of the most dramatic natural destinations in South America.

  • Otherworldly landscapes

    Dunes, salt flats and volcanoes.

  • Stargazing

    Some of the clearest skies in the world.

  • Geysers

    El Tatio at sunrise.

  • Floating lagoons

    Swim in salt water.

  • Flamingos

    Birds in high lagoons.

Travel tips

San Pedro de Atacama travel tips

Prepare for altitude.

  • Take it slow on arrival day.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Wear sun protection.
  • Bring layers for cold mornings.
  • Book tours in advance.
  • Carry cash for small purchases.
  • Use reputable operators.
