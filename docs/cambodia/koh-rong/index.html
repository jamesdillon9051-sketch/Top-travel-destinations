Welcome to Koh Rong and Koh Rong Samloem

Koh Rong and Koh Rong Samloem are the best known of Cambodia's islands, with long white beaches, clear water and jungle-covered hills. Koh Rong is bigger and livelier, with a party scene at Koh Touch, while Koh Rong Samloem is calmer and more upmarket.

The islands are reached by ferry from Sihanoukville in 45 minutes to two hours. Once there, activities include snorkelling, kayaking, jungle trekking, and watching bioluminescent plankton glow at night. Many resorts are simple bungalows, with limited electricity and mobile coverage.

The dry season, from November to May, brings calm seas, while the rainy season can cancel ferries and close some businesses. Development is changing the islands quickly, so check recent reviews.
