Welcome to Angkor Archaeological Park

Angkor was the capital of the Khmer Empire, which ruled much of South-East Asia from the 9th to the 15th century. The temples left behind are the largest religious monuments in the world, spread across forest and rice fields near Siem Reap, and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Angkor Wat, built in the early 12th century for King Suryavarman II, is the most famous, a vast temple mountain with five towers, a moat and miles of carved bas-reliefs. A few kilometres north, Angkor Thom was the last capital, and its Bayon temple is crowned by hundreds of serene stone faces. Ta Prohm is left half-swallowed by silk-cotton and strangler fig trees.

The temples are best visited over two or three days, beginning at dawn to see the light and avoid the heat. Tuk-tuk drivers act as guides and drivers, and a three-day or seven-day pass covers the park. Modest dress is required, with shoulders and knees covered, and the area can be crowded, especially at sunrise.

    The world's largest religious monument.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Angkor Archaeological Park

The park is divided into the main circuits.

  1. 01

    Angkor Wat

    The grand temple, with bas-reliefs and towers.

    Tip Visit at sunrise or late afternoon.

  2. 02

    Bayon

    The state temple of Angkor Thom, with its giant faces.

    Tip Go in the morning.

  3. 03

    Ta Prohm

    A temple wrapped in giant tree roots.

    Tip Arrive early to beat crowds.

  4. 04

    Angkor Thom South Gate

    A massive gate with stone guardians.

    Tip Photogenic.

  5. 05

    Phnom Bakheng

    A hilltop temple, popular at sunset.

    Tip Queues form; go early.

  6. 06

    Banteay Kdei and Srah Srang

    A quieter temple and reservoir.

    Tip Good for a restful stop.

When to go

Best time to visit Angkor Archaeological Park

The cool, dry season is the best time to visit, though it is also the busiest.

  • November-February

    Best time

    Cool, dry and sunny.

  • March-May

    Think twice

    Very hot.

  • June-October

    Quiet

    Green season, with rain showers.

  • Early November

    Good

    Just after the rains.

Travel tips

Angkor Archaeological Park travel tips

Tips for visiting.

  • Buy your pass at the official ticket office.
  • Cover shoulders and knees.
  • Start early to avoid heat.
  • Bring water and sunscreen.
  • Hire a licensed guide.
  • Respect worshippers at active shrines.
  • Do not climb restricted structures.
