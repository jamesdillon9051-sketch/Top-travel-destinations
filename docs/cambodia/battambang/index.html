Welcome to Battambang

Battambang is Cambodia's second-largest city but feels like a quiet provincial town, with faded French-colonial shopfronts, a river, and a creative community. It is a favourite stop between Siem Reap and Phnom Penh, and many travellers stay longer than planned.

The famous bamboo train, or norry, is a bamboo platform on rails powered by a small engine, and gives a fun if touristy ride. Nearby, Wat Banan sits on a hilltop with five towers, Phnom Sampov holds a cave where millions of bats stream out at dusk, and the countryside is dotted with villages and rice fields.

Art is a theme here. Phare Ponleu Selpak, a non-profit arts school, offers circus performances, and small galleries and cafes show local artists. It is an easy place to relax, with good food and a gentle pace.
