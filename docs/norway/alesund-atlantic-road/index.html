Welcome to Ålesund & the Atlantic Road

Ålesund is genuinely one of Norway's most architecturally distinctive towns, rebuilt almost entirely in Art Nouveau style after a catastrophic 1904 fire destroyed nearly the whole town, with Kaiser Wilhelm II of Germany personally funding part of the reconstruction.

The result is genuinely striking — turrets, spires and ornate facades spread across several small islands connected by bridges, best appreciated from the Aksla viewpoint above town, reached by a genuinely worthwhile climb of 418 steps.

Nearby, the Atlantic Road adds a completely different kind of drama — a short stretch of highway hopping across small islands on low bridges, with the open Norwegian Sea crashing on either side, genuinely ranking among the most memorable short drives anywhere in the world.
