Welcome to Trondheim

Trondheim is genuinely Norway's historic pilgrimage capital, founded over a thousand years ago and still centred on Nidaros Cathedral, the country's largest medieval building and the traditional burial place of Norwegian kings.

The city's genuine character today mixes that deep history with a large student population, giving central Trondheim a livelier, younger feel than its medieval core might suggest — cafés and bars fill the colourful wooden warehouses of Bakklandet that once served the city's river trade.

It's also genuinely a practical stop on a longer Norway trip, sitting roughly midway along the country's length and serving as a stop on the Hurtigruten coastal ferry route between the fjords of the south and the Arctic north.
