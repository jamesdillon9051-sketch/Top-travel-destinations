Welcome to Bern

Bern is the capital of Switzerland, or more precisely its 'federal city', since the Swiss constitution does not name a capital. It is a small city of about 140,000 people, set on a hill in a tight loop of the Aare river, and it is much calmer than Zurich or Geneva. The old town was founded in 1191 and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with about six kilometres of covered arcades lined with shops, cellars and cafés.

Its landmarks are easy to find. The Zytglogge is a medieval clock tower with an astronomical clock that performs a small show every hour, the Münster is Switzerland's tallest cathedral, and the Bundeshaus, the Federal Palace, is open for tours. Along the streets, colourful fountains from the 16th century carry statues, and the Bear Park by the river keeps bears, the city's symbol.

In summer, locals jump in the Aare and drift downstream to the pools, a joyful and unusual ritual. Bern is also where Albert Einstein developed his theory of relativity while working in a patent office, and his apartment on Kramgasse is a small museum. A day covers the old town, and it makes a good stop between Zurich and the mountains.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Bern

The old town is compact and walkable.

  1. 01

    Zytglogge

    A medieval clock tower with an astronomical clock from 1530, which puts on a show four minutes before each hour.

    Tip Watch from Kramgasse.

  2. 02

    Bundeshaus

    The Swiss Federal Palace, home to parliament, open for free guided tours when not in session.

    Tip Book and bring ID.

  3. 03

    Rosengarten

    A hillside garden above the river with the best view of the old town.

    Tip Go at sunset.

  4. 04

    Old town arcades

    Six kilometres of covered arcades, with shops, cellar bars and fountains.

    Tip Wander Marktgasse and Kramgasse.

  5. 05

    Bern Münster

    A late-Gothic cathedral with Switzerland's highest church tower, at 100 metres.

    Tip Climb the tower.

  6. 06

    Einstein House

    The apartment where Einstein lived from 1903 to 1905.

    Tip Small, cheap and quick.

Things to do

Best things to do in Bern

Bern is for strolling and swimming.

  1. Swim in the Aare

    Float from Eichholz to the Marzili pools.

    Tip Only if you are a confident swimmer.

  2. Walk the old town

    Follow the arcades and fountains.

    Tip Go early.

  3. Visit the Bear Park

    See bears by the river.

    Tip Free.

  4. Go up Gurten

    A hill with views, reached by funicular.

    Tip Good for families.

  5. Visit Zentrum Paul Klee

    A museum of Klee's works in a wavy building.

    Tip Take a bus.

  6. Shop at the Saturday market

    Local produce and cheese.

    Tip Go in the morning.

Travel tips

Bern travel tips

A few tips help you enjoy Bern.

  • Watch the clock show early.
  • Visit the Rosengarten at sunset.
  • Swim only if confident, since the river is fast.
  • Book Bundeshaus tours ahead.
  • Browse the Saturday market.
  • Ask your hotel for a transport pass.
  • Combine with Interlaken.

FAQ

Bern: frequently asked questions

Is Bern worth visiting?

Yes, for its old town and relaxed feel.

How long do I need?

One day.

Is Bern the capital?

It is the federal city, the seat of government.

Can I swim in the Aare?

Yes in summer, but the current is strong.

What is Bern known for?

Its old town, bears and the clock tower.

Is it a UNESCO site?

Yes, the old town was listed in 1983.
