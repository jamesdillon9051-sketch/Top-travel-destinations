Welcome to Lucerne

Lucerne is a compact city of about 85,000 people at the point where the Reuss river leaves Lake Lucerne, ringed by mountains. Its symbol is the Chapel Bridge, a covered wooden footbridge from the 14th century with painted panels and an octagonal stone water tower, which was rebuilt after a fire in 1993. The old town has painted facades, medieval walls and squares, and the whole centre can be walked in an afternoon.

The setting is what makes Lucerne special. Mount Pilatus, legendary as the home of dragons, and Rigi, the 'Queen of the Mountains', both rise from the lake, and both can be climbed by cog railway and cable car. Historic paddle steamers cross the lake to villages such as Vitznau and Weggis, and a day's excursion combines a boat, a mountain and a train ride.

Lucerne is also a place with culture. The Lion Monument, carved into a cliff in the 1820s, commemorates Swiss guards killed in Paris in 1792, the Swiss Museum of Transport is one of Europe's best, and the KKL concert hall hosts a major music festival each summer. It is about 45 minutes from Zurich, making it an easy first base.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Lucerne

The old town is small, and the mountains are half-day excursions.

  1. 01

    Chapel Bridge (Kapellbrücke)

    A covered wooden bridge with painted triangular panels of local history, and a water tower that once served as a prison.

    Tip Go early or at sunset.

  2. 02

    Lion Monument (Löwendenkmal)

    A dying lion carved into a sandstone cliff to honour the Swiss Guards who died defending the Tuileries in 1792.

    Tip Free to visit.

  3. 03

    Mount Pilatus

    A 2,132-metre mountain reached by the world's steepest cog railway from Alpnachstad, or by cable car from Kriens, with views and walks at the top.

    Tip Make a loop: boat, train up, cable car down.

  4. 04

    Rigi

    A 1,798-metre mountain with wide views and Europe's oldest mountain railway, opened in 1871.

    Tip Go up on a clear morning.

  5. 05

    Old Town and Spreuer Bridge

    Painted houses, squares and a second wooden bridge with a Dance of Death painting cycle.

    Tip Walk the city wall towers.

  6. 06

    Swiss Museum of Transport

    A big museum of trains, planes and cars, popular with families.

    Tip Allow half a day.

Things to do

Best things to do in Lucerne

Lucerne combines a town, a lake and a mountain.

  1. Cruise Lake Lucerne

    Take a paddle steamer to Vitznau or Weggis.

    Tip Boats are included in the Swiss Travel Pass.

  2. Climb Pilatus

    Cog railway up and cable car down.

    Tip Check the weather.

  3. Ride the Rigi railway

    Europe's oldest mountain railway.

    Tip Go up from Vitznau.

  4. Walk the city wall

    Climb the Museggmauer towers.

    Tip Open in summer.

  5. Attend a concert

    The Lucerne Festival in summer.

    Tip Book early.

  6. Swim in the lake

    Public beaches in summer.

    Tip Cold water.

  • Luzerner Lebkuchen

    Spiced gingerbread.

    Where to try it Bakeries.

    Alpine cheese dishes.

  • Swiss chocolate

    Chocolate from local makers.

When to go

Best time to visit Lucerne

Lucerne is mild, with warm summers and cool, grey winters.

  • Spring (April–May)

    Good

    Blossom and quiet.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Best time

    Long days and lake cruises.

  • Autumn (September–October)

    Best time

    Clear views and colour.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Quiet

    Christmas markets, and snow in the mountains.

Worth planning around: Foggy days in November and January, when the mountains are hidden.

Itinerary

Two days in Lucerne

The town on the first day, and a mountain on the second.

  1. Day1

    The old town

    Bridges and lion.

    • Morning Chapel Bridge and the old town.
    • Afternoon Lion Monument and Spreuer Bridge.
    • Evening Dinner on the lake.
  2. Day2

    A mountain

    Pilatus or Rigi.

    • Morning Boat and cog railway to the summit.
    • Afternoon Walk and lunch at the top.
    • Evening Return to Lucerne.

Travel tips

Lucerne travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Go up mountains in the morning, before clouds build.
  • Check webcams first.
  • Make a loop rather than returning the same way.
  • Use lake boats with a pass.
  • Book hotels early for the summer festival.
  • Visit the bridge early or late.
  • Bring a warm layer for the summits.

FAQ

Lucerne: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Lucerne?

Two to three days.

Is Lucerne worth it?

Yes. It is one of the most beautiful Swiss towns.

Pilatus or Rigi?

Pilatus is more dramatic. Rigi has broader views and a gentler feel.

How far is Lucerne from Zurich?

About 45 minutes by train.

Can I swim in the lake?

Yes in summer, at public beaches.

Is the Chapel Bridge original?

It was largely rebuilt after a fire in 1993.
