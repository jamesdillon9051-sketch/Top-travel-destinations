Welcome to Geneva

Geneva sits at the south-western tip of Lake Geneva, where the Rhône flows out toward France, and it is surrounded by French territory on three sides. It is the second-largest city in Switzerland, home to about 200,000 people, and one of the most international, with the European headquarters of the United Nations, the Red Cross, the World Health Organization and dozens of other organisations.

The city's landmark is the Jet d'Eau, a fountain in the harbour that shoots water about 140 metres into the air, visible from across the city. On the left bank, the old town climbs a hill to St. Peter's Cathedral, where John Calvin preached, and the Reformation Wall in Parc des Bastions celebrates the leaders of the Protestant movement. Along the lakeshore, promenades, parks and swimming spots run for kilometres, and on clear days Mont Blanc floats on the horizon.

Geneva is also a city of museums, watches and chocolate, and a base for exploring Lake Geneva, the Jura and Chamonix, an hour away in France. It is expensive, but rewarding, and two days is enough for the main sights.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Geneva

The sights are compact, on both sides of the lake and river.

  1. 01

    Jet d'Eau

    A fountain that shoots 500 litres of water per second to a height of about 140 metres, installed in 1891 and now the city's symbol.

    Tip Walk out along the pier to feel the spray.

  2. 02

    Lake Geneva promenade

    A lakeside walk from the Jet d'Eau to the Bains des Pâquis, with views to the Alps and swimming platforms.

    Tip Swim at the Bains des Pâquis in summer.

  3. 03

    St. Peter's Cathedral

    The Protestant cathedral where John Calvin preached, with a tower climb for views and an archaeological site beneath.

    Tip Climb the tower.

  4. 04

    Reformation Wall

    A 100-metre wall in Parc des Bastions with large statues of Calvin, Farel, Beza and Knox, and other reformers.

    Tip Next to a giant chess set.

  5. 05

    Palais des Nations

    The UN's European headquarters, open for guided tours.

    Tip Book and bring your passport.

  6. 06

    International Red Cross and Red Crescent Museum

    A museum about humanitarian action and the movement founded in Geneva.

    Tip Allow two hours.

Things to do

Best things to do in Geneva

Geneva mixes lake time and museums.

  1. Swim at Bains des Pâquis

    A popular public bathing spot, with a sauna and fondue in winter.

    Tip Go in summer.

  2. Take a lake cruise

    Boats run to Lausanne and Montreux.

    Tip Included in the Swiss Travel Pass.

  3. Wander the old town

    Lanes, squares and antique shops.

    Tip Start at Place du Bourg-de-Four.

  4. See watches

    The Patek Philippe Museum.

    Tip Book ahead.

  5. Take a day trip to Chamonix

    Visit Mont Blanc, an hour away in France.

    Tip Bring your passport.

  6. Visit the CERN

    The particle physics laboratory near the city.

    Tip Book tours in advance.

Transport

Getting to and around Geneva

Geneva has an international airport and rail links.

Getting there

  • Train from the airport

    About 7 to 10 minutes to Cornavin station.

    Free public transport ticket at the airport.

  • Train from Lausanne

    About 35 to 45 minutes.

    Frequent.

  • Train from Zurich or Bern

    Under three hours from Zurich and about 1.5 hours from Bern.

    Book with SBB.

Getting around

Trams, buses and boats cover the city.

  • Trams and buses

    Frequent.

    Hotel guests often get a free pass.

  • Yellow taxi boats

    Mouettes cross the harbour.

    Cheap.

  • On foot

    Lakefront and old town.

    Wear comfortable shoes.

When to go

Best time to visit Geneva

Geneva has warm summers and cool, grey winters.

  • Spring (April–May)

    Good

    Blossom and mild weather.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Best time

    Swimming and long evenings.

  • Autumn (September–October)

    Best time

    Clear days.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Quiet

    Grey, with Christmas markets.

Worth planning around: Foggy winter days.

Travel tips

Geneva travel tips

A few tips help you enjoy Geneva.

  • Use the free transport ticket at the airport.
  • Ask your hotel for a transport pass.
  • Book UN tours ahead with your passport.
  • Swim in summer at the Bains des Pâquis.
  • Try fondue in winter.
  • Watch for the Jet d'Eau's schedule.
  • Bring a jacket for the wind.

FAQ

Geneva: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Geneva?

Two days.

Is Geneva expensive?

Yes, one of the most expensive cities.

Is the Jet d'Eau always on?

Usually, but it can stop in strong wind or frost.

What language is spoken?

French.

Can I see Mont Blanc?

On clear days, yes.

Is Geneva good for a day trip to Chamonix?

Yes, about an hour by road or bus.
