Welcome to Zermatt

Zermatt is a village of about 6,000 people at 1,600 metres in the Mattertal valley in Valais, and it is the base for the Matterhorn, a pyramid of rock at 4,478 metres that is one of the best known mountains on earth. First climbed in 1865, in an ascent that ended in tragedy, it still draws climbers, and it is the shape on the Toblerone wrapper.

The village bans petrol cars, and electric taxis and carts move along the main street, which keeps the air clean and the mood calm. Around it are more than 35 lifts and trains, including the Gornergrat cog railway, which climbs to 3,089 metres for a panorama of the Monte Rosa massif and the Gorner Glacier, and the Matterhorn Glacier Paradise cable car, which reaches 3,883 metres, the highest in Europe.

Zermatt is expensive, but the setting is unbeatable. In summer there are walks such as the Five Lakes trail, where each lake mirrors the Matterhorn if the water is calm, and in winter there is skiing, which is possible on glaciers almost all year. It takes about three and a half hours from Zurich or Geneva, and is best with two nights.

Why visit

Why visit Zermatt?

Zermatt gives the best view of Switzerland's icon and a car-free village to enjoy it in.

  • The Matterhorn

    Visible from the village streets.

  • Gornergrat railway

    A cog railway to a 3,089-metre balcony.

  • Hiking and lakes

    The Five Lakes walk and Riffelsee.

  • Car-free calm

    Electric taxis and clean air.

  • Year-round skiing

    Glacier slopes.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Zermatt

The village is the base, and the lifts do the work.

  1. 01

    Gornergrat

    A cog railway climbing to a 3,089-metre viewpoint with a panorama of 29 peaks over 4,000 metres and the Gorner Glacier.

    Tip Take an early train for clear skies.

  2. 02

    Riffelsee

    A small lake at 2,757 metres where the Matterhorn reflects on calm mornings, reached by a short walk from Riffelberg station.

    Tip Go at sunrise if the weather is calm.

  3. 03

    Zermatt village

    A village of wooden chalets, shops and restaurants, with the Matterhorn Museum and the Hinterdorf old quarter.

    Tip Look at the mountain at dusk.

  4. 04

    Gornergrat panorama

    The view from the summit terrace, over glaciers, Monte Rosa and the Matterhorn.

    Tip Take warm clothes.

  5. 05

    Matterhorn Glacier Paradise

    A cable car to 3,883 metres, with an ice palace and views into Italy.

    Tip Go early.

  6. 06

    Matterhorn Museum

    An underground museum about the first ascent and the history of Zermatt.

    Tip Good for cloudy days.

Things to do

Best things to do in Zermatt

Zermatt is about views and walking.

  1. Ride the Gornergrat

    Take the cog railway to the top.

    Tip Buy a return ticket, or walk down.

  2. Walk the Five Lakes trail

    A hike past five mountain lakes.

    Tip Start early.

  3. Hike to Schwarzsee

    A lake and chapel at the foot of the Matterhorn.

    Tip Take the cable car up.

  4. Ski on the glacier

    Year-round runs at high altitude.

    Tip Check the snow report.

  5. Eat fondue in a hut

    Mountain huts serve cheese and rösti.

    Tip Book for sunset.

  6. Visit the Gorner Gorge

    A short walk through a gorge.

    Tip Open in summer.

Transport

Getting to and around Zermatt

Zermatt is reached by train only.

Getting there

  • Train from Visp

    About 1 hour on the Matterhorn Gotthard Railway.

    Reserve for scenic trains.

  • From Zurich or Bern

    About 3.5 hours with a change in Visp.

    Frequent.

  • Drive to Täsch

    Park at Täsch and take the shuttle train.

    Cars cannot enter Zermatt.

Getting around

The village is small and walkable.

  • On foot

    Everything in the village.

    Wear comfortable shoes.

  • Electric taxi

    For luggage and hotels.

    Book.

  • Mountain lifts

    Cable cars and railways.

    Buy a lift pass.

When to go

Best time to visit Zermatt

Zermatt suits summer hikers and winter skiers.

  • Spring (April–May)

    Quiet

    Some lifts closed.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Best time

    Hiking and clear views.

  • Autumn (September–October)

    Best time

    Clear skies and fewer crowds.

  • Winter (December–March)

    Good

    Skiing and snow.

Worth planning around: Cloudy days, since the Matterhorn may hide.

Travel tips

Zermatt travel tips

A few tips help you make the most of Zermatt.

  • Check the Matterhorn webcam.
  • Go early for clear views.
  • Bring layers and sun protection.
  • Book hotels early.
  • Consider staying in Täsch to save money.
  • Buy a lift pass for several days.
  • Take a picnic.

FAQ

Zermatt: frequently asked questions

Can I drive to Zermatt?

No. Park in Täsch and take the train.

How high is the Matterhorn?

4,478 metres.

How many days do you need?

Two or three.

Is Zermatt expensive?

Yes, among the priciest Swiss resorts.

When is the best view?

Early morning, when the sky is clear.

Can I ski in summer?

Yes on the high glacier.
