Welcome to Lausanne and Lavaux

The northern shore of Lake Geneva, in the canton of Vaud, is Switzerland's sunny south-facing riviera. Between Lausanne and Montreux, steep terraces of vines, built by monks in the 12th century, tumble to the water. This landscape, Lavaux, about 30 km long, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and its villages, such as Saint-Saphorin and Cully, produce fine white wines from the chasselas grape.

Lausanne, the region's main city, is built on three hills above the lake. Its Gothic cathedral is one of the finest in Switzerland, the old town is steep and cobbled, and the waterfront district of Ouchy has promenades, parks and paddle steamers. Lausanne is also the Olympic capital, home to the International Olympic Committee and a strong Olympic museum. At the other end of the shore, Montreux, known for its jazz festival and Freddie Mercury statue, has a palm-lined lakeside promenade and, just beyond, the Château de Chillon, a medieval castle on a rock in the water.

The best way to see it is on foot and by train, using the regional trains that run along the lakeshore, and the boats that link the towns. A day can be spent walking between vineyards, tasting wine at family cellars and sipping a glass with lake views. Two days lets you add Lausanne and Chillon.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Lausanne and Lavaux

The sights are along the lakeshore, linked by train and boat.

  1. 01

    Lavaux vineyard terraces

    A 30-km stretch of terraces, with walking paths between Lausanne and Vevey and wine cellars in stone villages.

    Tip Walk from Lutry or Cully to St-Saphorin.

  2. 02

    Château de Chillon

    A medieval island castle on Lake Geneva, once home to the Counts of Savoy, and the subject of Lord Byron's poem The Prisoner of Chillon.

    Tip Go early to avoid tour buses.

  3. 03

    Lausanne Cathedral

    A Gothic cathedral consecrated in 1275, with a tower whose night watchman still calls out the hours.

    Tip Climb the tower for views.

  4. 04

    Ouchy

    Lausanne's waterfront, with paddle steamers, promenades, cafés and the Olympic Museum.

    Tip Take the metro down from the centre.

  5. 05

    Montreux promenade

    A lakeside path lined with palms and flowers, with a statue of Freddie Mercury.

    Tip Walk to Chillon along the lake.

  6. 06

    Olympic Museum

    A museum on the history of the Games, with a park.

    Tip Allow two hours.

When to go

Best time to visit Lausanne and Lavaux

The lakeshore has a mild climate, with warm summers and grape harvests in autumn.

  • Spring (April–May)

    Good

    Vines bud, and blossom.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Good

    Warm, though vineyard walks can be hot.

  • Autumn (September–October)

    Best time

    Grape harvest and golden vines.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Quiet

    Quiet, with Christmas markets in Montreux.

Worth planning around: Midday heat on the terraces in July and August.

Travel tips

Lausanne and Lavaux travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Walk downhill from Chexbres to save effort.
  • Book tastings in advance.
  • Carry water on the terraces.
  • Go early to Chillon.
  • Take the boat one way.
  • Wear good shoes.
  • Visit in September for harvest.
