Welcome to Lugano and Ticino

Ticino is the only Swiss canton that is entirely Italian-speaking, and it feels like a different country from the German-speaking north. South of the Alps, palm trees and camellias grow on the shores of Lake Lugano and Lake Maggiore, the air is warmer, and the pace is slower. Espresso replaces filter coffee, risotto and polenta appear on menus, and the wine is merlot.

Lugano is the main city, a lakeside town of about 63,000 people, with an arcaded old town around Piazza della Riforma, a lakefront promenade and funiculars up to Monte Brè and Monte San Salvatore for views over the lake. Ticino's other centres include Locarno and its neighbour Ascona on Lake Maggiore, famous for a film festival and sunshine, and Bellinzona, with three medieval castles that make up a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Beyond the towns are wild valleys of granite, chestnut woods and stone villages. The Verzasca Valley has turquoise water, and the Ponte dei Salti, a stone bridge near Lavertezzo. Ticino is a summer resort, but it is also lovely in spring, when camellias bloom, and autumn, when the chestnuts fall. Three days lets you feel the region, and it is a short train ride through the Gotthard Base Tunnel from Zurich.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Lugano and Ticino

Lugano is the base, with Locarno and Bellinzona easy trips.

  1. 01

    Lake Lugano and Monte Brè

    A lake ringed by hills, with a funicular up Monte Brè and Monte San Salvatore for views, and a shoreline promenade.

    Tip Take the funicular at sunset.

  2. 02

    Piazza della Riforma

    The main square of Lugano, lined with pastel buildings and cafés.

    Tip Have a coffee here.

  3. 03

    Locarno and Ascona

    Two lakeside towns on Lake Maggiore, with a cable car up to Cardada and a promenade of pastel houses.

    Tip Visit the Madonna del Sasso.

  4. 04

    Bellinzona castles

    Three medieval castles, Castelgrande, Montebello and Sasso Corbaro, protecting a pass, and a UNESCO site.

    Tip Walk the ramparts.

  5. 05

    Verzasca Valley and Ponte dei Salti

    A valley of clear green water and stone villages, with a double-arched stone bridge at Lavertezzo.

    Tip Swim in summer, and take care of the currents.

  6. 06

    Melide and Swissminiatur

    A model Switzerland in miniature, popular with families.

    Tip Fun for children.

When to go

Best time to visit Lugano and Ticino

Ticino is the sunniest part of Switzerland, with mild winters and hot summers.

  • Spring (April–May)

    Best time

    Camellias and mild days.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Good

    Hot, with swimming, but busy.

  • Autumn (September–October)

    Best time

    Golden light and chestnuts.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Quiet

    Mild but quiet.
