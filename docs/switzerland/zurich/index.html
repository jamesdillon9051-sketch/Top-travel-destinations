Welcome to Zurich

Zurich is Switzerland's largest city and financial centre, home to about 440,000 people, and the country's main gateway, with an airport connected to the centre by a 10-minute train. It sits at the northern end of Lake Zurich, split by the Limmat river, and its old town of cobbled lanes, guild halls and church spires is compact enough to walk in an afternoon.

The city is cleaner, greener and more relaxed than its banking reputation suggests. In summer, locals swim in the lake and float down the river, and open-air bathhouses turn into evening bars. Museums include the Kunsthaus, one of Switzerland's best art museums, the Swiss National Museum and a lively design scene in Zurich West. Bahnhofstrasse is one of the world's most expensive shopping streets.

Zurich works best as a two-day stop at the start or end of a trip. It is also a good base for day trips, since Lucerne is 45 minutes away, and Rhine Falls, Winterthur and the mountains of central Switzerland are within an hour or two.

Why visit

Why visit Zurich?

Zurich is an easy, polished city that mixes history, water and culture.

  • A lively old town

    Medieval lanes, church towers and guild houses.

  • Lake and river

    Swimming spots in summer.

  • Museums and art

    The Kunsthaus and design in Zurich West.

  • Food

    Veal, chocolate and modern restaurants.

  • The gateway

    Trains to the whole country.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Zurich

Most sights cluster around the Limmat and the lake.

  1. 01

    The Old Town and Grossmünster

    A Romanesque church with twin towers, where the Reformation in Zurich began under Huldrych Zwingli, with a climbable tower and views over the old town.

    Tip Climb the tower in the morning.

  2. 02

    Lindenhof

    A tree-shaded square on a hill above the Limmat, on the site of a Roman fort, with a chess board and views.

    Tip Good at sunset.

  3. 03

    Lake Zurich and Bürkliplatz

    The lakefront promenade, boats and swimming spots, with the Alps visible on clear days.

    Tip Take a lake boat.

  4. 04

    Bahnhofstrasse

    A tree-lined shopping street from the station to the lake, with chocolate shops, watchmakers and banks.

    Tip Window shopping is free.

  5. 05

    Fraumünster and Chagall windows

    A church with stained glass windows by Marc Chagall.

    Tip Check opening hours.

  6. 06

    Kunsthaus Zürich

    One of Switzerland's leading art museums, with Impressionists and Giacometti.

    Tip Allow two hours.

Things to do

Best things to do in Zurich

Zurich rewards a slow pace and a swim.

  1. Swim in the Limmat or the lake

    Public bathhouses and free swimming spots.

    Tip Go on hot days, from June.

  2. Walk the old town

    Cobbled lanes and guild halls.

    Tip Start at Lindenhof.

  3. Go up Uetliberg

    A local mountain with city views, reached by train.

    Tip Go on a clear day.

  4. Shop for chocolate

    Sprüngli and other makers.

    Tip Try the Luxemburgerli.

  5. Visit the Swiss National Museum

    History in a castle-like building.

    Tip Allow two hours.

  6. Take a day trip to Rhine Falls

    Europe's largest waterfall by volume.

    Tip One hour away.

Local food

What to eat in Zurich

Zurich's food is Swiss classics and modern international restaurants.

  • Zürcher Geschnetzeltes

    Sliced veal in a creamy sauce with rösti.

    Where to try it Traditional restaurants in the old town.

  • Chocolate and Luxemburgerli

    Delicate macaron-like sweets.

    Where to try it Sprüngli on Paradeplatz.

  • Cheese fondue

    A shared pot of melted cheese.

    Where to try it Old-town taverns.

  • Cervelat sausage

    A grilled sausage, Switzerland's national sausage.

    Where to try it Street stalls and parks.

  • Coffee and pastries

    Swiss cafés and bakeries.

    Where to try it Cafés near the lake.

Transport

Getting to and around Zurich

Zurich has Switzerland's largest airport.

Getting there

  • Train from the airport

    About 10 to 15 minutes to Zurich HB, every few minutes.

    The best option.

  • Train from other cities

    Lucerne is 45 minutes, Bern about an hour and Basel under an hour.

    Book with SBB.

  • International trains

    Links to Munich, Milan, Paris and Vienna.

    Book ahead.

Getting around

The city is compact and has trams.

  • Trams and buses

    Frequent and reliable.

    The ZürichCARD covers transport and museums.

  • On foot

    The old town is walkable.

    Wear comfortable shoes.

  • Lake boats

    Ferries on Lake Zurich.

    Included in some passes.

When to go

Best time to visit Zurich

Zurich has warm summers and cold, grey winters.

  • Spring (April–May)

    Good

    Blossom and mild days.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Best time

    Swimming and street festivals.

  • Autumn (September–October)

    Best time

    Clear days and colour.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Quiet

    Christmas markets, but grey and cold.

Worth planning around: Grey November and January.

Itinerary

    The old town

    Churches and lanes.

    • Morning Grossmünster and Lindenhof.
    • Afternoon Fraumünster and Bahnhofstrasse.
    • Evening Dinner by the Limmat.
  2. Day2

    Lake and art

    Water and museums.

    • Afternoon Lake boat and a swim.

Travel tips

Zurich travel tips

A few tips make Zurich easier and cheaper.

  • Take the train from the airport.
  • Consider the ZürichCARD.
  • Drink from the public fountains.
  • Pack for swimming in summer.
  • Book restaurants on weekends.
  • Shops close on Sundays.
  • Buy picnic food at Coop or Migros.

FAQ

Zurich: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Zurich?

Two days. A third allows a day trip.

Is Zurich expensive?

Yes, among the most expensive cities in the world.

Can I swim in the lake?

Yes, at public baths and free spots in summer.

How far is Zurich from Lucerne?

About 45 minutes by train.

Is it worth visiting?

Yes, especially at the start or end of a trip.

What's the airport like?

Efficient, with a fast train to the centre.
