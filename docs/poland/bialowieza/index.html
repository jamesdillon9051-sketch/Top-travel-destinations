Welcome to Białowieża

Białowieża Forest straddles the border between Poland and Belarus, and is one of the last and largest remaining parts of the primeval forest that once covered the European plain. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and its oaks, hornbeams and spruces, some over 400 years old, grow among fallen logs and mossy ground that have been left to rot naturally for centuries.

The forest's flagship animal is the European bison, the wisent, the heaviest land mammal in Europe. It was hunted to extinction in the wild by 1919, and was reintroduced from zoo animals here, and today about 800 live in the forest on the Polish side. Also present are wolves, lynx, elk, red deer, and many rare birds and beetles, and the forest is a prime birdwatching site for woodpeckers and owls.

The strictly protected core of Białowieża National Park can only be visited with a licensed guide, on foot or in a horse-drawn cart, while wider areas can be explored independently. The village of Białowieża has wooden houses, guesthouses and a palace park. It is remote, about four hours from Warsaw, and the best visits are two days in late spring or autumn.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Białowieża

Most visits combine guided walks in the strict reserve with independent trails.

  1. 01

    Białowieża National Park strict reserve

    The oldest, most pristine part of the forest, visited only with a guide, with ancient oaks and dead wood left in place.

    Tip Book a guide in advance.

  2. 02

    European bison

    The wisent, Europe's biggest land animal. Wild sightings are possible but not certain.

    Tip Go early morning or dusk.

  3. 03

    Bison Show Reserve

    An enclosure where you can see bison, elk, wolves and other animals for certain.

    Tip A good fallback.

  4. 04

    Palace Park

    A landscaped park with old trees and a museum, on the site of a former tsarist hunting lodge.

    Tip Free to walk.

  5. 05

    Royal Oaks Route

    A trail among giant oaks.

    Tip Bring a guide.

  6. 06

    Bison Trail (Żubr)

    A hiking route in the forest.

    Tip Wear boots.

When to go

Best time to visit Białowieża

The forest is lovely in spring and autumn, and bison are active at dawn.

    Best time

    Best time

FAQ

Białowieża: frequently asked questions

Can I see bison?

Often but not always. The Show Reserve is a sure option.

Do I need a guide?

Yes for the strict reserve.

How do I get there?

Train or bus via Hajnówka, or drive.

Is it safe near the border?

Yes, but check current advice, since the Belarus border area has restrictions.

How many days?

Two.

When is the best time?

Spring and autumn.
