Welcome to Wrocław

Wrocław is Poland's fourth-largest city, on the Oder river in the south-west, and it has been Bohemian, Austrian, Prussian and German (as Breslau) before becoming Polish after 1945. That layered past shows in its architecture. The Market Square, one of the largest in Poland, is surrounded by pastel gabled houses and a Gothic town hall, and behind it run cobbled streets and canals.

The city is built on islands and riverbanks, with about 100 bridges. Ostrów Tumski, the oldest part, is a quiet island of churches, gas lamps and a cathedral with two tall spires, and a lit-lantern-lighting ceremony each evening. South-east of the centre, the Centennial Hall, a pioneering concrete dome of 1913, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Wrocław is also playful. Hundreds of small bronze gnomes hide around the streets, a legacy of a 1980s anti-communist art protest, and hunting for them is a popular activity. As a university city, it has lively bars, and it was European Capital of Culture in 2016. Two days is enough for the highlights.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Wrocław

Most sights are within a short walk of the Market Square.

  1. 01

    Market Square (Rynek)

    A large square with a Gothic town hall, pastel houses and cafés, and the heart of city life.

    Tip Visit at night.

  2. 02

    Ostrów Tumski

    The oldest part of the city, an island with churches, a cathedral and gas lamps, lit by hand each evening.

    Tip Go at dusk.

  3. 03

    Centennial Hall

    A reinforced concrete dome completed in 1913, then the largest of its kind, and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with a multimedia fountain nearby.

    Tip See the fountain show.

  4. 04

    The gnomes

    Hundreds of tiny bronze gnomes, or krasnale, around the city, in doorways, on pavements and on lampposts.

    Tip Pick up a gnome map.

  5. 05

    Racławice Panorama

    A giant circular painting of a 1794 battle, in a special rotunda.

    Tip Book a slot.

  6. 06

    Wrocław Cathedral tower

    A tower with a city view.

    Tip Take the lift.
