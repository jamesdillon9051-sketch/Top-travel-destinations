Welcome to Kraków

Kraków is Poland's most visited city and its cultural capital, home to about 800,000 people, and it was the seat of the Polish kings for five centuries. Unlike Warsaw, it escaped major destruction in the Second World War, so its medieval old town, ringed by the Planty park where the walls once stood, is intact, and a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1978.

At the centre is the Main Market Square, the largest medieval town square in Europe, with the Renaissance Cloth Hall in the middle and St. Mary's Basilica at one corner, from whose tower a trumpeter plays a call every hour. To the south, Wawel Hill holds the royal castle and cathedral. Further south is Kazimierz, the historic Jewish quarter, now full of restored synagogues, cafés and bars, and across the river is Podgórze, with the Schindler's Factory museum.

Kraków is also the base for two major excursions: the Wieliczka Salt Mine, a UNESCO-listed complex of chambers and chapels carved from salt, and Auschwitz-Birkenau. It has a vibrant student life, and its cellar bars and restaurants are excellent value. Three days covers the city, with more for excursions.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Kraków

The old town is compact and best on foot.

  1. 01

    Main Market Square and the Cloth Hall

    The largest medieval square in Europe, with the Renaissance Cloth Hall trading arcade, the town hall tower and St. Mary's Basilica with its altarpiece by Veit Stoss.

    Tip Listen for the hourly trumpet call.

  2. 02

    Wawel Castle and Cathedral

    The seat of Polish kings for centuries, with a Renaissance courtyard, state rooms and a cathedral where monarchs were crowned and buried.

    Tip Book timed tickets online.

  3. 03

    Kazimierz

    The historic Jewish quarter, with synagogues, a lively square and many bars and restaurants, and a serious past to remember.

    Tip Visit the Old Synagogue.

  4. 04

    Wieliczka Salt Mine

    A UNESCO-listed mine 14 km from the city, worked since the Middle Ages, with chapels and statues carved from rock salt.

    Tip Allow half a day, and dress warmly.

  5. 05

    Schindler's Factory

    A museum about Kraków under Nazi occupation, in Oskar Schindler's former enamel factory.

    Tip Book tickets in advance.

  6. 06

    Planty Park

    A green ring around the old town, on the site of the medieval walls.

    Tip Good for a walk.

    A boiled and baked bread ring.

    Where to try it Street carts.

    A toasted baguette pizza from Plac Nowy.

    Where to try it Plac Nowy in Kazimierz.

    Sour rye soup.

    Kraków-style baked cheesecake.

    Where to try it Cafés.

Travel tips

Kraków travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Book Wawel, Wieliczka and Schindler's Factory in advance.
  • Go to the square early or late.
  • Eat in milk bars.
  • Watch for pickpockets.
  • Use Bolt or Uber.
  • Wear layers in the mine.
  • Visit Kazimierz with respect.
