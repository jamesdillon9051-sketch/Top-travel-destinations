Welcome to Poznań

Poznań is one of Poland's oldest cities, and the place where the Polish state began. In the 10th century, its first rulers built a fortress on the island of Ostrów Tumski, and the country's first cathedral stood here. Today it is a city of about half a million people, a university and trade-fair town midway between Berlin and Warsaw.

The centre is the Old Market Square, a broad, colourful square lined with painted merchant houses and dominated by a Renaissance Town Hall, built in the 16th century. Every day at noon, two mechanical goats emerge from the clock tower and butt heads twelve times, a tradition tied to a legend about a kitchen mishap. The square is full of cafés and bars, and its restaurants serve traditional dishes such as duck with apples.

A short walk east, Ostrów Tumski has the cathedral and the Porta Posnania interactive museum, and on St. Martin's Day, 11 November, the city celebrates with the St. Martin's croissant, a rich pastry filled with poppy seeds. Poznań is small, relaxed, and best on a day or two, often combined with a trip to Gniezno, the first capital.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Poznań

Sights are within walking distance of the Old Market Square.

  1. 01

    Old Market Square

    A large square lined with merchant houses in pastel colours, with cafés, a fountain and the Renaissance Town Hall.

    Tip Sit at a café to watch the show.

  2. 02

    Town Hall and the goats

    The Renaissance Town Hall, which houses a museum, and a clock where two mechanical goats emerge at noon.

    Tip Arrive by 11:55.

  3. 03

    Ostrów Tumski

    The cathedral island where Poland's first rulers built their stronghold, with a Gothic cathedral and a heritage centre.

    Tip Visit the Porta Posnania.

  4. 04

    Imperial Castle

    A neo-Romanesque castle built for Kaiser Wilhelm II in the early 1900s, now a cultural centre.

    Tip Walk the park.

  5. 05

    Croissant Museum

    A show bakery where you can watch St. Martin's croissants being made.

    Tip Book a slot.

  6. 06

    Gniezno Cathedral

    The cathedral of Poland's first capital, an hour from Poznań, with bronze doors from the 12th century.

    Tip A half-day trip.
