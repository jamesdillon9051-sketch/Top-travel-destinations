Welcome to Gdańsk

Gdańsk, on the Baltic coast, was for centuries one of the richest ports in northern Europe, a Hanseatic trading city that shipped grain and timber, and grew wealthy on amber. Its Main Town is a display of tall, narrow merchant houses with sculpted gables, and the lively Long Market at its heart, lined with elaborate facades and topped by a Neptune fountain from the 17th century.

The city is also a place where twentieth-century history was made. The first shots of the Second World War were fired at Westerplatte in 1939, and most of the old town was destroyed in 1945 and then rebuilt with great care. In 1980, workers in the Gdańsk Shipyard formed Solidarity, the independent trade union led by Lech Wałęsa that helped bring down communism, and the European Solidarity Centre tells the story.

Away from history, Gdańsk is a relaxed seaside city, with the amber-shop lanes of Mariacka Street, seafood on the waterfront and beaches along the Bay of Gdańsk. Together with Sopot and Gdynia it forms the Tricity. Two or three days lets you cover the old town, the museums and a day at the beach.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Gdańsk

The Main Town is compact, and the museums are a short walk or tram ride away.

  1. 01

    Long Market and Neptune's Fountain

    The main street of the old town, lined with ornate merchant houses, with the Artus Court and a bronze Neptune in the middle.

    Tip Walk it early or late.

  2. 02

    St. Mary's Church

    One of the largest brick churches in the world, with a tower you can climb for views over the city.

    Tip Climb the 400 steps.

  3. 03

    The Motława waterfront and the Crane

    A medieval wooden port crane, the largest in Europe in its day, beside the river promenade with restaurants and boat trips.

    Tip Go at sunset.

  4. 04

    Museum of the Second World War

    A large, immersive museum on the causes and human cost of the war, in a striking modern building.

    Tip Allow at least three hours.

  5. 05

    Westerplatte

    The peninsula where the first shots of the Second World War were fired, with a memorial.

    Tip Take a boat or bus.

  6. 06

    European Solidarity Centre

    A museum about the Solidarity movement, beside the shipyard gate.

    Tip Allow two hours.
