Welcome to Aït Benhaddou

Aït Benhaddou is a ksar, a fortified village of earthen buildings, on the old caravan route from the Sahara to Marrakech. Its houses, granaries and towers, built from rammed earth and mud brick and decorated with geometric patterns, climb a hillside above a river. UNESCO inscribed it in 1987 as an outstanding example of southern Moroccan earthen architecture. Most of the buildings date from the 17th century, though the site is much older.

Only a handful of families still live in the old village, and most of the residents now live in a newer village on the far bank of the river. But the ksar has found a second life as a film set, and has appeared in productions from Lawrence of Arabia and Gladiator to Game of Thrones.

Nearby Ouarzazate, 30 km to the south-east, has been Morocco's film capital since the 1980s, with large studios open to visitors. It also has the Taourirt Kasbah, and a large solar power complex on its outskirts. The road connecting these places crosses the High Atlas at the Tizi n'Tichka pass and follows the Drâa and Dadès valleys through palm groves and gorges.

Why visit

Why visit Aït Benhaddou?

This is southern Morocco's most photogenic corner, and the gateway to the desert.

  • A perfect earthen village

    Aït Benhaddou shows how mud brick can create a fortress, and is one of the best-preserved ksars in Morocco.

  • Film history

    Studios and film sets in Ouarzazate, and locations used in famous productions.

  • Kasbah country

    Dozens of crumbling and restored kasbahs line the valleys, part of the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs.

  • Dramatic roads

    The Tizi n'Tichka pass and the Dadès and Todra gorges are among the great drives in Africa.

  • A stop on the way

    It is the natural halfway point between Marrakech and the Sahara.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Aït Benhaddou

The ksar is the main attraction, with Ouarzazate and the road offering more.

  1. 01

    The ksar of Aït Benhaddou

    The earthen village on the hillside, reached by a footbridge across the river. A climb to the top gives views over the valley and the palm groves.

    Tip Go early or late, and cross the river at sunset for the best view.

  2. 02

    The village doors and details

    Carved wooden doors and traditional wooden locks, and the decorated towers of the old houses.

    Tip Ask before entering private houses.

  3. 03

    Taourirt Kasbah, Ouarzazate

    A former residence of the Glaoui family, who ruled the region in the early 20th century, with painted rooms and a maze of corridors.

    Tip Guides help explain the rooms.

  4. 04

    The film studios

    Atlas Studios and CLA Studios have hosted major productions, and their sets are open to visitors.

    Tip Check current opening.

  5. 05

    Skoura Oasis

    A palm grove with kasbahs, including the well-known Amridil Kasbah, about 40 km from Ouarzazate.

    Tip Walk or cycle among the palms.

  6. 06

    The Tizi n'Tichka pass

    A high pass, about 2,260 metres, with hairpin bends and views of the Atlas.

    Tip Start early, and check road conditions in winter.

Things to do

Best things to do in Aït Benhaddou

The area is good for photography, slow drives and short walks.

  1. Watch the sunset over the ksar

    The ksar turns golden in the evening, and the far bank gives the best view.

    Tip Go about an hour before sunset.

  2. Visit a film studio

    Walk through the sets of ancient Egypt, Rome and Jerusalem.

    Tip Combine with Taourirt.

  3. Walk in the Skoura palm grove

    Paths lead among date palms and mud kasbahs, with irrigation channels.

    Tip Bring a hat.

  4. Drive the kasbah road

    Continue east to the Dadès Valley and Todra Gorge, with stops at kasbahs and viewpoints.

    Tip Allow a full day.

  5. Stay in a kasbah hotel

    Kasbahs converted to guesthouses offer traditional hospitality.

    Tip Book ahead.

  6. Visit the solar plant

    The Noor Ouarzazate complex is one of the world's largest concentrated solar plants, visible from the road.

    Tip It is best seen from a distance unless a tour is arranged.

Local food

What to eat in Aït Benhaddou

The food is Berber, simple and hearty, based on grains, dates and meat.

  • Tagine

    Lamb, chicken or vegetables cooked in a clay pot.

    Where to try it Restaurants in Aït Benhaddou.

  • Berber omelette

    Eggs cooked in a tagine with tomatoes, onions and spices.

    Where to try it Cafés.

  • Dates and almonds

    Local dates, fresh from the palm oases.

    Where to try it Markets.

  • Couscous

    A Friday staple, with vegetables and meat.

    Where to try it Guesthouses.

  • Mint tea

    The welcome drink, often on rooftop terraces.

    Where to try it Everywhere.

Transport

Getting to and around Aït Benhaddou

Aït Benhaddou is about 180 km from Marrakech, over the Tizi n'Tichka pass.

Getting there

  • Drive from Marrakech

    About 4 hours without stops, and longer in traffic or snow.

    The road is winding, so start early.

  • Organised day trip

    Day tours from Marrakech to Aït Benhaddou are popular but long.

    Staying a night is better.

  • Bus

    CTM and Supratours run to Ouarzazate, and taxis go to Aït Benhaddou.

    Book ahead.

  • Flight to Ouarzazate (OZZ)

    Limited flights from Casablanca and some European cities.

    Check the schedule.

Getting around

A car or driver is the easiest way to move around the area.

  • Driver or hire car

    Best for the kasbah road and the passes.

    Avoid night driving.

  • Taxi

    Grands taxis link Ouarzazate and Aït Benhaddou.

    Agree the fare.

  • On foot

    The ksar and its surroundings are walkable.

    Wear sun protection.

When to go

Best time to visit Aït Benhaddou

The south is hot and dry in summer, and cold at night in winter.

  • Spring (March–May)

    Best time

    Warm days and cool nights, with green oases.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Think twice

    Very hot, often above 40°C.

  • Autumn (September–November)

    Best time

    Warm and pleasant.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Good

    Mild days, cold nights. Snow is possible on the pass.

Worth planning around: July and August.

Travel tips

Aït Benhaddou travel tips

A few things help you make the most of the area.

  • Stay a night rather than visiting on a day trip.
  • Start early to cross the Tizi n'Tichka pass.
  • Cross the river at sunset for the best view of the ksar.
  • Bring water and sun protection.
  • Hire a local guide for the ksar if you want the story.
  • Check winter weather before crossing the pass.

FAQ

Aït Benhaddou: frequently asked questions

Is Aït Benhaddou worth visiting?

Yes. It is one of Morocco's most impressive earthen villages, and it lies on the way to the Sahara.

Can you go inside the ksar?

Yes, on foot by crossing the river. Few families live there, and some houses are shops or museums.

What films were shot here?

Lawrence of Arabia, Gladiator, Kingdom of Heaven and Game of Thrones, among others.

How long does it take to drive from Marrakech?

About four hours over the Tizi n'Tichka pass, longer with stops.

Is it worth staying in Ouarzazate?

It has more facilities, but Aït Benhaddou and Skoura are more atmospheric for a night.

Is the Tizi n'Tichka pass safe?

It is a good road, but winding, and can have snow in winter. Drive carefully in daylight.
