Welcome to Arusha and Mount Meru

Arusha is the starting point for nearly every northern safari, and a lively city in its own right. Built on the slopes of Mount Meru at about 1,400 metres, it has a pleasant climate, a busy market, craft shops, restaurants and a growing cafe scene. Most visitors spend a night before and after safari, but a day or two here is worthwhile.

Mount Meru, a volcano rising to 4,562 metres, is Tanzania's second-highest peak and is often climbed as an acclimatisation hike before Kilimanjaro. The route passes through Arusha National Park, so you walk with an armed ranger among buffalo and giraffes, and on a clear morning the view of Kilimanjaro from the summit is memorable.

Arusha National Park is small but varied, with Ngurdoto Crater, the Momella Lakes with flamingos, colobus monkeys in the forest and canoeing safaris. It is only a short drive from the city, which makes it ideal for a first or last day. Nearby Lake Duluti and the coffee farms on the slopes offer easy half-day outings.

Travel tips

Arusha and Mount Meru travel tips

A few tips for your stay.

  • Stay a night before safaris to rest.
  • Avoid carrying valuables in the city.
  • Use official taxis.
  • Bargain politely in markets.
  • Check national park fees.
  • Carry cash for markets.
  • Book Meru climbs with a ranger.
