Welcome to Ruaha National Park

Ruaha is Tanzania's largest national park and one of the least visited. The landscape is rugged, with rolling bush, baobab-studded hills and the Great Ruaha River, which brings life to the dry country.

The park sits where southern and eastern African ecosystems meet, so it holds antelope that are hard to find in the north, such as greater kudu, sable and roan, along with large prides of lions, leopards, cheetahs, wild dogs and big elephant herds. In the dry season, animals concentrate along the river, giving great viewing from the camps.

There are few camps and fewer vehicles, so the feel is remote and private. Most visitors fly in from Dar es Salaam or the Serengeti circuit. The remoteness and cost make it a destination for repeat safari-goers, but it rewards the effort.

Travel tips

Ruaha National Park travel tips

Remote parks need preparation.

  • Book camps early.
  • Carry malaria precautions.
  • Pack for dust and sun.
  • Bring binoculars.
  • Use soft bags for flights.
  • Respect distance from wildlife.
  • Check camp open dates.
