Welcome to Ngorongoro Crater

Ngorongoro Crater is what remains of a giant volcano that collapsed some two to three million years ago, leaving a caldera about 20 kilometres across with walls rising 600 metres. The floor holds grassland, a soda lake, swamp and forest, and a permanent year-round water supply that keeps a remarkably dense collection of animals in place.

About 25,000 large animals live in the crater, including lions, hyenas, elephants, hippos, buffalo, wildebeest and zebra, and it is one of the best places in Africa to see the endangered black rhino. Lake Magadi hosts flamingos at times. Because the crater walls act as a natural enclosure, game viewing is excellent in any season.

The Ngorongoro Conservation Area is a multi-use area, where Maasai pastoralists still graze their herds on the highlands. The crater rim sits at about 2,300 metres, so nights are cold and misty. Most visitors descend for a half-day game drive from a lodge on the rim, and the area also includes Olduvai Gorge, a key site in the study of early humans, and the Empakaai Crater.

Why visit

Why visit Ngorongoro Crater?

Ngorongoro offers one of the most concentrated wildlife experiences in Africa.

  • A natural amphitheatre

    Animals everywhere in one compact area.

  • Black rhino

    One of the best places to see them.

  • Rim views

    Dramatic landscapes from the lodges.

  • Maasai culture

    Pastoralists share the highlands.

  • Early human history

    Olduvai Gorge nearby.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Ngorongoro Crater

The crater floor is the main draw, with several sights nearby.

  1. 01

    Crater floor game drive

    A half-day drive among lions, hippos, elephants and wildebeest.

    Tip Go early to beat the crowds.

  2. 02

    Lake Magadi

    A soda lake that attracts flamingos at times.

    Tip Binoculars help.

  3. 03

    Lerai Forest

    A fever-tree forest where elephants and leopards sometimes hide.

    Tip Look for large tuskers.

  4. 04

    Olduvai Gorge

    A palaeontological site where early human fossils were found.

    Tip Visit the small museum.

  5. 05

    Empakaai Crater

    A smaller caldera with a flamingo lake, reached on foot.

    Tip Hire a ranger for the hike.

  6. 06

    Crater rim viewpoints

    Sweeping views at sunrise and sunset.

    Tip Dress warmly.

Things to do

Best things to do in Ngorongoro Crater

Visits here are about game drives and short excursions.

  1. Descend at dawn

    Start the game drive at gate opening.

    Tip Take a flask of hot coffee.

  2. Look for black rhino

    Ask your guide to search the grassland.

    Tip Rhinos are often distant, so bring binoculars.

  3. Walk Empakaai

    Hike the rim with a ranger.

    Tip A change from vehicle safaris.

  4. Visit a Maasai boma

    See a traditional homestead.

    Tip Choose community-managed visits.

  5. Enjoy sunset on the rim

    From your lodge terrace.

    Tip Bring layers.

  6. Spot hyenas at their den

    Near the crater hyena sites.

    Tip Best in the morning.

Transport

Getting to and around Ngorongoro Crater

Most visitors arrive by road from Arusha.

Getting there

  • Drive from Arusha

    About 3 to 4 hours to the rim.

    Usually part of a safari circuit.

  • Fly to Lake Manyara or Serengeti

    Then drive on.

    Saves time on longer trips.

  • Safari package

    Operators handle fees and transfers.

    The usual option.

Getting around

Inside the conservation area you will need a 4x4 and a guide.

  • 4x4 safari vehicle

    With a driver-guide.

    Required for the descent.

  • Walking

    Only with a ranger on the rim.

    Not allowed on the floor.

  • Shuttle

    From Karatu to the gate.

    Ask your hotel.

When to go

Best time to visit Ngorongoro Crater

Dry months give the easiest roads and the best visibility, though the crater is good year-round.

  • June-September

    Best time

    Dry, cool and good viewing, but busier.

  • October-December

    Good

    Short rains with greener scenery.

  • January-March

    Good

    Warm, with fewer visitors and many calves.

  • April-May

    Quiet

    Long rains, mist and lowest prices.

Worth planning around: April and May, when mist and mud can limit visibility.

Travel tips

Ngorongoro Crater travel tips

These tips help you plan.

  • Dress in layers, since the rim is cold and misty.
  • Book a lodge on the rim for the views.
  • Descend early, as vehicle numbers are limited by time.
  • Bring binoculars for rhinos.
  • Pack a picnic lunch.
  • Respect Maasai residents.
  • Keep window sills clear and stay in the vehicle.

FAQ

Ngorongoro Crater: frequently asked questions

How long do I need?

A half-day in the crater, plus an overnight.

Can I walk into the crater?

No, vehicles only.

Will I see rhinos?

Often, but at a distance.

Is it worth the fees?

Most say yes for the density of wildlife.

What about the weather?

Cool and misty at altitude.

Can I combine it with the Serengeti?

Yes, it is on the usual route.
