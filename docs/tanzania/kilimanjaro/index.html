Welcome to Mount Kilimanjaro

Kilimanjaro rises from the plains of northern Tanzania to 5,895 metres, the highest point in Africa and the tallest free-standing mountain in the world. It has three volcanic cones: Kibo, Mawenzi and Shira, and Uhuru Peak on the rim of Kibo is the summit most climbers aim for.

It is one of the few seven-summit peaks that needs no technical climbing skills. The trek passes through five ecological zones: farmland, rainforest, heath and moorland, alpine desert and the arctic summit. Several routes lead to the top, with different lengths, difficulty and scenery.

Altitude is the real challenge. Longer routes give your body more time to adapt, and success rates are much higher on seven- to nine-day itineraries. Climbs must be arranged through a licensed operator, with guides, cooks and porters, and the mountain's permit fees are significant. You do not have to reach the top to enjoy the lower slopes, which offer day walks, waterfalls and coffee farm tours.

Why visit

Why visit Mount Kilimanjaro?

Kilimanjaro is a bucket-list adventure that anyone fit and well-prepared can attempt.

  • Africa's highest peak

    Reach 5,895 metres.

  • Five climate zones

    Jungle to ice on one walk.

  • No technical climbing

    A trek, not a mountaineering feat.

  • Multiple routes

    Choose your pace.

  • Rich local culture

    Chagga and Maasai communities.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Mount Kilimanjaro

The mountain and its foothills offer several highlights.

  1. 01

    Uhuru Peak

    The summit on the rim of Kibo.

    Tip Start the final push around midnight.

  2. 02

    Machame Route

    The "Whisky route", scenic and popular.

    Tip Allow seven days.

  3. 03

    Lemosho Route

    Marangu Route

  5. 05

  6. 06

When to go

Best time to visit Mount Kilimanjaro

Two dry seasons give the clearest weather: January to March and June to October.

  • January-March

    Best time

    Dry, warm and clear.

  • June-October

    Best time

    Cooler and dry, the busiest season.

  • April-May

    Think twice

    Long rains, with wet and cloudy conditions.

  • November-December

    Quiet

    Short rains, fewer climbers.

Worth planning around: April, May and November, when rain and cloud are common.

Travel tips

Mount Kilimanjaro travel tips

Planning a climb takes some preparation.

  • Choose a longer route to adapt to altitude.
  • Hire a reputable, licensed operator.
  • Check porter welfare standards.
  • Pack warm layers and good boots.
  • Train with long hikes beforehand.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Tip guides and porters fairly.

FAQ

Mount Kilimanjaro: frequently asked questions

How hard is it?

Demanding because of altitude, not technique.

Which route is best?

Lemosho and Machame are popular, Lemosho has the best odds.

Do I need a guide?

Yes, it is required.

How much does it cost?

Typically US$2,000 and up.

What about altitude sickness?

Climb slowly and tell your guide.

When should I go?

January to March or June to October.
