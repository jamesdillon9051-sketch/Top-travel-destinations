Welcome to Nyerere National Park (Selous)

Nyerere National Park was carved from the northern part of the former Selous Game Reserve, one of the largest protected areas in Africa. The Rufiji River winds through it, feeding lakes, channels and floodplains where hippos, crocodiles and huge numbers of birds gather.

What sets Nyerere apart is the variety of safari styles. In addition to normal game drives, camps offer boat safaris on Lake Tagalala and the Rufiji, and guided walking safaris with armed rangers, which are not allowed in most other Tanzanian parks. The park is one of the best places in the country to see African wild dogs, along with lions, elephants, giraffes and buffalo.

It is easily reached by a short flight from Dar es Salaam, making it a good add-on for a beach holiday on Zanzibar. Most camps are small, and visitor numbers are low, giving a more private experience than the northern circuit.

Travel tips

Nyerere National Park (Selous) travel tips

A few tips for southern safaris.

  • Book camps early, since they are small.
  • Check which camps are open in the rains.
  • Pack light for small aircraft.
  • Bring insect repellent.
  • Wear neutral clothing.
  • Ask about wild dog sightings.
  • Respect ranger instructions.
