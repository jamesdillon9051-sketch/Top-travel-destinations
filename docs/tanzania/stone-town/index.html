Welcome to Stone Town, Zanzibar

Stone Town is the old heart of Zanzibar City, a tangle of narrow alleys, coral-stone houses and bustling bazaars. For centuries it was a trading hub for spices, ivory and, darkly, enslaved people, and its architecture mixes Swahili, Arab, Indian and European styles. The whole district is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The best way to see it is to walk without a plan. Look for the ornate carved wooden doors, studded with brass spikes, and for the balconied merchant houses. The House of Wonders, the Old Fort, the Anglican cathedral on the former slave market and the Sultan's Palace Museum all tell parts of the story, and at dusk the Forodhani Gardens fill with food stalls.

Zanzibar is also famed for its spices, and half-day spice farm tours from town are popular. Stone Town is the main base for catching ferries and flights to other islands, so most visitors spend two nights here before heading to the beaches. Dress modestly, since the population is largely Muslim.

Why visit

Why visit Stone Town, Zanzibar?

Stone Town is one of the most atmospheric historic towns in East Africa.

  • A living old town

    Not a museum, people live and trade here.

  • Carved doors

    Hundreds of artisan examples.

  • Layered history

    Swahili, Omani and colonial.

  • Food and markets

    Seafood, street snacks and spices.

  • Easy base

    Close to the airport and beaches.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Stone Town, Zanzibar

Most sights are within a short walk of each other.

  1. 01

    Carved doors

    Studded wooden doors across the old town.

    Tip Look for Indian and Swahili styles.

  2. 02

    House of Wonders (Beit al-Ajaib)

    A grand waterfront palace turned museum.

    Tip Check opening status before going.

  3. 03

    Old Fort

    A 17th-century Omani fort with an amphitheatre.

    Tip Free to enter.

  4. 04

    Forodhani Gardens

    Waterfront park with sunset food stalls.

    Tip Bargain and check prices.

  5. 05

    Former slave market

    A memorial and cathedral on the site.

    Tip Allow time for the exhibition.

  6. 06

    Darajani Market

    A busy covered market for fish, spices and fruit.

    Tip Go in the morning.

When to go

Best time to visit Stone Town, Zanzibar

Travel tips

Stone Town, Zanzibar travel tips

A few tips for visiting.

  • Dress modestly in the old town.
  • Carry cash for markets.
  • Be cautious with street food freshness.
  • Hire a licensed guide.
  • Avoid walking alone late at night.
  • Respect prayer times.
  • Book rooftop tables ahead.
