Best places to visit in Austria

Imperial Vienna's palaces and coffee houses, Salzburg's baroque old town, and an Alpine landscape of lakes and peaks that made half the country a postcard before photography existed.

1
Capital CityMajor City

Vienna

Vienna

Austria's imperial capital, its Habsburg-era palaces, opera houses and coffee houses genuinely still central to how the city lives today.

Don't miss Schönbrunn Palace's gardens at golden hour, the former imperial summer residence's gravel paths stretching toward a hilltop gloriette.

Salzburg

Mozart's baroque birthplace, its UNESCO-listed old town crowned by a hilltop fortress and surrounded by Sound of Music countryside.

Austria's most photographed village, its pastel houses wedged between a mountainside and a turquoise Alpine lake.

Best places to visit in China

A country so large that its bullet trains cover the distance of Western Europe, with three thousand years of continuous history at one end of the line and neon megacities at the other.

1
Capital CityHistoric Site

Beijing

Beijing Municipality

China's capital for most of the last eight centuries, where a walled Ming palace, tai-chi parks and some of the country's best duck sit inside a modern megacity of 22 million.

Don't miss The Forbidden City — 980 buildings and nearly 9,000 rooms, the seat of Chinese emperors for nearly five hundred years.

Explore Destination → — Beijing
2
Historic SiteArchaeology

Xi'an

Shaanxi

China's ancient capital for eleven dynasties and over a thousand years, where an emperor's buried army meets a walled old city and the best street food in the country.

Don't miss The Terracotta Army — over 8,000 individually sculpted life-size warriors, discovered by farmers digging a well in 1974.

Explore Destination → — Xi'an
3
Major CityCoast Beaches

Shanghai

Shanghai Municipality

China's financial capital and its most international city, where a preserved 1920s waterfront stares across the river at a skyline that is barely three decades old.

Don't miss The Bund at dusk — a row of 1920s European banking houses facing the futuristic towers of Pudong across the Huangpu River.

Explore Destination → — Shanghai
4
Scenic RegionNature Wildlife

Guilin & Yangshuo

Guangxi

China's most photographed landscape: limestone karst peaks by the thousand, a slow river cruise between two towns, and rice-paddy cycling at the quieter end.

Don't miss The Li River cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo — four hours drifting between thousands of forested karst peaks rising straight out of the water.

Explore Destination → — Guilin & Yangshuo
5
National ParkNature Wildlife

Zhangjiajie

Hunan

China's strangest national park: over three thousand sandstone pillars, a glass bridge slung between two cliffs, and the tallest outdoor elevator on earth.

Don't miss The Avatar Hallelujah Mountain — a needle-thin sandstone pillar wreathed in cloud that directly inspired the floating mountains of Avatar.

Explore Destination → — Zhangjiajie
6
Major CityNature Wildlife

Chengdu

Sichuan

Sichuan's capital, where giant pandas, the country's spiciest food and a genuinely unhurried pace of life make the case for slowing an itinerary down.

Don't miss The Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding — dozens of pandas, from newborn cubs to full-grown adults, in naturalistic enclosures best seen at their most active in the early morning.

Explore Destination → — Chengdu
7
Major CityCoast Beaches

Hong Kong

Hong Kong Special Administrative Region

A Special Administrative Region of China with its own laws, currency and border, where dense high-rise city meets green mountains, fishing villages and some of the best dim sum on earth.

Don't miss The Star Ferry crossing Victoria Harbour at dusk — the cheapest, best view in the city, skyscrapers lighting up on both shores at once.

Explore Destination → — Hong Kong
8
Historic SiteNature Wildlife

Lijiang & Yunnan

Yunnan

China's most ethnically diverse province, where a UNESCO-listed canal town, snow-capped mountains and terraced valleys sit far from the country's historic east-coast centres.

Don't miss Lijiang's Old Town at dawn — canal-lined cobbled lanes and Naxi-style wooden houses, genuinely quiet before the day's crowds arrive.

Anhui

The mountain that shaped a thousand years of Chinese landscape painting, with Ming and Qing-dynasty villages preserved almost untouched at its base.

Don't miss Sunrise over Huangshan's sea of clouds — granite peaks breaking through mist below the summit hotels, the single image most associated with classical Chinese landscape painting.

Explore Destination → — Huangshan & the Anhui Villages

Historic SiteScenic Region

Jiangsu & Zhejiang

Two canal-and-lake cities under ninety minutes from Shanghai, one built around the most celebrated classical gardens in China, the other around a lake that has shaped a thousand years of poetry.

Don't miss The Humble Administrator's Garden in Suzhou — the largest and most celebrated classical garden in China, its pavilions, ponds and rockeries composed like a three-dimensional painting.

Best places to visit in France

Alpine summits, Atlantic oyster beds, Roman ruins and the world's most visited city — inside one very well-connected country.

1
Major CityCapital City

Paris

Île-de-France

France's capital packs more world-class museums, monuments and neighbourhood restaurants into six square miles than anywhere else in Europe. It rewards walking more than ticking off a list.

Don't miss The Louvre, the Musée d'Orsay and Sainte-Chapelle — three of Europe's great collections within one Métro ride of each other.

Explore Destination → — Paris
2
Scenic RegionFood & Wine

Provence & the Luberon

Vaucluse, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

A landscape of ochre cliffs, perched stone villages and vineyards between the Rhône and the Alps, with the best-preserved Roman monuments outside Italy on its western edge.

Don't miss The Pont du Gard, a three-tier Roman aqueduct that has stood for two thousand years without mortar.

Explore Destination → — Provence & the Luberon
3
Coast & BeachesMajor City

Nice & the French Riviera

Alpes-Maritimes, Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur

France's fifth city curves around a two-mile bay of blue water, with an old town that was Italian until 1860 and a local rail line linking a dozen coastal villages.

Don't miss The Cours Saleya flower market, then the coastal train to Villefranche, Èze and Menton for €2 a hop.

Explore Destination → — Nice & the French Riviera
4
Historic SiteUNESCO Site

The Loire Valley

Centre-Val de Loire

France's royal playground for two centuries: Renaissance châteaux, formal gardens and crisp white wine strung along 170 miles of the country's longest river, an hour from Paris.

Don't miss Chenonceau, arched on five spans across the River Cher — the most beautiful building in France by most reckonings.

Explore Destination → — The Loire Valley
5
UNESCO SiteHistoric Site

Mont-Saint-Michel

Manche, Normandy

A rock in a vast tidal bay, crowned by an abbey that took five centuries to build. Some three million people visit a year, and almost all of them leave before the best hours.

Don't miss The abbey after the day-trippers leave, when the village empties and the bay goes silent.

Explore Destination → — Mont-Saint-Michel
6
MountainsNature & Wildlife

Chamonix & Mont Blanc

Haute-Savoie, Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes

A valley town beneath a 4,808-metre summit, where glaciers reach almost to the treeline and a network of lifts puts high-alpine terrain within reach of anyone.

Don't miss The Aiguille du Midi: a rock spire with a viewing terrace at 3,842 m, reached by the highest vertical-ascent cable car in the world.

Explore Destination → — Chamonix & Mont Blanc
7
Major CityFood & Wine

Bordeaux

Gironde, Nouvelle-Aquitaine

An 18th-century city of pale limestone facades along a curving river, transformed over two decades from soot-blackened port to one of France's most liveable cities.

Don't miss The Miroir d'Eau opposite the Place de la Bourse: the world's largest reflecting pool, and the best free show in France.

Explore Destination → — Bordeaux
8
Historic SiteCoast & Beaches

Normandy & the D-Day Beaches

Calvados and Manche, Normandy

Five beaches, a dozen museums, cliff-top batteries and immaculately kept cemeteries, in a green farming region that also gave the world Camembert, cider and Calvados.

Don't miss The Normandy American Cemetery above Omaha Beach — 9,388 graves in white marble, looking down on the sand.

Explore Destination → — Normandy & the D-Day Beaches
9
Culture & ArtsUNESCO Site

Strasbourg & Alsace

Bas-Rhin and Haut-Rhin, Grand Est

A region that changed nationality four times in seventy-five years and kept both cultures — French administration, German architecture, and a food and wine tradition that belongs to neither.

Don't miss Strasbourg Cathedral's astronomical clock and the 142-metre spire you can climb for a view into three countries.

Explore Destination → — Strasbourg & Alsace
10
Historic SiteNature & Wildlife

The Dordogne & Sarlat

Dordogne, Nouvelle-Aquitaine

A limestone valley of castles, walnut groves and honey-coloured villages, holding the densest concentration of prehistoric sites in Europe and France's richest regional cooking.

Don't miss Lascaux — the most sophisticated prehistoric art ever found, seen through a replica so exact you forget it is one.

Best places to visit in Germany

A federal country of sixteen states that never fully merged into one culture, from Bavarian beer halls to Hanseatic ports, threaded together by a rail network and a history it faces more directly than almost any other nation.

1
CityHistoric Site

Berlin

Berlin

Germany's capital: a city that confronts its 20th-century history directly, rebuilt into one of Europe's most creative, affordable and genuinely alive capitals.

Don't miss The Brandenburg Gate at dusk, once the literal dividing line of the city, now the backdrop to a Berlin that has spent three decades reunifying in more than just name.

Explore Destination → — Berlin
2
CityCulture

Munich

Bavaria

Bavaria's capital: beer halls, world-class museums, a genuine mountain backdrop and a confident regional identity distinct from the rest of Germany.

Don't miss The Marienplatz's Glockenspiel chiming above the Gothic New Town Hall, with the Bavarian Alps visible on a clear day from the tower above.

Explore Destination → — Munich
3
Historic SiteScenic Region

The Romantic Road & Neuschwanstein

Bavaria

A signposted route through Bavaria's best-preserved medieval towns, ending at Neuschwanstein and the Alpine foothills — Germany's most fairytale corner.

Don't miss Neuschwanstein Castle rising from the forest above Hohenschwangau, the single most photographed building in Germany and the direct inspiration for Sleeping Beauty's castle.

Explore Destination → — The Romantic Road & Neuschwanstein
4
CityCoast & Beaches

Hamburg

Hamburg

Germany's great port city: a Hanseatic trading history, a genuinely working harbour, and a distinct maritime identity in the north.

Don't miss The Elbphilharmonie's glass wave-shaped crown rising above the harbour, a concert hall built on top of a century-old warehouse and now the city's defining new landmark.

Explore Destination → — Hamburg
5
Historic SiteScenic Region

Cologne & the Rhine Valley

North Rhine-Westphalia and Rhineland-Palatinate

Germany's fourth-largest city, its landmark Gothic cathedral, and the UNESCO-listed Rhine Gorge downstream — castles, vineyards and river towns in close succession.

Don't miss Cologne Cathedral's twin spires, the tallest twin-spired church in the world, somehow surviving World War II's bombing of the city around it almost completely intact.

Explore Destination → — Cologne & the Rhine Valley
6
Historic SiteCity

Dresden

Saxony

Saxony's capital: a baroque old town meticulously rebuilt after 1945's devastating bombing, holding some of Germany's finest art collections along the Elbe.

Don't miss The Frauenkirche's rebuilt dome, its facade a genuine patchwork of blackened original stones and pale new ones, standing again after firebombing left it a heap of rubble for over four decades.

Explore Destination → — Dresden
7
Nature WildlifeMountains

The Black Forest

Baden-Württemberg

A dense forested mountain region in Germany's southwest, home to spa towns, cuckoo clocks, Black Forest gateau and some of the country's best hiking.

Don't miss Triberg's waterfall crashing through the forest, Germany's highest, and the small town beneath it that essentially invented the cuckoo clock as a global souvenir industry.

Explore Destination → — The Black Forest
8
Historic SiteCity

Nuremberg

Bavaria

A medieval Franconian city, rebuilt after wartime destruction, carrying both a genuine imperial past and a direct, honestly presented Nazi-era history.

Don't miss The Kaiserburg's fortified silhouette above Nuremberg's rebuilt medieval old town, a skyline that has represented imperial power, propaganda and reckoning within the same century.

Historic SiteRomantic

Heidelberg and the Neckar Valley

A baroque university town on the Neckar river, its old town genuinely undamaged by the war, below a ruined castle and along a valley lined with more castles still.

Don't miss The view from the Philosophers' Walk across the Neckar to Heidelberg Castle and the old town below it, the single most reproduced postcard image of Romantic Germany.

Germany's largest island, its white chalk cliffs and elegant seaside resorts forming a Baltic coastline most visitors never associate with the country.

Don't miss The Königsstuhl chalk cliff dropping sheer into the Baltic's blue-green water, framed by beech forest, one of Germany's most photographed natural views.

The birthplace of Western civilization's founding myths and monuments, spread across a mainland and thousands of islands where ancient ruins, Byzantine monasteries and whitewashed villages sit within a single, well-connected country.

1
Historic SiteCity

Athens

Attica

Greece's capital, its ancient monuments standing directly within a genuinely lively modern city rather than curated apart from it.

2
IslandRomantic

Cyclades

A volcanic caldera island, its whitewashed villages perched along the rim above brilliant blue water, Greece's most photographed island for real reason.

Explore Destination → — Santorini
3

Greece's most famous nightlife island, its genuinely beautiful whitewashed old town and windmills often overshadowed by its reputation for beach clubs and parties.

Explore Destination → — Mykonos
4
IslandHistoric Site

Crete

Crete

Greece's largest island, its scale genuinely rewarding a dedicated trip across Minoan ruins, Venetian harbour towns, mountain gorges and excellent beaches.

Don't miss The Palace of Knossos, the ceremonial heart of Europe's oldest advanced civilization, still standing nearly 4,000 years after it was first built.

Explore Destination → — Crete
5
IslandHistoric Site

Rhodes

Dodecanese

A Dodecanese island pairing one of Europe's best-preserved medieval walled towns with the clifftop Acropolis of Lindos and dependable Aegean beaches.

Don't miss The medieval Old Town of Rhodes, a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of Europe's largest inhabited medieval towns, its walls and knights'-era streets still standing largely intact.

Explore Destination → — Rhodes
6
Historic SiteUNESCO Site

Meteora

Thessaly

Six working monasteries perched atop sheer sandstone pillars in central Greece, a UNESCO World Heritage site unlike anywhere else in the country.

Don't miss The Holy Trinity Monastery, standing alone atop its own isolated rock pillar, reachable only by a steep staircase carved into the stone itself.

Explore Destination → — Meteora
7
Historic SiteUNESCO Site

Delphi

Central Greece

The ancient world's most important oracle site, its ruined sanctuary spread dramatically across a mountainside once believed to mark the centre of the earth.

Don't miss The Temple of Apollo, home to the Oracle of Delphi, whose pronouncements genuinely shaped decisions across the ancient Greek world for centuries.

Explore Destination → — Delphi
8
Historic SiteCoast & Beaches

Peloponnese

A genuinely elegant Venetian port town paired with the Peloponnese's dense concentration of ancient sites, from Mycenae to Epidaurus.

Explore Destination → — Nafplio and the Peloponnese
9
IslandHistoric Site

Corfu

Ionian Islands

A genuinely lush Ionian island shaped by centuries of Venetian rule, its UNESCO-listed old town paired with striking coastline and green scenery.

Don't miss Corfu's Old Town, a genuinely well-preserved Venetian fortress town and UNESCO World Heritage Site, its architecture unlike anywhere else in Greece.

Explore Destination → — Corfu
10
CityHistoric Site

Thessaloniki

Central Macedonia

Greece's genuinely underrated second city, a Byzantine and Ottoman crossroads with a food and nightlife scene many Greeks rate above Athens's own.

Don't miss The White Tower on Thessaloniki's waterfront, the city's defining Byzantine and Ottoman-era landmark and the anchor of its genuinely lively seafront promenade.

Best places to visit in Iceland

A volcanic island where glaciers, waterfalls, geysers and black sand beaches sit within a single day's drive of a capital city, under skies that barely darken in summer or brighten in winter.

Capital CityMajor City

Reykjavik

Capital Region

Iceland's small, walkable capital, its colourful rooftops, geothermal pools and harbourfront concert hall anchoring almost every trip to the country.

Explore Destination → — Reykjavik

Southern Iceland

Iceland's most-visited route, looping past a UNESCO rift valley, an erupting geyser and a thundering waterfall within a single day from Reykjavik.

Don't miss Watching Strokkur's water dome swell for a few seconds before erupting in a sudden column of steam and spray, metres from where you're standing.

Explore Destination → — The Golden Circle
3

Iceland's single most photographed stretch of coast, its waterfalls, black sand beaches and glaciers strung along one accessible road from Reykjavik.

Explore Destination → — The South Coast
4

Explore Destination → — Jökulsárlón and Vatnajökull
5
Nature & WildlifeCoast & Beaches

Western Iceland

A compact peninsula holding glaciers, lava fields, black churches and dramatic coastline within a single day's loop, often called 'Iceland in miniature.'

Don't miss Kirkjufell's near-perfect cone reflected in its own waterfall's pool, genuinely one of the most photographed views in Iceland.

Explore Destination → — Snæfellsnes Peninsula
6

A world-famous geothermal spa on Iceland's volcanic front doorstep, surrounded by lava fields, hot springs and a lighthouse at the edge of the sea.

Don't miss Sinking shoulder-deep into the Blue Lagoon's warm, milky-blue water with a silica mask on, steam rising into cold air around you.

Explore Destination → — Blue Lagoon & the Reykjanes Peninsula
7

North Iceland

A geothermal lake region in North Iceland, ringed by lava formations, mudpots and waterfalls, with Akureyri and Húsavík's whale watching close by.

8

Northwest Iceland

Iceland's remote northwest arm, a maze of fjords with towering bird cliffs, a red sand beach and some of the country's least-visited scenery.

9

Iceland's remote volcanic highlands, home to a natural hot spring, rainbow-coloured rhyolite mountains and the multi-day Laugavegur trek.

Don't miss Soaking in Landmannalaugar's naturally warm stream as the low sun turns the surrounding rhyolite mountains shades of orange, pink and green.

Explore Destination → — Landmannalaugar & the Highlands
10

Vestmannaeyjar, South Iceland

A dramatic volcanic archipelago off Iceland's south coast, home to a 1973 eruption's frozen aftermath and vast puffin colonies.

Don't miss Standing on the rim of Eldfell, a volcano that didn't exist before 1973, looking down over the town it nearly buried.

Explore Destination → — The Westman Islands
