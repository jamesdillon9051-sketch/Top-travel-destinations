Welcome to Anuradhapura

Anuradhapura was the capital of the first Sinhalese kingdom from the 4th century BCE, and for more than a thousand years a centre of Theravada Buddhism and of civilisation in the island. At its height, it was one of the largest cities in South Asia, with monasteries housing thousands of monks, giant reservoirs and palaces. Abandoned in the 11th century, it was rediscovered in the 19th, and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Unlike many ruins, Anuradhapura is still a place of living worship. The Sri Maha Bodhi, a sacred fig tree grown from a cutting of the tree under which the Buddha attained enlightenment, is said to be the oldest historically documented tree in the world, planted in 288 BCE. Pilgrims in white bring flowers and lamps, and the giant white stupas, huge domed shrines, still dominate the skyline. The Ruwanwelisaya, from the 2nd century BCE, is the most revered.

The site is spread over several square kilometres, so bicycles or tuk-tuks are the best way to see it. Other highlights include the massive Jetavanaramaya, once the tallest brick structure in the world, the Abhayagiri monastery, the Twin Ponds and Isurumuniya Rock Temple. A day and a half is a good visit, combined with Sigiriya and Polonnaruwa.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Anuradhapura

The sights are spread over a large area.

  1. 01

    Ruwanwelisaya

    A gleaming white stupa about 100 metres tall, built by King Dutugemunu in the 2nd century BCE.

    Tip Go at dawn or dusk.

  2. 02

    Sri Maha Bodhi

    The sacred fig tree grown from a cutting of the original Bodhi tree, planted in 288 BCE.

    Tip Dress in white if possible.

  3. 03

    Jetavanaramaya

    A huge brick stupa, once one of the tallest structures in the ancient world.

    Tip Cycle to it.

  4. 04

    Abhayagiri Monastery

    A monastery complex with a stupa, moonstones and ponds.

    Tip Allow an hour.

  5. 05

    Isurumuniya

    A rock temple with carvings of lovers and horses.

    Tip Visit in the evening.

  6. 06

    Kuttam Pokuna (Twin Ponds)

    Ancient bathing pools.

    Tip Photogenic.
