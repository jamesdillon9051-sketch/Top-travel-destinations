Welcome to Kandy

Kandy is the cultural capital of Sri Lanka's hill country, set around a small artificial lake among green hills at about 500 metres. It was the last capital of the Sinhalese kings, who held out against the Portuguese, Dutch and British until 1815, and its old core is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Its heart is the Sri Dalada Maligawa, the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, said to house a tooth of the Buddha, brought to Sri Lanka in the 4th century. Pilgrims come daily to offer lotus flowers, and rituals called pujas take place three times a day with drumming. In July or August, the Esala Perahera, a grand procession of elephants, dancers and drummers, honours the relic for ten nights.

Kandy also has the Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya, with orchids and giant trees, nightly Kandyan dance shows, and lakeside walks. It is a hub for the hill country, and the start of the train to Nuwara Eliya and Ella. Two days is enough, and Kandy is busy and traffic-heavy, so a hill-side hotel gives calm.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Kandy

Sights are close to the lake.

  1. 01

    Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

    The palace-temple complex holding the tooth relic in a golden casket, visited by pilgrims and tourists.

    Tip Go for the evening puja.

  2. 02

    Kandy Lake

    An artificial lake built by the last king, with a walking path.

    Tip Walk at dusk.

  3. 03

    Kandyan dance show

    A nightly show of drumming, acrobatics and fire-walking, in the Kandyan tradition.

    Tip Book seats.

  4. 04

    Peradeniya Royal Botanical Gardens

    A 147-acre garden with orchids, palms and giant trees, 6 km from Kandy.

    Tip Allow half a day.

  5. 05

    Bahirawakanda Buddha

    A giant white Buddha on a hill.

    Tip For city views.

  6. 06

    Gadaladeniya Temple

    A 14th-century temple outside town.

    Tip Combine with a tour.

Travel tips

Kandy travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Dress modestly at the temple.
  • Book Perahera seats early.
  • Go to the puja for the drums.
  • Use tuk-tuk apps.
  • Bring a raincoat.
  • Take cash.
  • Stay uphill for calm.
