Welcome to Colombo

Colombo is Sri Lanka's largest city and commercial capital, home to about 750,000 people in the city proper and more than 5 million in its metropolitan area. It has been a trading port for over a thousand years, visited by Arab, Chinese, Portuguese, Dutch and British merchants, and its layered history shows in its streets. Most visitors arrive here, and many treat it as a place to pass through, but it is worth a day.

The seafront Galle Face Green is the city's living room, a long lawn where families fly kites and vendors sell street food at sunset. In the historic Fort district, colonial buildings house shops and hotels, and Pettah, the bustling market district, is a chaotic maze of stalls selling everything. The Gangaramaya Temple is a colourful, eclectic Buddhist temple and museum, and the Jami Ul-Alfar Mosque, a red-and-white candy-striped building, is among the most photogenic.

In recent years Colombo has grown more cosmopolitan, with the Lotus Tower, Asia's tallest, rising above the skyline, and a thriving restaurant and café scene in Colombo 7 and Colombo 3. The city is hot, humid and congested, so start early and rest at midday. One or two days is enough.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Colombo

The sights are spread around the city.

  1. 01

    Galle Face Green

    A 500-metre seafront lawn built by the British, popular at sunset for kites, walks and snacks like isso wade.

    Tip Go at 5:30 pm.

  2. 02

    Colombo skyline and the Lotus Tower

    The Lotus Tower is a 350-metre communications tower with an observation deck.

    Tip Go at sunset.

  3. 03

    Pettah market

    A tangle of streets with stalls of clothing, spices, hardware and fruit.

    Tip Go in the morning.

  4. 04

    Gangaramaya Temple

    A temple complex with a museum of gifts, statues and an eclectic collection of objects.

    Tip Dress modestly.

  5. 05

    Jami Ul-Alfar Mosque

    A red-and-white striped mosque in Pettah.

    Tip Photograph from outside.

  6. 06

    National Museum

    The country's main museum, with royal regalia.

    Tip Allow two hours.
