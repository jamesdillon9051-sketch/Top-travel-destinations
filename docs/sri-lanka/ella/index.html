Welcome to Ella

Ella is a small village in Sri Lanka's south-east hill country, at about 1,000 metres, perched above a gap in the hills through which you can see all the way to the coast on clear days. Once a sleepy tea-country stop, it is now the island's main backpacker hub, with guesthouses, cafés and bars along a single main street.

Its attractions are natural. The Nine Arch Bridge, built in the 1920s from stone and brick without steel, curves through jungle and tea bushes, and trains cross it a few times a day. Little Adam's Peak is an easy 30-minute climb with panoramic views, Ella Rock is a longer and more demanding hike, and Ravana Falls is a broad waterfall beside the road. Nearby, tea factories such as Uva Halpewatte offer tours.

Most visitors arrive by train from Kandy or Nuwara Eliya, about 6 to 7 hours through tea country, a journey often listed as one of the world's best. Weather is cooler and can be foggy or rainy, and mornings are usually clearest. Two days is a good stay, with time for hikes and cafés.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Ella

Sights are around the village, mostly walkable.

  1. 01

    Nine Arch Bridge

    A stone viaduct with nine arches, in a jungle setting, where trains cross a few times a day.

    Tip Check the train timetable.

  2. 02

    Little Adam's Peak

    An easy hill to climb, giving views of Ella Gap and Ella Rock.

    Tip Go at sunrise.

  3. 03

    Ella Rock

    A tougher hike of 3 to 4 hours through tea estates.

    Tip Hire a guide.

  4. 04

    Ravana Falls

    A broad waterfall beside the main road.

    Tip Best after rain.

  5. 05

    Tea factory

    Estates offering tours and tastings.

    Tip Go in the morning.

  6. 06

    The Ella train station

    A scenic station, where the famous hill-country line ends.

    Tip Book seats in advance.

Travel tips

Ella travel tips
