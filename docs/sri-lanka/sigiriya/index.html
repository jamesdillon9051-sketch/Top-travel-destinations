Welcome to Sigiriya

Sigiriya, or Lion Rock, is a 200-metre granite column rising from the jungle plain in central Sri Lanka, and the site of one of Asia's most extraordinary ancient cities. In the 5th century CE, King Kashyapa, who had seized the throne by killing his father, built a palace on its flat summit and a garden city at its foot. After his defeat, the site became a Buddhist monastery, and was abandoned around the 14th century. It is now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The climb takes about an hour, up staircases past the remains of the Lion Gate, where two huge stone paws survive, and the Mirror Wall, once so polished that the king could see himself in it. On the way, a sheltered gallery holds frescoes of celestial maidens, painted 1,500 years ago. At the summit, the ruins of the palace and a pool give views across the forests. The symmetrical water gardens at the foot are among the oldest surviving landscape gardens in Asia.

Nearby Pidurangala Rock offers a less crowded view of Sigiriya itself at sunrise, and Dambulla, 20 km away, has a cave temple with more than 150 Buddha statues and painted ceilings. Entry to Sigiriya is expensive for foreigners, and the site can be very hot and busy, so go early.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Sigiriya

Sigiriya, Pidurangala and Dambulla make a good two-day set.

  1. 01

    Sigiriya Rock

    The 200-metre rock with the ruined palace, Lion Gate, Mirror Wall and frescoes, climbed by about 1,200 steps.

    Tip Go at 7 am opening time.

  2. 02

    The Sigiriya frescoes

    Paintings of women in a sheltered gallery, thought to depict apsaras or the king's consorts.

    Tip Photography is not allowed.

  3. 03

    Dambulla Cave Temple

    Five caves with 153 Buddha statues and painted ceilings dating back to the 1st century BCE.

    Tip Remove shoes and cover shoulders.

  4. 04

    Inside the caves

    Cave interiors painted with scenes from the Buddha's life and previous births.

    Tip Climb early to avoid crowds.

  5. 05

    Pidurangala Rock

    A smaller rock near Sigiriya with a reclining Buddha and a famous sunrise view of Sigiriya.

    Tip Take a torch for a pre-dawn climb.

  6. 06

    Sigiriya water gardens

    Symmetrical pools, fountains and terraces at the base of the rock.

    Tip See them first.

Travel tips

Sigiriya travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Go at opening time.
  • Bring water and sun protection.
  • Wear modest clothes for Dambulla.
  • Keep away from wasps on the rock.
  • Buy tickets at the official counter.
  • Take cash.
  • Do not photograph frescoes.
