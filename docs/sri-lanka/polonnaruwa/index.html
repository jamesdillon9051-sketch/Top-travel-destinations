Welcome to Polonnaruwa

Polonnaruwa succeeded Anuradhapura as the capital of Sri Lanka in the 11th century, first under the Chola invaders and then under the great Sinhalese kings Vijayabahu I and Parakramabahu I. In the 12th century, Parakramabahu created a magnificent city and the vast Parakrama Samudra, the Sea of Parakrama, a giant reservoir that irrigated the surrounding land. The city was abandoned in the 13th century, and its ruins are now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Compared with Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa is compact and easier to explore. The Quadrangle is a raised terrace with the Vatadage, a circular relic house with carved moonstones and Buddha statues, and other shrines. The Royal Palace once had seven storeys. And the Gal Vihara, a group of four Buddha images carved from a single granite rock face, including a 14-metre reclining Buddha, is considered the finest example of Sinhalese rock carving.

The best way to see the site is by bicycle, early in the morning. Just outside, Minneriya and Kaudulla national parks host 'The Gathering', when hundreds of wild elephants gather at the reservoir in the dry season, from July to October. A day at Polonnaruwa, combined with an afternoon safari, is an excellent day.

Why visit

Why visit Polonnaruwa?

  • Gal Vihara

    Four colossal Buddhas.

  • The Vatadage

Main attractions

Top things to see in Polonnaruwa

The main sites are within a few kilometres.

  1. 01

    Gal Vihara

    A rock temple with four Buddha statues carved from granite in the 12th century, including a reclining Buddha and a standing one.

    Tip Visit early or late.

  2. 02

    The Quadrangle and Vatadage

    A raised terrace with the circular Vatadage, the Hatadage and Atadage shrines.

    Tip Look at the moonstones.

  3. 03

    Minneriya elephants

    A seasonal gathering of wild elephants on the reservoir shores from July to October.

    Tip Book an afternoon jeep.

  4. 04

    Royal Palace

    The ruined palace of Parakramabahu with thick walls.

    Tip Imagine the seven storeys.

  5. 05

    Parakrama Samudra

    The huge reservoir, with sunset views.

    Tip Go at sunset.

  6. 06

    Lankatilaka

    A giant image house with a headless Buddha.

    Tip Impressive walls.
