Welcome to Lake Balaton

Lake Balaton is the largest lake in Central Europe, about 77 km long and 14 km across at its widest, but only around 3 metres deep on average, so its water warms quickly in summer. It has been Hungary's favourite holiday destination for over a century, called the 'Hungarian sea', and every summer the shores fill with families, sailors and swimmers.

The two shores are different. The south shore is flat, with sandy beaches and lively resort towns such as Siófok, known for parties. The north shore is hilly and more attractive, with volcanic hills around Badacsony, vineyards, lavender fields, and historic towns. The Tihany Peninsula has a Benedictine abbey from the 11th century and views of the lake, and Balatonfüred, an elegant spa town, has a shady promenade named after the poet Rabindranath Tagore.

At the western end, Keszthely has the grand baroque Festetics Palace, and nearby Hévíz has the largest thermal lake in Europe. The region is also a wine country, producing crisp whites like Olaszrizling and Kéknyelű. Two or three days on the north shore gives the best taste, ideally in June or September.

Why visit

Why visit Lake Balaton?

Balaton is Hungary's summer playground, with a scenic north shore and excellent wines.

  • A huge warm lake

    Shallow and good for swimming.

  • Tihany Abbey

    A landmark peninsula.

  • Volcanic wine hills

    Badacsony vineyards.

  • Palaces and spas

    Keszthely and Hévíz.

  • Cycling

    A path around the lake.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Lake Balaton

The north shore has the best sights.

  1. 01

    Tihany Abbey and Peninsula

    A baroque abbey church on a hill, above a village of thatched cottages, with views over the lake and lavender fields in early summer.

    Tip Go in late June for lavender.

  2. 02

    Badacsony

    A basalt hill with terraced vineyards, hiking trails and wine cellars, and beautiful views of the lake.

    Tip Climb it in the morning.

  3. 03

    Balatonfüred and the Tagore Promenade

    An elegant spa town with a tree-lined waterfront promenade, a heart hospital and a sailing harbour.

    Tip Stroll at sunset.

  4. 04

    Festetics Palace, Keszthely

    A grand baroque palace with a library of 86,000 volumes, formal gardens and a museum.

    Tip Allow two hours.

  5. 05

    Hévíz thermal lake

    Europe's largest biologically active thermal lake, warm enough to swim in year-round.

    Tip Bring water shoes.

  6. 06

    Siófok

    The party resort town on the south shore.

    Tip Go for the beaches and nightlife.

    Volcanic whites.

    Thermal lake.

When to go

Best time to visit Lake Balaton

Balaton peaks in July and August, though these are the busiest months.

  • Spring (April–May)

    Quiet

    Quiet, with some facilities closed.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Best time

    Swimming and festivals.

  • Autumn (September–October)

    Best time

    Harvest and calm.

  • Winter (November–March)

    Quiet

    Cold, with the lake sometimes freezing.

Worth planning around: Weekends in peak July and August.

Travel tips

Lake Balaton travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Visit in June or September.
  • Book accommodation early for July.
  • Bring water shoes.
  • Rent a bike.
  • Try the fish.
  • Avoid Siófok if you want quiet.
  • Check the ferry times.
