Welcome to Pécs

Pécs is a city of about 140,000 people at the foot of the Mecsek hills in southern Hungary, and it has one of the mildest climates in the country. It has been settled since Roman times, when it was called Sopianae, and its layers of history are unusually visible: Roman-era Christian tombs, a medieval cathedral, an Ottoman mosque, and Art Nouveau ceramics from the Zsolnay factory.

The most famous building is the Mosque-Church of Pasha Gazi Kasim on Széchenyi Square, built in the 16th century during the Ottoman occupation and later converted into a Catholic church, with Islamic prayer niche and Christian symbols side by side. Nearby, the four-towered cathedral is a neo-Romanesque giant, and the Early Christian Necropolis, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, contains painted burial chambers from the 4th century.

The Zsolnay Cultural Quarter celebrates the local porcelain, with its shimmering eosin-glazed tiles visible on rooftops across the city. Pécs was European Capital of Culture in 2010, and has good cafés, museums and a warm, unhurried feel. To the south, the Villány wine region produces some of Hungary's best reds. Two days is ideal.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Pécs

Sights are close together in the compact centre.

  1. 01

    Mosque-Church of Gazi Kasim Pasha

    A 16th-century Ottoman mosque, now a Catholic church, with Islamic architecture and Christian symbols.

    Tip Look inside.

  2. 02

    Pécs Cathedral

    A grand cathedral with four towers and a dazzling interior, largely rebuilt in the 19th century.

    Tip Visit the square.

  3. 03

    Early Christian Necropolis

    UNESCO-listed 4th-century burial chambers, with wall paintings.

    Tip Book a guided visit.

  4. 04

    Zsolnay Cultural Quarter

    The former porcelain factory, now museums and cafés.

    Tip Look at the tiles.

  5. 05

    Széchenyi Square

    The main pedestrian square, with cafés and fountains.

    Tip Good for people-watching.

  6. 06

    Mecsek hills

    Forests and viewpoints above the city.

    Tip Take a bus.
