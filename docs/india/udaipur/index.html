Welcome to Udaipur

Udaipur was founded in 1559 by Maharana Udai Singh II of the Mewar dynasty, after the capital at Chittorgarh had been repeatedly besieged by Mughal armies. The Mewar rulers, who had resisted the Mughals for generations, chose a site in a valley of the Aravalli hills beside a large artificial lake, Lake Pichola, and built a series of palaces and gardens in the following centuries.

The centrepiece is the City Palace, a huge complex of courtyards, balconies and domes rising directly from the lake and still partly occupied by the royal family. Across the water, the Lake Palace, built as a summer palace in the 18th century and now a luxury hotel, and Jag Mandir, another island palace, appear to float on the surface. The old town of white havelis and lanes runs down to the shore.

Udaipur is popular for weddings and honeymoons, and it can be busy, but it is also one of the most relaxed cities in Rajasthan, with a good food scene, craft shops and cooking classes. It is a fine place to slow down after the intensity of Delhi and Jaipur.

Why visit

Why visit Udaipur?

Udaipur combines scenery, palaces and atmosphere in a way that few Indian cities can.

  • The lake setting

    Lake Pichola, hills and palaces make it the most picturesque city in Rajasthan.

  • The City Palace

    One of the largest palace complexes in Rajasthan, with museums, painted halls and terraces over the water.

  • A relaxed old town

    Winding lanes, rooftop cafés, shops and workshops, easy to explore on foot.

  • Culture and crafts

    Miniature painting, puppet shows, folk dance and silverware.

  • Day trips

    The Jain temples of Ranakpur and the great fort at Kumbhalgarh are within reach.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Udaipur

Most sights are around Lake Pichola, with hilltop views and a couple of gardens.

  1. 01

    The City Palace

    A sprawling palace of granite and marble begun in 1559 and added to for nearly 400 years, with courtyards, mirrored halls, painted rooms and balconies with views over the lake. Part is a museum.

    Tip Go in the morning and hire a guide.

  2. 02

    Lake Pichola and the Lake Palace

    A large artificial lake with two island palaces. Jag Niwas, or the Lake Palace, is now a luxury hotel, and cannot be visited unless you book a meal or a stay.

    Tip Take a boat trip at sunset.

  3. 03

    Jag Mandir

    A palace on a second island, said to have given shelter to the future Mughal emperor Shah Jahan, and now used for events and dining. It can be reached by boat.

    Tip Check whether boats are landing when you visit.

  4. 04

    Jagdish Temple

    A large Hindu temple, dedicated to Vishnu and built in the mid-17th century, with elaborately carved pillars and a steep flight of steps from the street.

    Tip Remove your shoes and dress modestly.

  5. 05

    Saheliyon ki Bari

    The Garden of the Maids of Honour, a fountain garden of pools, lotus ponds and marble kiosks, built for the women of the royal household.

    Tip Best in the morning.

  6. 06

    Sajjangarh (Monsoon Palace)

    A hilltop palace with views over the lakes, particularly at sunset.

    Tip Take a taxi.

Things to do

Best things to do in Udaipur

Udaipur is for slow days, evenings by the water and food.

  1. Cruise Lake Pichola at sunset

    A boat trip gives views of the City Palace, Jag Mandir and the Lake Palace in the golden light.

    Tip Book the sunset slot.

  2. Take a cooking class

    Learn to make Rajasthani dishes and chai with a local family.

    Tip Book through your guesthouse.

  3. See a folk dance and puppet show

    Evening shows at Bagore Ki Haveli feature music, dance and puppetry.

    Tip Buy tickets in advance.

  4. Visit Ranakpur and Kumbhalgarh

    A day trip to the marble Jain temples of Ranakpur, with more than 1,400 carved pillars, and the huge 15th-century fort of Kumbhalgarh with its long wall.

    Tip Hire a driver for the day.

  5. Shop for miniatures and silver

    Workshops around the City Palace sell miniature paintings and silver jewellery.

    Tip Check the quality and the price.

  6. Have dinner on a rooftop

    The old town has many restaurants with terraces facing the palace.

    Tip Book a table for sunset.

Local food

What to eat in Udaipur

Mewari food is similar to that of Rajasthan, with local variations, and Udaipur has a good range of restaurants.

  • Dal baati churma

    Baked wheat balls with lentils and a sweet crumbled wheat dessert.

    Where to try it Traditional restaurants.

  • Laal maas

    A spicy red mutton curry.

    Where to try it Rajasthani restaurants.

  • Gatte ki sabzi

    Gram-flour dumplings in a spiced yoghurt gravy, popular with vegetarians.

    Where to try it Family restaurants.

  • Mirchi vada

    A large green chilli stuffed with potato and fried in batter.

    Where to try it Street stalls.

  • Masala chai and lassi

    Sweet, milky tea and cool yoghurt drinks.

    Where to try it Rooftop cafés.

Accommodation

Where to stay in Udaipur

Udaipur has many small heritage hotels facing the lake, and prices vary widely.

  • Old city near Lake Pichola

    Havelis and guesthouses with rooftop terraces overlooking the lake.

    Best for: Views and walking

  • Around Fateh Sagar

    Hotels by another lake, with more modern facilities.

    Best for: Quiet and space

  • Palace hotels

    Luxury heritage properties on and around the lake.

    Best for: A special-occasion stay

Transport

Getting to and around Udaipur

Udaipur is in southern Rajasthan, about 400 km from Jaipur.

Getting there

  • Flight

    Direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur and other cities.

    The airport is 40 minutes from the centre.

  • Train

    Overnight trains from Delhi and Jaipur take about 8 to 12 hours.

    Udaipur City station is near the old town.

  • Road

    About 6 to 7 hours from Jaipur, or about 3 hours from Ranakpur.

    A driver is comfortable but long.

Getting around

The old town is walkable, and rickshaws and cabs cover the rest.

  • Walking

    The lake front and the old town are on foot.

    Watch for scooters in narrow lanes.

  • Auto-rickshaws

    Cheap for short trips.

    Agree the fare.

  • Taxi or driver

    Best for day trips to Ranakpur and Kumbhalgarh.

    Book through your hotel.

When to go

Best time to visit Udaipur

Udaipur is cooler than the rest of Rajasthan, but summers are hot, and lake levels are best after a good monsoon.

  • Winter (November–February)

    Best time

    Mild and sunny. Peak season, with weddings.

  • Spring (March–April)

    Good

    Warm, and lakes are lower.

  • Summer (May–June)

    Think twice

    Very hot.

  • Monsoon (July–September)

    Good

    Green hills and full lakes, humid, and fewer visitors.

Worth planning around: May and June. Wedding season in winter can make hotels expensive.

Travel tips

Udaipur travel tips

Udaipur is easy, and a few tips help.

  • Book boat trips for the sunset slot.
  • Choose a hotel with a lake view or a rooftop.
  • Stay three nights if you can. It is a place people want to linger.
  • Bargain in the shops, and compare prices.
  • Be wary of drivers who insist on commission stops.
  • Book weddings-season hotels early.

FAQ

Udaipur: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Udaipur?

Two days for the main sights, and three if you want a day trip to Ranakpur and Kumbhalgarh.

Can I visit the Lake Palace?

Generally only as a guest or with a reservation for a meal or drink. It is not an open tourist attraction.

Is Udaipur better than Jaipur?

They are different. Udaipur is more romantic and relaxed, while Jaipur is larger, busier and has more shopping.

What is the best time to visit?

October to March. The lakes are full after the monsoon.

Is Udaipur safe?

Generally yes. The main problems are touts and overcharging.

How do I get from Jaipur to Udaipur?

By flight, overnight train or a long drive of about 6 to 7 hours.
