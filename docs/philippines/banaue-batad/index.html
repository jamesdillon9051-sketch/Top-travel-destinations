Welcome to Banaue and Batad

In the Cordillera Mountains of northern Luzon, the Ifugao people have shaped whole mountainsides into stepped rice paddies, held by stone or mud walls, watered by a network of channels from the forest above. Built over about 2,000 years, and still farmed by hand, the Ifugao Rice Terraces are a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and often called the eighth wonder of the world, although the phrase is a promotional one.

Banaue is the gateway town, with terrace views from the town viewpoints, an Ifugao museum and guesthouses. The most spectacular terraces are at Batad, an hour and a half by jeepney and then a 30-minute walk from the road, where the terraces form a giant amphitheatre around a small village, with a trek to Tappiya Falls. Other terrace clusters include Bangaan, Hapao and Cambulo.

The area is remote, cool in the mornings and misty, and best explored on foot. Many visitors combine Banaue with Sagada, another mountain destination, and it is an eight- to nine-hour overnight bus trip from Manila. Planting season, from February to April, and the golden harvest from May to June, offer contrasting scenery.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Banaue and Batad

Batad is the highlight, with several other terrace sites.

  1. 01

    Batad Rice Terraces

    A natural amphitheatre of terraces with a village at the centre, seen from the Tappiya viewpoint.

    Tip Stay the night for sunrise.

  2. 02

    Banaue viewpoint

    The main terraces near Banaue town, with viewpoints along the road.

    Tip Go at sunrise.

  3. 03

    Tappiya Falls

    A waterfall at the end of a hike through Batad's terraces.

    Tip Bring a swimsuit.

  4. 04

    Bangaan Terraces

    A village with terraces near the road.

    Tip A short stop.

  5. 05

    Ifugao Museum

    A museum with woodcarving and artefacts.

    Tip Learn about the culture.

  6. 06

    Ifugao people

    Elders in traditional dress pose for photos in Banaue, for a donation.

    Tip Ask permission and tip.
