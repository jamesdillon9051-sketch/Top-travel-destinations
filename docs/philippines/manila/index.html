Welcome to Manila

Manila is the capital of the Philippines and the heart of Metro Manila, a huge urban area of about 14 million people made up of 16 cities. It is loud, hot, congested and endlessly busy, and most visitors treat it as a gateway. But with a little planning, it offers real rewards: colonial history, superb food, museums and an infectious energy.

The city was founded by the Spanish in 1571, and Intramuros, the walled city, was the centre of Spanish power in Asia. Its walls, gates, churches and Fort Santiago survive, although much was destroyed in the Second World War, including much of the rest of the old city. San Agustin Church, completed in 1607, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the oldest stone church in the country. Rizal Park, nearby, honours the national hero José Rizal, who was executed in 1896.

Elsewhere, Binondo is the world's oldest Chinatown, and a great place for a food crawl, and Manila Bay is famed for its sunsets. Modern Makati and Bonifacio Global City have malls, restaurants and nightlife. Manila has traffic and pollution, and street crime can occur, so travel with care and use Grab. Two days covers the highlights.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Manila

Sights cluster around Intramuros and Rizal Park.

  1. 01

    Fort Santiago

    A citadel on the Pasig River, where José Rizal was imprisoned before his execution, now a park and museum.

    Tip Go in the morning.

  2. 02

    Manila Bay sunset

    The Baywalk on Roxas Boulevard is a classic sunset spot, glowing orange over the water.

    Tip Arrive by 5:30 pm.

  3. 03

    Binondo Chinatown

    The world's oldest Chinatown, founded in 1594, packed with dumpling houses, bakeries and temples.

    Tip Join a food tour.

  4. 04

    Jeepneys of Manila

    The iconic public transport of the Philippines, a decorated and crowded jeepney.

    Tip Ask locals for the route.

  5. 05

    San Agustin Church

    The oldest stone church in the country, a UNESCO site with a painted ceiling and a museum.

    Tip Visit the museum.

  6. 06

    National Museum complex

    Free museums of art, natural history and anthropology.

    Tip Check opening hours.
