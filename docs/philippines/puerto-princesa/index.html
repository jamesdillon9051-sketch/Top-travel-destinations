Welcome to Puerto Princesa

Puerto Princesa is the capital of Palawan, a clean and orderly city on the island's east coast, and the main gateway for visitors, with an international airport and connections to El Nido and Coron. It is known as the City in a Forest, and for its cleanliness and environmental policies.

The main attraction is the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park, about 80 km north, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature. Its centrepiece is an 8-km underground river, one of the longest navigable in the world, which flows through a limestone cave into the sea. Visitors take a paddled boat for about 1.5 km into the cave, past chambers filled with stalactites and stalagmites, bats and swiftlets. Tickets are limited by permit, and must be arranged in advance through the city's tourism office.

Near the city, Honda Bay has sandbars, coral gardens and island hopping, and a firefly watching tour in the Iwahig River is popular in the evening. Most visitors spend two days in Puerto Princesa before heading north to El Nido by van, about 5 to 6 hours.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Puerto Princesa

The river is the highlight; other sights are nearby.

  1. 01

    Puerto Princesa Underground River

    An 8-km subterranean river, of which the first 1.5 km can be visited by boat, with cathedral-like chambers.

    Tip Book permits days ahead.

  2. 02

    Limestone formations

    The park's karst landscape of jagged limestone, forest and cliffs.

    Tip Look up at the peaks.

  3. 03

    Honda Bay

    Island hopping to sandbars such as Luli and Starfish Island.

    Tip Go early.

  4. 04

    Iwahig firefly watching

    A boat trip at night to see fireflies in the mangroves.

    Tip Book in the afternoon.

  5. 05

    Baker's Hill

    A hill garden with bread and a view.

    Tip Good for families.

  6. 06

    Plaza Cuartel

    A memorial to the 1944 massacre of prisoners of war.

    Tip Reflective stop.
