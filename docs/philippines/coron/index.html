Welcome to Coron

Coron is a town on Busuanga Island in the Calamian Islands, north of Palawan, and the base for a spectacular area of steep limestone islands, lakes and reefs. In September 1944, a US Navy air attack sank a fleet of Japanese supply ships in Coron Bay, and about a dozen wrecks now lie at depths of 10 to 40 metres, making it one of the best wreck-diving destinations in the world.

Above water, the highlights are Kayangan Lake, often called the cleanest lake in Asia, a brackish lake in a crater ringed by cliffs, and Barracuda Lake, where a thermocline makes the water suddenly warm. Twin Lagoon is two lagoons separated by a rock, and you swim through a gap from one to the other. There are also snorkelling reefs, beaches, and the Maquinit hot springs, a saltwater pool heated by geothermal activity.

The town has a busy waterfront and a viewpoint at Mount Tapyas reached by 700 steps. Most visitors spend three days on island tours, dives and relaxation, and many combine Coron with El Nido by ferry. Note that much of the area belongs to the Tagbanua people, who manage several sites, and fees support their ancestral domain.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Coron

Boat tours cover most of the sights.

  1. 01

    Kayangan Lake

    A crater lake reached by a short climb, with clear water and limestone walls, and a swimming platform.

    Tip Go early.

  2. 02

    Twin Lagoon

    Two lagoons connected through a narrow gap, with warm and cool layers of water.

    Tip Time for low tide.

  3. 03

    Coron's limestone islands

    The islands of the bay are steep karst formations.

    Tip Take a boat at sunrise.

  4. 04

    Japanese shipwrecks

    Wrecks such as the Akitsushima and Kogyo Maru, for divers.

    Tip Use a licensed dive centre.

  5. 05

    Maquinit Hot Springs

    A seaside hot spring, best at sunset.

    Tip Go in the evening.

  6. 06

    Mount Tapyas

    A hill with a giant cross and views over the town.

    Tip Climb at sunrise.
