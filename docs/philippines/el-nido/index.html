Welcome to El Nido

El Nido sits at the northern tip of Palawan, an elongated island in the west of the Philippines, on the edge of Bacuit Bay. The bay holds 45 islands and islets, mostly limestone karst, whose sheer cliffs, caves and lagoons feel like a fantasy landscape. The area is part of the Bacuit Archipelago and Palawan, often named the best island in the world by travel magazines.

The town itself is small and functional, a strip of guesthouses, dive shops, restaurants and tour offices on a curved beach. Visitors come for island-hopping: standard tours, called A, B, C and D, run by outriggers that visit lagoons such as the Big Lagoon and Small Lagoon, the Secret Lagoon, Shimizu Island, 7 Commando Beach, Hidden Beach, Matinloc Shrine and Helicopter Island. Each tour costs the same and covers different sites, so many visitors do two or three.

El Nido has grown fast, and can be crowded at peak times. Booking early tours, choosing small groups, and visiting quieter places like Nacpan Beach, Cadlao Island and Corong-Corong makes for a better trip. Weather is best from December to May, and the sea can be too rough for boats in the rainy season.

Why visit

Why visit El Nido?

El Nido is the best place in the Philippines for classic lagoon-and-limestone scenery.

  • Limestone lagoons

    Emerald water and cliffs.

  • Island hopping

    Four standard tours.

  • Hidden beaches

    Nacpan and 7 Commando.

  • Snorkelling and diving

    Reefs and marine life.

  • Sunsets

    Over Bacuit Bay.

Main attractions

Top things to see in El Nido

The sights are all on the water or on nearby beaches.

  1. 01

    Big Lagoon

    A wide lagoon on Miniloc Island, ringed by cliffs, and best explored by kayak.

    Tip Go on Tour A early.

  2. 02

    Limestone islands of Bacuit Bay

    The bay's islands are steep karst formations covered in forest.

    Tip Take a boat at sunrise.

  3. 03

    Secret Lagoon

    A hidden lagoon reached through a small hole in the cliff, where you must wade or swim.

    Tip Go before midday.

  4. 04

    Nacpan Beach

    A 4-km white-sand beach north of El Nido, reached by tricycle.

    Tip Good for sunset.

  5. 05

    Shimizu Island

    A snorkelling stop and lunch spot with clear water.

    Tip Bring a mask.

  6. 06

    Taraw Cliff

    A limestone cliff you can climb for a view over the bay.

    Tip Wear sturdy shoes.

Travel tips

El Nido travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Book tours a day ahead.
  • Bring cash.
  • Wear reef-safe sunscreen.
  • Go early to avoid crowds.
  • Check the weather.
  • Bring a dry bag.
  • Respect the environmental fee.
