Welcome to Cebu

Cebu is a long, narrow island in the Visayas, and Cebu City on its east coast is the second-largest city in the Philippines and the country's oldest, founded by the Spanish in 1565. The city has key historical sites: Magellan's Cross, planted in 1521 to mark the arrival of Christianity, and the Basilica del Santo Niño, home to the oldest Christian relic in the country. It is also the transport hub of the Visayas, with an international airport and ferries to Bohol, Negros, Leyte and beyond.

Beyond the city, Cebu is a paradise for water lovers. In Moalboal on the south-west coast, millions of sardines form a shimmering ball just off the beach, reachable by snorkelling from the shore. Nearby, Kawasan Falls in Badian is a series of turquoise waterfalls where canyoneering tours descend gorges, and Oslob is known for whale shark feeding, which is controversial for its effects on the animals. Off the north tip, Malapascua is the best place in the world to dive with thresher sharks.

Cebuano food is famous, especially lechon, the whole roast pig, considered the country's best. Three days covers the city, Moalboal and Kawasan, and Cebu is the natural gateway to Bohol.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Cebu

Cebu City is compact, and the marine sights are along the south-west coast.

  1. 01

    Magellan's Cross

    A cross planted by Ferdinand Magellan in 1521, housed in a small chapel with a painted ceiling.

    Tip Free to visit.

  2. 02

    Basilica del Santo Niño

    The oldest church in the country, home to a statue of the Christ Child, given by Magellan in 1521.

    Tip Dress modestly.

  3. 03

    Moalboal sardine run

    A gigantic school of sardines near the shore at Panagsama Beach, available to snorkellers and divers.

    Tip Keep your distance and do not touch.

  4. 04

    Kawasan Falls

    Multi-tier turquoise waterfalls in Badian, with canyoneering tours.

    Tip Go early.

  5. 05

    Oslob whale sharks

    Whale shark encounters, which involve feeding and are criticised by conservationists.

    Tip Consider skipping or choose ethical alternatives.

  6. 06

    Malapascua

    A small island north of Cebu, famous for thresher shark dives.

    Tip Dive at dawn.

Travel tips

Cebu travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Go to Kawasan early.
  • Do not touch sardines.
  • Reconsider Oslob.
  • Book canyoneering with reputable operators.
  • Use Grab in the city.
  • Carry cash.
  • Book ferries early.
