Welcome to Siargao

Siargao is a teardrop-shaped island off the north-east coast of Mindanao, and the surfing capital of the Philippines. Its main break, Cloud 9, a fast, hollow reef wave breaking just off General Luna, has hosted international surfing competitions since the 1990s, and the island now supports a laid-back scene of surf schools, cafés, yoga studios and hostels. It was hit hard by Typhoon Rai in December 2021, and has since rebuilt.

Surfing is best from August to November, when swells are biggest, but Siargao is an attractive island year-round. Coconut palm forests cover much of it, and inland there are mangrove-lined lagoons. Off the coast near General Luna, three islands, Naked Island, Daku Island and Guyam Island, form the classic island-hopping trip. In the north-east, the Magpupungko rock pools appear at low tide, and Sugba Lagoon offers clear green water for swimming.

Beginners can learn to surf on gentler waves, and non-surfers can enjoy scooter rides through palm groves, sunsets and beach clubs. The town of General Luna is the base, and the island is reached by a short flight from Cebu or Manila. The vibe is relaxed and increasingly cosmopolitan, and it is calmer than Boracay.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Siargao

Sights are spread around the island, best by scooter.

  1. 01

    Cloud 9 boardwalk

    A wooden boardwalk and viewing tower over the famous surf break, good for watching surfers.

    Tip Go at sunrise.

  2. 02

    Magpupungko Rock Pools

    Natural tidal pools in the rocks on the north-east coast, best at low tide.

    Tip Check the tide chart.

  3. 03

    Sugba Lagoon

    A clear green lagoon with a diving platform.

    Tip Join a tour.

  4. 04

    Naked Island

    A sandbar with no trees, off General Luna.

    Tip Bring shade.

  5. 05

    Sohoton Cove

    A lagoon and cave system on nearby Bucas Grande.

    Tip Book a boat.

  6. 06

    Coconut Road

    A road lined with palm trees.

    Tip Good for photos.
