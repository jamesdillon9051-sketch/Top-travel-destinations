Welcome to Hurghada

Hurghada is genuinely Egypt's original Red Sea resort city, a long strip of beach hotels on the mainland coast that first put this stretch of coastline on the map.

Its appeal rests genuinely on easy access to exceptional offshore diving and snorkelling, from Giftun Island's reefs to the wrecks scattered across Abu Nuhas.

Most visitors genuinely split time between resort beach days and boat trips, with the more boutique lagoon town of El Gouna an easy day trip up the coast.

Why visit

Why visit Hurghada?

Hurghada's case rests genuinely on being the most accessible, best-value base for Red Sea beach and diving holidays.

  • It's genuinely the most established Red Sea resort city

    Decades of tourism infrastructure mean flights, hotels and dive centres for every budget.

  • The offshore diving is genuinely exceptional

    Giftun Island's reefs and the Abu Nuhas wrecks rank among the Red Sea's best.

  • It's genuinely strong value for a Red Sea base

    Generally more affordable than Sharm El Sheikh, with comparable water and diving quality.

  • El Gouna is genuinely a short, easy day trip

    A more boutique lagoon town with its own beaches, restaurants and marina.

  • It works genuinely well for families

    All-inclusive resorts, calm swimming beaches and organised excursions suit every age.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Hurghada

Hurghada's essential sights are genuinely spread between its beach resort strip and the reefs and islands offshore.

  1. 01

    Hurghada's beach strip

    Genuinely a long run of sandy beach hotels facing the Red Sea, the backbone of most visits here.

    Tip Genuinely most resort beaches are private to guests, though public beach clubs exist nearby.

  2. 02

    Giftun Island

    Genuinely a protected island a short boat ride offshore, its fringing reefs among the best snorkelling near Hurghada.

    Tip Genuinely book a full-day boat trip that includes multiple snorkel or dive stops.

  3. 03

    Hurghada Marina

    Genuinely the town's smartest stretch, lined with restaurants, cafés and yachts departing for reef trips.

    Tip Genuinely a pleasant spot for an evening stroll and dinner by the water.

  4. 04

    El Gouna

    Genuinely a more boutique, design-conscious lagoon town twenty minutes up the coast, popular as a day trip or standalone stay.

    Tip Genuinely rent a bike once there — El Gouna's lagoons and bridges are made for it.

  5. 05

    Dolphin House (Shaab El Erg)

    Genuinely a reef known for a resident pod of wild spinner dolphins, a real highlight for snorkellers on a good day.

    Tip Genuinely choose an operator that keeps a respectful distance and never chases the pod.

  6. 06

    The Abu Nuhas wrecks

    Genuinely known among divers as the Red Sea's 'Ships' Graveyard', a reef scattered with several historic wrecks.

    Tip Genuinely a trip best suited to certified divers with some wreck diving experience.

  7. 07

    Sakkala and El Dahar

    Genuinely Hurghada's older, more local quarters, with a working market, mosque and a far less touristy feel than the resort strip.

    Tip Genuinely worth a taxi over for an authentic meal away from the hotels.

Things to do

Best things to do in Hurghada

Hurghada genuinely centres on the water, with boat trips to reefs, wrecks and islands filling most itineraries.

  1. Snorkel or dive Giftun Island

    A genuinely classic Hurghada day out, boating to the island's reefs for snorkelling or diving.

    Tip Genuinely choose a smaller boat trip over a large day-cruise for a better experience in the water.

  2. Dive the wrecks at Abu Nuhas

    A genuinely bucket-list trip for certified divers, exploring several historic shipwrecks in one reef system.

    Tip Genuinely book through a dive centre experienced with the site's currents and depths.

  3. Take a boat trip to see wild dolphins

    A genuinely memorable excursion out to Shaab El Erg's resident spinner dolphin pod.

    Tip Genuinely go with an operator that limits group size and respects the dolphins' space.

  4. Ride a glass-bottom boat

    A genuinely easy way to see the reef for non-swimmers or anyone travelling with young children.

    Tip Genuinely widely offered from the marina and resort tour desks.

  5. Go on a desert safari by quad bike

    A genuinely popular half-day break from the water, riding out into the desert with a Bedouin camp stop.

    Tip Genuinely afternoon or sunset departures avoid the hottest part of the day.

  6. Day-trip to El Gouna

    A genuinely worthwhile change of pace, exploring El Gouna's lagoons, marina and beach clubs by bike or taxi.

    Tip Genuinely easy to arrange independently — no need for an organised tour.

Local food

What to eat in Hurghada

Hurghada's food genuinely spans resort international dining alongside fresh Red Sea seafood and Egyptian staples.

  • Fresh Red Sea seafood

    Genuinely a highlight of dining here, fish and shellfish landed fresh from the surrounding waters.

    Where to try it Restaurants around the marina and throughout the resort strip.

  • Grilled kofta

    A genuinely reliable classic, found throughout the town's restaurants.

    Where to try it Restaurants throughout Hurghada.

  • Koshari

    Genuinely available at Egyptian restaurants away from the resort strip, a taste of home for local staff and visitors alike.

    Where to try it Local restaurants around Sakkala and El Dahar.

  • Ful medames

    A genuinely traditional breakfast option, found at both resort buffets and local cafés.

    Where to try it Cafés and resort breakfasts throughout Hurghada.

  • Taameya (Egyptian falafel)

    Genuinely Egypt's own fava bean version of falafel, available throughout the town.

    Where to try it Restaurants and street stalls throughout Hurghada.

  • Karkade (hibiscus tea)

    Genuinely a refreshing national drink, a nice complement to a day on the water.

    Where to try it Cafés and restaurants throughout the resort area.

Transport

Getting to and around Hurghada

Hurghada is genuinely well connected by direct flights, both domestic and international.

Getting there

  • Flight from Cairo

    Genuinely the standard domestic connection, taking about an hour.

    Frequent daily flights make Hurghada an easy Red Sea addition to a Nile-focused trip.

  • Direct international flights

    Genuinely available from many European cities, especially in high season.

    A popular direct resort destination not requiring a Cairo stopover.

  • Bus from Cairo or Luxor

    Genuinely a longer but budget-friendly overland option.

    Takes around six hours from Cairo, or roughly four from Luxor.

Getting around

Hurghada is genuinely spread along a long resort strip, with taxis and organised transfers the standard way around.

  • Resort shuttles and tour transfers

    Genuinely the standard way to reach boat trips and excursions from your hotel.

    Most tour operators include hotel pickup as part of the trip.

  • Taxis

    Genuinely useful for reaching the marina, restaurants or El Gouna.

    Widely available, though agree the fare before starting your journey.

  • On foot within your resort area

    Genuinely easy within a single stretch of the strip.

    Covering the full length of Hurghada on foot is impractical given its size.

When to go

Best time to visit Hurghada

Hurghada is genuinely a year-round destination, with water temperatures staying warm even in winter.

  • Spring and autumn (March–May, September–November)

    Best time

    Genuinely the most comfortable air and water temperatures for diving and beach time.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Good

    Genuinely very hot on land, though the water itself stays excellent for diving and snorkelling.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Good

    Genuinely mild and popular with visitors escaping colder climates, water temperatures still comfortable.

Worth planning around: Nothing strongly — Hurghada's genuinely warm Red Sea waters make it a workable destination in any season.

Travel tips

Hurghada travel tips

Practical notes that make a Hurghada visit noticeably smoother.

  • Book genuinely with a reputable dive centre for any wreck or reef diving.
  • Bring genuinely your own certification card if you're a qualified diver.
  • Pack genuinely reef-safe sunscreen to help protect the coral.
  • Agree genuinely a price before any independently arranged desert safari or taxi.
  • Stay genuinely hydrated and use sun protection, even on boat trips.
  • Consider genuinely a half-board or all-inclusive resort — independent dining options are more spread out than in Sharm El Sheikh.

FAQ

Hurghada: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Hurghada?

Four to six days genuinely allows for a proper mix of beach time, diving trips and a day trip to El Gouna.

How does Hurghada compare to Sharm El Sheikh?

Genuinely more affordable and less polished, with excellent offshore reefs and wreck diving rather than Sharm's dramatic drop-offs.

Is Hurghada good for families?

Yes, genuinely — all-inclusive resorts and calm beaches make it one of Egypt's most family-friendly bases.

Do you need to be a certified diver to visit?

No, genuinely not — excellent snorkelling and beginner dive courses are widely available.

Can beginners dive the wrecks at Abu Nuhas?

Some genuinely shallower sections are accessible to newer divers, though the deeper wrecks suit more experienced divers.

Can you combine Hurghada with the Nile Valley?

Yes, genuinely easily — a direct flight connects to Luxor or Cairo, making it a natural Red Sea extension to a Nile-focused Egypt trip.
