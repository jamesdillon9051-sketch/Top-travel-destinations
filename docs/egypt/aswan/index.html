Welcome to Aswan

Aswan is genuinely Egypt's most relaxed Nile city, its granite hills and wide river views giving it a noticeably calmer pace than Cairo or even Luxor.

It's also genuinely the heart of Nubian culture in Egypt, a distinct community with its own language, colourful architecture and traditions along this stretch of the Nile, predating the pharaonic history that surrounds it.

Most visitors genuinely spend a few days here, combining the beautifully relocated Philae Temple and a felucca sail with a genuinely essential day trip to Abu Simbel, Ramses II's colossal monument carved directly into a cliff face.

Aswan's case rests on genuinely combining major ancient monuments with a distinct living Nubian culture and a relaxed river pace.

  • Abu Simbel is genuinely one of Egypt's most awe-inspiring monuments

    Colossal statues of Ramses II carved directly into a cliff, later relocated to save it from flooding.

  • Philae Temple is genuinely beautifully set on its own island

  • Nubian culture here is genuinely distinct and living

  • Aswan is genuinely the most relaxed of Egypt's major Nile cities

Things to do

Best things to do in Aswan

Aswan genuinely rewards a relaxed pace, mixing major sites with the river itself.

  1. Sail a felucca around Aswan's islands

    A genuinely essential Aswan experience, drifting past granite boulders and green islands on a traditional sailboat.

    Tip Genuinely negotiate the price and route with your captain before setting off.

  2. Visit Abu Simbel at dawn

    Tip Genuinely book a tour or private driver ahead — the site sits about three hours from Aswan.

  3. Explore Philae Temple

    Tip Genuinely visit late afternoon for softer light on the temple's carved reliefs.

  4. Visit a Nubian village and market

  5. See the Unfinished Obelisk up close

  6. Explore Elephantine Island's ruins

    A genuinely rewarding half-day, combining ancient ruins with a Nubian village on the same island.

    Tip Genuinely the public ferry across is cheap and frequent, an easy independent excursion.

When to go

Aswan's genuinely desert climate makes it one of Egypt's hottest cities, with winter and shoulder season far more comfortable.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Best time

    Genuinely the mildest, most comfortable temperatures for sightseeing and felucca sails.

  • Shoulder months (October–November, March–April)

    Best time

    Good

FAQ

Aswan: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Aswan?

Two to three days genuinely covers Philae Temple, Abu Simbel and a felucca sail comfortably.

Is Abu Simbel worth the long day trip from Aswan?

Yes, genuinely — it's one of Egypt's most awe-inspiring monuments, and the journey is well organised via tour or private driver.

Why was Philae Temple relocated?

Genuinely to save it from the rising waters caused by the Aswan Dam, moved stone by stone to a nearby island in a major 20th-century engineering effort.

What is Aswan known for?

Genuinely its relaxed pace, Nubian culture, and major monuments including Philae Temple and nearby Abu Simbel.

Is Aswan part of a typical Nile cruise?

Yes, genuinely — most Luxor-Aswan cruises begin or end here, making it a natural stop on a classic Egypt itinerary.

Can you visit a Nubian village independently?

Yes, genuinely — boats to Nubian villages and Elephantine Island are easy to arrange directly from Aswan's corniche.
