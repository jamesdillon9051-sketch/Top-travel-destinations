Welcome to Cairo & Giza

Cairo is genuinely one of the world's great sprawling capitals, a city of over 20 million people that has grown right up to the edge of the Giza plateau, where the last surviving Ancient Wonder of the World still stands.

The Great Pyramids are genuinely almost impossibly old — already ancient ruins by the time of Cleopatra, built more than 4,500 years ago and still the most recognisable monuments on Earth.

Most visitors genuinely spend a few days here at the start of their Egypt trip, splitting time between Giza's pyramids, the treasures of the Grand Egyptian Museum, and the old city's centuries-old mosques and bazaars.

Why visit Cairo & Giza?

Main attractions

Top things to see in Cairo & Giza

Cairo and Giza's essential sights combine genuinely the world's most famous ancient monuments with a living historic old city.

  1. 01

    The Great Pyramids of Giza

    Genuinely the last surviving Ancient Wonder of the World, three monumental pyramids built over 4,500 years ago as royal tombs.

    Tip Genuinely arrive at opening time to beat both the desert heat and the busiest tour groups.

  2. 02

    The Great Sphinx

    Genuinely one of the world's largest and oldest monolithic statues, its lion's body and human face guarding the pyramid complex.

    Tip Genuinely the classic photo angle, appearing to touch the Sphinx's nose, requires standing at a specific spot your guide can show you.

  3. 03

    The Grand Egyptian Museum

    Genuinely one of the world's largest archaeological museums, housing Tutankhamun's complete treasure collection among thousands of other artefacts.

    Tip Genuinely allow a full half-day — the collection is far too extensive to rush.

  4. 04

    The Egyptian Museum, Tahrir Square

    Genuinely Cairo's original antiquities museum, a beautifully old-fashioned setting still holding significant artefacts.

    Tip Genuinely worthwhile even with the Grand Egyptian Museum now open, for a different, more historic display style.

  5. 05

    Khan el-Khalili

    Genuinely a centuries-old bazaar, its narrow lanes packed with spices, lanterns, jewellery and workshops still in daily use.

    Tip Genuinely polite bargaining is expected and part of the shopping experience here.

  6. 06

    The Cairo Citadel & Mohammed Ali Mosque

    Genuinely a striking Ottoman-style mosque atop a medieval fortress, with sweeping views over the whole city.

    Tip Genuinely the citadel's elevated position offers one of Cairo's best panoramic views, pyramids included on a clear day.

  7. 07

    Sultan Hassan Mosque

    Genuinely one of Cairo's most impressive medieval mosques, a masterpiece of Mamluk architecture dating to the 14th century.

    Tip Genuinely the mosque's massive scale is best appreciated from its central courtyard.

Things to do

Best things to do in Cairo & Giza

Cairo and Giza genuinely reward mixing the pyramids with the old city's mosques and bazaars.

  1. Ride a camel around the pyramids

    A genuinely classic, if slightly touristy, way to see the pyramids from a desert vantage point.

    Tip Genuinely agree the price and duration clearly before mounting — this is a common area for overcharging.

  2. Explore the Grand Egyptian Museum's collection

    A genuinely absorbing half-day, taking in thousands of years of ancient Egyptian history and artefacts.

    Tip Genuinely book tickets online ahead — the museum's popularity means walk-up queues can be long.

  3. Bargain at Khan el-Khalili

    A genuinely lively way to spend an afternoon, browsing spices, textiles and crafts through the bazaar's narrow lanes.

    Tip Genuinely a small glass of tea at a bazaar café makes a nice break between shopping stops.

  4. Climb the Cairo Citadel

    A genuinely worthwhile half-day, exploring the mosque's ornate interior and the citadel's sweeping city views.

    Tip Genuinely combine with a visit to the nearby National Military Museum, included within the citadel grounds.

  5. Explore Islamic Cairo's mosques

    A genuinely rewarding walk through centuries of Islamic architecture, still functioning as active places of worship.

    Tip Genuinely dress modestly and remove shoes before entering any mosque.

  6. View the pyramids from a desert panorama point

    A genuinely essential photo stop, a slightly elevated desert viewpoint capturing all three pyramids together.

    Tip Genuinely visit in late afternoon for the warmest, most flattering light on the stone.

Transport

Getting to and around Cairo & Giza

Cairo is genuinely Egypt's main international gateway, with most visitors arriving here first regardless of their onward plans.

Getting there

  • International flight to Cairo Airport

    Genuinely most international routes into Egypt land here.

    Well connected to Europe, the Middle East and increasingly further afield.

  • Domestic flight onward

    Genuinely the standard way to reach Luxor, Aswan and the Red Sea coast.

    Frequent daily flights connect Cairo to nearly every major Egypt destination.

  • Overnight train

    Genuinely a budget-friendly, if slower, option for reaching Luxor or Aswan.

    Sleeper cabins save a hotel night while covering real distance.

Getting around

Cairo is genuinely a sprawling, traffic-heavy city, and most visitors rely on taxis, rideshare apps or organised tours.

  • Rideshare apps and taxis

    Genuinely the easiest way around, widely available and inexpensive.

    Traffic can be heavy, so budget extra time between destinations.

  • Organised tours or private guides

    Genuinely a practical, efficient way to see Giza and the old city together.

    Widely available and often worthwhile for the historical context alone.

  • The Cairo Metro

    Genuinely a fast, cheap option for certain routes, avoiding the worst of the traffic.

    Limited coverage of tourist sites, but useful for some downtown journeys.

FAQ

Cairo & Giza: frequently asked questions

How many days do you need in Cairo and Giza?

Three days genuinely covers the pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum and Islamic Cairo comfortably.

Are the pyramids actually in Cairo?

Genuinely on Cairo's western edge, in Giza — technically a separate city that has effectively merged with greater Cairo's sprawl.

Is the Grand Egyptian Museum worth visiting alongside the older Egyptian Museum?

Yes, genuinely both are worthwhile — the Grand Egyptian Museum houses the major Tutankhamun collection, while the older museum offers a different, more historic display style.

Is Cairo safe for tourists?

Generally yes in main tourist areas, with genuinely persistent touting around Giza the main practical nuisance rather than serious danger.

How do you avoid pyramid scams?

Genuinely agree prices upfront for any camel ride, guide or 'special access' offer, and be firm about declining unsolicited assistance.

Can you enter the pyramids themselves?

Yes, genuinely, for an additional fee — though the interior chambers are relatively plain compared to the exterior's scale.
