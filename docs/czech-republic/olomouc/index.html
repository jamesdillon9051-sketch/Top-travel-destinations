Welcome to Olomouc

Olomouc is genuinely Moravia's other great historic city, a compact university town whose old town rivals Prague's for architecture while receiving a fraction of the visitors.

Its centrepiece is genuinely the Holy Trinity Column, a UNESCO-listed baroque sculpture the tallest of its kind anywhere in Central Europe.

The city is also genuinely home to one of the country's most unusual astronomical clocks, rebuilt after the war in bold socialist-realist style, and to Olomoucké tvarůžky, a pungent local cheese found nowhere else.

Why visit

Why visit Olomouc?

Olomouc's case rests genuinely on remarkable baroque architecture paired with a lively, youthful university city atmosphere.

  • The Holy Trinity Column is genuinely Central Europe's tallest baroque sculpture

    A UNESCO World Heritage Site standing 35 metres above the main square.

  • The Astronomical Clock is genuinely unlike any other in the country

    Rebuilt in striking socialist-realist mosaic style after wartime damage.

  • The old town is genuinely one of the country's best-preserved

    Baroque squares and six ornate fountains, with far fewer crowds than Prague.

  • It's genuinely home to a unique local cheese

    Olomoucké tvarůžky, a pungent speciality made nowhere else in the world.

  • It's genuinely a lively university city

    A large student population keeps Olomouc's café and bar scene genuinely buzzing.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Olomouc

Olomouc's essential sights are genuinely concentrated around its baroque Upper and Lower Squares.

  1. 01

    The Holy Trinity Column

    Genuinely the tallest baroque sculptural column in Central Europe, and a UNESCO World Heritage Site in its own right.

    Tip Genuinely visit at dusk, when floodlighting brings out the sculpture's full detail.

  2. 02

    The Olomouc Astronomical Clock

    Genuinely unlike any other clock in the country, rebuilt after wartime damage in bold socialist-realist mosaic style depicting workers rather than saints.

    Tip Genuinely a fascinating contrast to Prague's medieval version — worth seeking out specifically for that reason.

  3. 03

    St Wenceslas Cathedral

    Genuinely a soaring neo-Gothic cathedral with the second-tallest church tower in the country.

    Tip Genuinely combine with a stroll through the adjacent Přemyslid Palace grounds.

  4. 04

    Horní náměstí (Upper Square) and Town Hall

    Genuinely the city's grand baroque heart, its ornate town hall and column ringed by colourful facades.

    Tip Genuinely arrive at the top of the hour to see the Astronomical Clock's figures in motion.

  5. 05

    The Caesar Fountain

    Genuinely the grandest of the city's six baroque fountains, depicting Julius Caesar on horseback.

    Tip Genuinely try to spot all six fountains scattered across the old town during your visit.

  6. 06

    The Archbishop's Palace

    Genuinely a grand baroque palace that has hosted European royalty, including a young Emperor Franz Joseph's coronation confirmation.

    Tip Genuinely tour the ornate interior for a look at Olomouc's ecclesiastical history.

  7. 07

    Olomouc's historic squares

    Genuinely worth simply wandering, a near-complete baroque townscape with far fewer visitors than Prague's equivalent streets.

    Tip Genuinely explore both the Upper and Lower Squares to see the full scope of the old town.

Things to do

Best things to do in Olomouc

Olomouc genuinely rewards a relaxed pace, its baroque squares, fountains and cafés built for unhurried wandering.

  1. Marvel at the Holy Trinity Column

    A genuinely essential stop, taking in the sculpture's scale and detail from every angle.

    Tip Genuinely check inside the column's small chapel, open on select days.

  2. Watch the Astronomical Clock's figures in motion

    A genuinely quirky spectacle, mosaic figures of workers and athletes marking the hour instead of saints.

    Tip Genuinely arrive a few minutes before the hour for the best view of the moving figures.

  3. Tour St Wenceslas Cathedral

    A genuinely rewarding visit, taking in the cathedral's soaring neo-Gothic nave and treasury.

    Tip Genuinely climb the tower if it's open for one of the city's best views.

  4. Fountain-hop around the old town

    A genuinely fun, low-key way to explore, tracking down all six of the old town's baroque fountains.

    Tip Genuinely pick up a map from the tourist information office to help find them all.

  5. Tour the Archbishop's Palace

    A genuinely grand interior, including a throne room used for significant moments in Habsburg history.

    Tip Genuinely check opening hours ahead, as tours don't run daily.

  6. Wander between the Upper and Lower Squares

    A genuinely relaxed way to spend an afternoon, taking in the old town's baroque architecture at an easy pace.

    Tip Genuinely stop at a café along the way — Olomouc's student population keeps the scene lively.

Local food

What to eat in Olomouc

Olomouc's food genuinely means standard Czech and Moravian classics, plus one truly unique local speciality.

  • Olomoucké tvarůžky (Olomouc cheese)

    Genuinely a local speciality found nowhere else in the world, a pungent, low-fat curd cheese with a devoted following.

    Where to try it Restaurants and specialty shops throughout the old town.

  • Svíčková (marinated beef in cream sauce)

    Genuinely a reliable classic, found at traditional restaurants throughout the city.

    Where to try it Traditional restaurants around the Upper and Lower Squares.

  • Guláš with dumplings

    Genuinely a warming, filling classic, common on menus throughout the old town.

    Where to try it Traditional pubs throughout central Olomouc.

  • Smažený sýr (fried cheese)

    Genuinely a satisfying pub classic, a good contrast to the sharper flavour of tvarůžky.

    Where to try it Traditional pubs throughout the city.

  • Czech beer (pivo)

    Genuinely a natural pairing with tvarůžky, which is traditionally eaten with beer and bread.

    Where to try it Pubs and cafés throughout the old town.

  • Koláče (fruit-filled pastries)

    Genuinely a nice sweet stop, found at bakeries and cafés across the old town.

    Where to try it Bakeries around the Upper and Lower Squares.
