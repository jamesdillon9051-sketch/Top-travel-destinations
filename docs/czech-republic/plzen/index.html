Welcome to Plzeň

Plzeň is genuinely the birthplace of one of the world's most influential beer styles, the pale lager first brewed here in 1842 that went on to define pilsner everywhere.

Beyond the brewery, it's genuinely a handsome West Bohemian city in its own right, with a soaring Gothic cathedral and one of Europe's largest surviving synagogues.

It works genuinely well as a change of pace from Prague, a real working city rather than a purely tourist-facing centre, with a brewery tour that ranks among the country's best.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Plzeň

Plzeň's essential sights are genuinely spread between the historic brewery and the compact old town centre.

  1. 01

    The Pilsner Urquell Brewery

    Genuinely the birthplace of pilsner, brewing since 1842 on the same site it still operates from today.

    Tip Genuinely book the full tour, which ends with a taste straight from an oak lagering tank in the historic cellars.

  2. 02

    The brewery's historic gate

    Genuinely an ornate wrought-iron gateway, among the most photographed entrances in Czech brewing.

    Tip Genuinely a classic photo stop before or after the brewery tour itself.

  3. 03

    St Bartholomew's Cathedral

    Genuinely home to the tallest church tower in the Czech Republic, dominating the main square.

    Tip Genuinely climb the tower for a sweeping view over the old town's rooftops.

  4. 04

    St Bartholomew's Gothic interior

    Genuinely worth a proper look inside, a soaring Gothic nave with a revered medieval Madonna statue.

    Tip Genuinely check for a free organ recital during your visit.

  5. 05

    The Great Synagogue

    Genuinely one of the largest surviving synagogues in Europe, its Moorish-influenced towers a striking city landmark.

    Tip Genuinely check opening hours ahead — it operates as a functioning concert and exhibition space, not a daily-open museum.

  6. 06

    Náměstí Republiky (Republic Square)

    Genuinely the city's handsome main square, ringed by Renaissance and baroque townhouses.

    Tip Genuinely a nice spot to sit at a café and watch the city go by.

  7. 07

    The square's historic townhouses

    Genuinely worth a slow look, arcaded facades spanning several centuries of Bohemian architecture.

    Tip Genuinely look up — the upper facades carry far more detail than street level suggests.

Things to do

Best things to do in Plzeň

Plzeň genuinely centres on its historic brewery, with a handsome old town to fill out the rest of a visit.

  1. Tour the Pilsner Urquell Brewery

    A genuinely essential Plzeň experience, tracing the brewing process from the original 1842 recipe to today.

    Tip Genuinely book online ahead — English-language tour slots fill up in peak season.

  2. Taste unfiltered lager straight from the tank

    A genuinely unmissable finish to the brewery tour, unfiltered, unpasteurised beer poured in the historic cellars.

    Tip Genuinely it tastes noticeably different from the bottled version sold worldwide — worth comparing.

  3. Climb St Bartholomew's cathedral tower

    A genuinely rewarding climb, the country's tallest church tower offering a wide view over the city.

    Tip Genuinely worth it for the view alone on a clear day.

  4. Visit the Great Synagogue's interior

    A genuinely striking interior, restored after decades of neglect under communist rule.

    Tip Genuinely check for a concert during your visit — the acoustics inside are excellent.

  5. Wander Náměstí Republiky

    A genuinely relaxed way to spend an hour, taking in the square's historic facades and café terraces.

    Tip Genuinely check the tourist information office in the square for any events during your visit.

  6. See the brewery's modern production in action

    A genuinely interesting contrast, seeing how a 19th-century recipe scales to modern global production.

    Tip Genuinely included as part of the brewery's extended tour options.

Local food

What to eat in Plzeň

Plzeň's food genuinely centres on its brewing heritage, alongside standard hearty Czech classics.

  • Original unfiltered Pilsner Urquell

    Genuinely the reason to visit, tasting the unfiltered version only available at the source.

    Where to try it The brewery's own pub, and traditional pubs throughout the city.

  • Svíčková (marinated beef in cream sauce)

    Genuinely a reliable classic, found at traditional restaurants throughout the old town.

    Where to try it Traditional restaurants around Náměstí Republiky.

  • Guláš with dumplings

    Genuinely a warming pub staple, best paired with a fresh-poured Pilsner Urquell.

    Where to try it Traditional pubs throughout the city centre.

  • Vepřové koleno (roast pork knee)

    Genuinely a hearty, shareable option, well suited to a long pub session after the brewery tour.

    Where to try it Traditional beer halls throughout Plzeň.

  • Smažený sýr (fried cheese)

    Genuinely a satisfying pub classic, breaded fried cheese with tartar sauce and potatoes.

    Where to try it Traditional pubs throughout the city.

  • Koláče (fruit-filled pastries)

    Genuinely a nice sweet stop between sightseeing, found at bakeries around the old town.

    Where to try it Bakeries near Náměstí Republiky.
