Welcome to Bohemian Switzerland

Bohemian Switzerland is genuinely one of the Czech Republic's most dramatic landscapes, a national park of sandstone towers, deep gorges and dense forest hard against the German border.

Its centrepiece is genuinely Pravčická Gate, the largest natural sandstone arch in Europe, though the wider park's gorges and rock labyrinths reward days of exploration beyond it.

It's genuinely popular as a day trip from Prague, though the park's scale and network of trails make an overnight stay in Hřensko or Mezní Louka a genuinely worthwhile extension.

Why visit

Why visit Bohemian Switzerland?

Bohemian Switzerland's case rests genuinely on landscape unlike anywhere else in the Czech Republic.

  • Pravčická Gate is genuinely Europe's largest natural sandstone arch

    A striking geological centrepiece that gave the whole park its name.

  • Edmund Gorge is genuinely a singular experience

    A silent boat ride between sheer sandstone walls rising straight from the water.

  • The hiking here is genuinely excellent

    Well-marked trails through sandstone labyrinths and dense forest for every fitness level.

  • It's genuinely dramatically different from Bohemia's castle towns

    A pure nature escape rather than another historic centre.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Bohemian Switzerland

Bohemian Switzerland's essential sights are genuinely its sandstone arch, river gorges and rock formations.

  1. 01

    Pravčická Gate

    Genuinely the largest natural sandstone arch in Europe, and the park's defining landmark.

    Tip Genuinely arrive early — this is the park's single most visited spot and does get crowded by midday.

  2. 02

    The trail to Pravčická Gate

    Genuinely a rewarding walk in itself, climbing through forest and past smaller rock formations en route.

    Tip Genuinely allow an hour or so each way from Hřensko at an unhurried pace.

  3. 03

    Edmund Gorge (Edmundova soutěska)

    Genuinely one of the park's signature experiences, a narrow river gorge with sheer sandstone walls on both sides.

    Tip Genuinely a small boat ride covers part of the gorge — book at the entrance kiosk.

  4. 04

    Hřensko village

    Genuinely the park's main gateway village, tucked into a narrow valley where the Kamenice meets the Elbe.

    Tip Genuinely a natural base for hikes to both Pravčická Gate and Edmund Gorge.

  5. 05

    Tiské stěny (Tisá Rocks)

    Genuinely a maze-like labyrinth of sandstone towers and narrow passages, a little away from the park's busier core.

    Tip Genuinely quieter than Pravčická Gate, a good choice if you want the scenery without the crowds.

  6. 06

    The park's sandstone towers

    Genuinely scattered throughout the park, dramatic eroded towers rising straight out of dense forest.

    Tip Genuinely several marked viewpoints offer a wide look over the formations at once.

  7. 07

    Mezní Louka

    Genuinely a useful hub deep in the park, with trail access and a National Park Express bus stop.

    Tip Genuinely a good alternative base to Hřensko if you want to be closer to the park's interior trails.

Things to do

Best things to do in Bohemian Switzerland

Bohemian Switzerland genuinely rewards a full day of hiking, split between its signature sights and quieter corners.

  1. Hike to Pravčická Gate

    A genuinely essential hike, climbing through forest to the park's iconic sandstone arch.

    Tip Genuinely start early from Hřensko to beat both the crowds and the midday heat.

  2. Take a boat through Edmund Gorge

    A genuinely serene experience, gliding silently beneath sheer sandstone walls on a small wooden boat.

    Tip Genuinely combine with the Pravčická Gate hike for a full, satisfying day in the park.

  3. Explore the Tiské stěny rock labyrinth

    A genuinely fun, slightly adventurous walk, squeezing through narrow passages between towering rock walls.

    Tip Genuinely wear sturdy shoes — the paths are uneven and occasionally narrow.

  4. Wander Hřensko's riverside village

    A genuinely relaxed way to start or end a day of hiking, strolling the village's short riverside stretch.

    Tip Genuinely stock up on water and snacks here before heading into the park.

  5. Photograph the park's sandstone towers

    A genuinely rewarding pursuit, the park's marked viewpoints offering dramatic wide shots of the formations.

    Tip Genuinely golden hour light picks out the sandstone's texture particularly well.

  6. Base yourself at Mezní Louka for the interior trails

    A genuinely useful option for a longer stay, putting you closer to trails deeper in the park.

    Tip Genuinely the National Park Express bus connects Mezní Louka easily back to Hřensko.

Transport

Getting to and around Bohemian Switzerland

Bohemian Switzerland is genuinely reachable as a day trip from Prague, though it takes a bit more effort than closer Bohemian towns.

Getting there

  • Organised day trip from Prague

    Genuinely the easiest option, covering both Pravčická Gate and Edmund Gorge in one day.

    Widely available from Prague tour operators, typically a full-day trip.

  • Train to Děčín, then bus

    Genuinely doable independently, with a change partway.

    Takes around 2.5 to 3 hours total from Prague.

  • Car or private driver

    Genuinely the most flexible option, especially for reaching quieter corners like Tiské stěny.

    About two hours from Prague.

Getting around

Getting around the park genuinely means hiking trails, with a shuttle bus covering the longer stretches.

  • On foot

    Genuinely the only way to reach Pravčická Gate and most of the park's key sights.

    Well-marked trails suit a range of fitness levels.

  • National Park Express shuttle bus

    Genuinely useful for covering longer distances between trailheads.

    Connects Hřensko, Mezní Louka and other key points through the park.

  • Small boat through Edmund Gorge

    Genuinely the only way to properly experience the gorge itself.

    Board at the entrance kiosk a short walk from Hřensko.

When to go

Best time to visit Bohemian Switzerland

Bohemian Switzerland is genuinely best visited outside peak summer crowds and winter's icy trails.

  • Late spring (May–June)

    Best time

    Genuinely comfortable hiking temperatures with fresh green forest.

  • Summer (July–August)

    Good

    Genuinely the busiest season, especially at Pravčická Gate — arrive early to beat the crowds.

  • Autumn (September–October)

    Best time

    Genuinely spectacular forest colour, with noticeably fewer visitors than summer.

  • Winter (November–February)

    Good

    Genuinely beautiful under snow, though some trails and the gorge boat close for the season.

Worth planning around: Genuinely check for seasonal trail or gorge-boat closures before a winter visit.

Travel tips

Bohemian Switzerland travel tips

Practical notes that make a Bohemian Switzerland visit noticeably smoother.

  • Start genuinely early to reach Pravčická Gate before the day's tour groups.
  • Wear genuinely proper hiking shoes — trails are well maintained but uneven in places.
  • Check genuinely seasonal opening hours for the Edmund Gorge boat ride ahead of a visit.
  • Bring genuinely water and snacks — options thin out once you're on the trails.
  • Consider genuinely staying overnight for a far less rushed, less crowded experience.
  • Pack genuinely a rain layer — weather in the gorges can shift quickly.

FAQ

Bohemian Switzerland: frequently asked questions

Is Bohemian Switzerland worth a day trip from Prague?

Yes, genuinely — Pravčická Gate and Edmund Gorge together make for one of the most rewarding nature day trips near Prague.

How do you get to Bohemian Switzerland from Prague?

Genuinely easiest by organised day trip, or independently by train to Děčín followed by a local bus.

How difficult is the hike to Pravčická Gate?

Genuinely moderate — well-marked forest trails suited to most fitness levels, taking about an hour each way from Hřensko.

Is the Edmund Gorge boat ride worth it?

Yes, genuinely — it's a uniquely peaceful way to see the park's sheer sandstone walls up close.

How many days do you need in Bohemian Switzerland?

One full day genuinely covers the highlights; two days allows time for quieter spots like Tiské stěny.

Is Bohemian Switzerland crowded?

Pravčická Gate genuinely gets busy by midday in summer; an early start or quieter corners of the park stay noticeably calmer.
