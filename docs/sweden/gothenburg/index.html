Welcome to Gothenburg

Gothenburg, or Goteborg, is Sweden's second-largest city and its main port. It is friendlier and more laid-back than the capital, with canals built by Dutch engineers, wide boulevards, parks and a strong food culture.

The Haga district preserves 19th-century wooden houses now filled with cafes, selling giant cinnamon buns, and shops. Feskekorka, the fish church, is a market hall built like a church, and the seafood from the west coast is excellent. Slottsskogen park, with its free zoo, is the city's green lung, and the Liseberg amusement park is a classic family day out.

Ferries run to the southern archipelago, a group of car-free islands with fishing harbours and swimming rocks. The city is easy to explore on trams and on foot.
