Welcome to Stockholm

Stockholm is built across fourteen islands where Lake Malaren meets the Baltic Sea, and water is part of nearly every view. The medieval old town of Gamla Stan sits at its heart, with cobbled lanes, colourful buildings and the Royal Palace, while modern neighbourhoods such as Sodermalm and Ostermalm offer design shops, cafes and nightlife.

The museums are among Europe's best. The Vasa Museum houses a preserved 17th-century warship that sank on its maiden voyage, Skansen is the world's oldest open-air museum, and the ABBA Museum, the Nordic Museum and the Fotografiska photography museum draw crowds. City Hall, where the Nobel banquet is held, has a beautiful Golden Hall and tower views.

The city is clean, safe and easy to get around by metro, ferry and on foot. Summer brings long days and outdoor life, winter is dark and cosy with Christmas markets, and the archipelago offers island escapes all year. Plan three days to see the highlights.

Why visit

Why visit Stockholm?

Stockholm is one of Europe's most beautiful capitals.

  • Water everywhere

    Islands, bridges and ferries.

  • Gamla Stan

    A perfectly preserved medieval core.

  • Museums

    Vasa, Skansen and more.

  • Design and food

    Fika, craft and new Nordic.

  • Archipelago access

    Thousands of islands nearby.

  3. 03

When to go

Best time to visit Stockholm

Late spring to early autumn is warm and bright, with very long days around midsummer.

  • May-June

    Best time

    Long days and fresh greenery.

  • July-August

    Good

    Warm, busy and lively.

  • September

    Good

    Mild, golden autumn.

  • December-February

    Quiet

    Cold, dark and cosy.

Worth planning around: November to February if you dislike cold and darkness.

Travel tips

Stockholm travel tips

Tips for Stockholm.

  • Buy a travel pass for transport.
  • Book Vasa and ABBA tickets ahead.
  • Expect cashless payments.
  • Pack layers year-round.
  • Try a fika daily.
  • Walk or cycle where possible.
  • Check winter daylight hours.
