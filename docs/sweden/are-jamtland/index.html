Welcome to Are and Jamtland

Are is Sweden's largest ski resort, set beneath the Areskutan mountain and beside the lake Aresjon. The village is lively, with cosy cafes, restaurants and bars, and has been a host for World Cup skiing and winter sports events. Slopes serve all levels, from family runs to challenging off-piste.

Jamtland, the surrounding province, is a landscape of lakes, valleys and mountain ranges, with Storsjon (Sweden's fifth-largest lake) and the monster legend of Storsjoodjuret. Tannforsen, Sweden's largest waterfall by volume, and the Jamtland trails offer excellent hiking and mountain biking in summer.

Ostersund, the regional capital, is a pleasant lakeside town with museums, a lively food scene and a focus on local produce. Combined with Are, it makes a good winter or summer break away from the big cities.
