Welcome to Gangneung

Gangneung is the largest city on Korea's east coast, with about 210,000 people, and it sits between the Taebaek mountains and the East Sea. It was one of the host cities of the 2018 Winter Olympics, and a KTX line opened for the games has cut the journey from Seoul to under two hours, making the coast a very easy weekend trip.

The city has beaches, culture and a long history. Gyeongpo Beach is a broad strand backed by pine forests and a lake, Ojukheon is the birthplace of Yi I, the great Confucian scholar Yulgok, and the mother-and-son culture of the Shin Saimdang family, and Seongyojang is a large 18th-century mansion. Each year the Gangneung Danoje festival, listed by UNESCO, celebrates shamanic ritual, mask dance and traditional games.

Gangneung is also Korea's coffee capital. Anmok Beach has a street of cafés facing the waves, and the city holds a coffee festival each autumn. Add fresh seafood, the soft tofu of Chodang village, and the Jeongdongjin sunrise, and it makes a relaxing two days, and a natural pairing with nearby Seoraksan.

Why visit

Why visit Gangneung?

Gangneung is a relaxed coastal alternative to the big cities, easy to reach from Seoul.

  • Pine-lined beaches

    Gyeongpo and Anmok face the East Sea.

  • Coffee culture

    A street of seaside cafés.

  • Scholar heritage

    Ojukheon and Seongyojang.

  • A UNESCO festival

    The Danoje festival each spring.

  • Fast train link

    Under two hours from Seoul.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Gangneung

Sights are spread between the city and the coast, and buses link them.

  1. 01

    Gyeongpo Beach and Lake

    The city's main beach, a broad strand with a pine grove, a lake with a pavilion and a boardwalk.

    Tip Go at sunrise.

  2. 02

    Ojukheon

    A 16th-century house and museum, the birthplace of the scholar Yulgok and his mother, the artist Shin Saimdang, surrounded by black bamboo.

    Tip Allow an hour.

  3. 03

    Anmok Coffee Street

    A stretch of cafés along Anmok Beach, some roasting their own beans.

    Tip Come in the afternoon.

  4. 04

    Seongyojang

    A large 18th-century mansion of a noble family, with a traditional garden.

    Tip Quiet in the morning.

  5. 05

    Jeongdongjin

    A seaside village famous for sunrise, with a railway station just off the beach.

    Tip Take the sea-view train.

  6. 06

    Chodang Sundubu Village

    A village known for soft tofu made with seawater.

    Tip Go at lunch.

When to go

Best time to visit Gangneung

Gangneung is cooler in summer than inland, and snowy in winter.

  • Spring (March–May)

    Best time

    Cherry blossom and mild weather.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Good

    Beach season, but busy.

  • Autumn (September–November)

    Best time

    Clear skies and the coffee festival.

  • Winter (December–February)

    Quiet

    Snowy and cold.

Worth planning around: Peak beach weekends in August.

Travel tips

Gangneung travel tips

A few tips help you plan.

  • Book KTX in advance.
  • Go for sunrise.
  • Try many cafés.
  • Visit on weekdays.
  • Bring a jacket for the sea breeze.
  • Combine with Seoraksan.
  • Book hotels early in summer.

FAQ

Gangneung: frequently asked questions

How far is Gangneung from Seoul?

About two hours by KTX.

Is Gangneung good for a weekend?

Yes.

What is Gangneung known for?

Beaches, coffee and Ojukheon.

Can I swim?

In summer, when beaches open.

Can I combine it with Seoraksan?

Yes, they are about an hour apart.

When is Danoje?

Around the fifth lunar month, usually May or June.
