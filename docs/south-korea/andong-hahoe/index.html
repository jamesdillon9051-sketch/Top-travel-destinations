Welcome to Andong

Andong is a small city in the hills of North Gyeongsang, and one of the strongest keepers of Korea's Confucian traditions. It has long been a centre of scholarship and clan life, and its families still practise ancestral rites. Around the city are academies, pavilions and villages that show how the yangban aristocracy of the Joseon dynasty lived.

Hahoe Folk Village is the star. Its name means 'river-bend', and the Nakdong River wraps around the settlement in an S-shaped curve. Members of the Pungsan Ryu clan have lived here for about 600 years, and the village still has real residents, with thatched-roof houses for commoners and grand tile-roofed homes for the aristocracy. Together with Yangdong in Gyeongju, it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The famous Hahoe mask dance, a satirical folk drama of masks made from alder wood, is performed on many days.

Nearby are Byeongsan Seowon, a Confucian academy with a beautiful hillside setting, and Andong Dam and its walking paths. Andong's food is distinct too, with jjimdak (braised chicken), salted mackerel and its own strong soju. It is about two hours from Seoul by train and works as a peaceful stop between the capital and the south-east.

Why visit

Why visit Andong?

Hahoe and Andong are the best place to feel traditional Korean rural life.

  • A living village

    Residents still live in the thatched houses.

  • Mask dance

    A satirical folk theatre tradition.

  • Confucian heritage

    Academies and pavilions.

  • Distinct food

    Jjimdak and Andong soju.

  • Quiet and scenic

    River, pines and cliffs.

Main attractions

Top things to see in Andong

Hahoe is the main draw, with more places nearby.

  1. 01

    Hahoe Folk Village

    A clan village of about 600 years, with earthen walls, thatched houses, big tile-roofed mansions and a great pine forest along the river.

    Tip Stay overnight in a hanok.

  2. 02

    Hahoe Mask Dance

    A traditional play with wooden masks that pokes fun at aristocrats and monks, performed in the village.

    Tip Check the performance times.

  3. 03

    Buyongdae Cliff

    A cliff across the river that gives a view of the village's shape.

    Tip Take the ferry across.

  4. 04

    Byeongsan Seowon

    A 16th-century Confucian academy overlooking the river, also a UNESCO site.

    Tip Visit in the afternoon.

  5. 05

    Wolyeonggyo Bridge

    A wooden footbridge across the Nakdong River in Andong.

    Tip Walk at night.

  6. 06

    Andong Mask Museum

    A museum of masks from Korea and the world.

    Tip Near the village.

Local food

What to eat in Andong

Andong is known for hearty, salty and spicy dishes.

  • Andong jjimdak

    Chicken braised in soy sauce with noodles, potatoes and chilli.

    Where to try it Jjimdak Alley.

  • Andong salted mackerel (ganjaebi)

    Mackerel cured in salt and grilled.

    Where to try it Restaurants in the city.

  • Heotjesabap

    Rice mixed with vegetables, once a ritual dish.

    Where to try it Traditional restaurants.

  • Andong soju

    A strong distilled spirit.

    Where to try it Museum and shops.

  • Buchimgae

    Savoury pancakes.

    Where to try it Village restaurants.

Transport

Getting to and around Andong

Andong is reached by train or bus.

Getting there

  • Train from Seoul

    About two hours by KTX-Eum or Mugunghwa train from Cheongnyangni.

    Book in advance.

  • Bus from Seoul

    About three hours.

    Frequent.

  • Bus to Hahoe

    A local bus from Andong takes about 50 minutes.

    Check the schedule.

Getting around

Buses are infrequent, so plan around them.

  • Local bus

    Runs to Hahoe and Byeongsan.

    Check the times.

  • Taxi

    Good for a half-day tour.

    Agree the price.

  • Rental car

    Convenient for rural sights.

    Need an international permit.

When to go

Best time to visit Andong

Andong has hot summers and cold winters.

  • Spring (March–May)

    Best time

    Flowers and mild weather.

  • Summer (June–August)

    Think twice

    Hot and humid.

  • Autumn (September–November)

    Best time

  • Winter (December–February)

    Quiet

    Cold and quiet.

Travel tips

Andong travel tips

A few tips help you visit Andong.

  • Check the bus times.
  • Stay overnight in Hahoe.
  • Respect residents.
  • Try the local soju.
  • Bring cash.
  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Book hanok stays early.

FAQ

Andong: frequently asked questions

How long do I need?

One or two days.

Do people live in Hahoe?

Yes.

How do I get there from Seoul?

Train or bus to Andong, then a local bus.

Is it a UNESCO site?

Yes, listed in 2010.

When is the mask festival?

Usually late September or early October.

Can I combine it with Gyeongju?

Yes, by bus or train.
